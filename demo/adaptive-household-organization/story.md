Elena Vance and her partner Marcus Chen live in a rented two-bedroom apartment. Across 5 sessions (38 simulated days), their needs shift from basic space orientation to hybrid remote work (glare in Bedroom 1) and finally hosting Elena's brother for 3 weeks in the 3.5m × 3.0m second room (`bedroom_2_multipurpose`) with a strict $250 budget cap.

A generic assistant without personal-context reconciliation fails by either recommending a $649 sofa bed (over budget), relegating Grandfather's mid-century leather armchair to the damp basement locker B-14, blocking the 80 cm door swing / north window radiator, or leaving guest bedding inside Marcus's 09:00-11:00 AM telehealth camera backdrop.
