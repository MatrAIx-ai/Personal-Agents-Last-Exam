## Method

Each record family has one authoritative date field. Timestamps (all with a UTC offset, Mountain or Pacific) are converted to a local date in America/Denver. A record counts when its local date is in the window and it is not after the dataset clock; missing or unparseable dates are counted as invalid and listed. Distinct dates are counted per family and once globally (a date with several records or families counts once). Each family names the Agent tool that reads it; the activity log is read through Infra's env.snapshot.

## Loading and queries

`node tests/dataset.integration.cjs --runtime DIR` loads `seed.json` through the public runtime with `env.load_seed` and the dataset clock, then reads it back through the App's MCP tools: every place's issuances, alerts and the account's watches, notices and saved places, month by month against this report, the newest observation, and one write on the loaded data. Its report is in `docs/validation/rainshadow/`.
