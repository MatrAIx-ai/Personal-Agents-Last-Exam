## Data

All people, addresses, senders, parcels, tracking numbers, pickup points and policy profiles are invented. Addresses use a fictional town and are not deliverable. `contribution/dataset.json` records the two sources: hand-authored directory records, and histories produced by deterministic replay. There is no random input and no collected or scraped data.

## External references

The approved proposal (commit `4e27c75`, dated 2026-11-18) cites three public carrier help pages as workflow references. They were consulted while the proposal was drafted, on or before that date, and were not accessed during implementation.

- UPS, changing a delivery: https://www.ups.com/us/en/track/change-delivery
- FedEx Delivery Manager: https://www.fedex.com/en-us/delivery-manager.html
- FedEx, when and where to pick up a package: https://www.fedex.com/en-us/customer-support/faqs/receiving/delivery/when-where-pickup-my-package.html

What was taken from them is the general idea that a recipient can change a delivery, hold a parcel, redirect it to a pickup point and have someone else collect it. No text, data, branding, screenshot or image was copied. The carrier, policy profiles, cutoffs, deadlines, pass and authorization rules in this App are synthetic and are not a description of either carrier's actual policies.
