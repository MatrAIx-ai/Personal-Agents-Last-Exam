OFFICE OF CONSUMER DEVICE DATA
Docket OCDD-2026-0031
Proposed rule: Retention and Sharing of Consumer Wearable Sensor Data

AGENCY: Office of Consumer Device Data (OCDD).
ACTION: Proposed rule; request for comments.
SUMMARY: The Office proposes to permit makers of consumer wearables to retain raw step, heart-rate and sleep sensor data for three years after collection and to share de-identified sensor data with research partners by default, with an opt-out available in device settings.
DATES: Comments must be received on or before 2026-12-04 at 11:59 PM America/New_York.
ADDRESSES: Submit comments through CivicDesk under docket OCDD-2026-0031. All comments received are part of the public record.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:
The proposal sets a three-year retention ceiling, defines de-identification by removal of direct identifiers, and asks whether small clinics and individual practitioners who use patient wearable data in care should have a defined deletion path when care ends.
