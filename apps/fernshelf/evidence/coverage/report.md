# Fernshelf dataset coverage

Generated by `node scripts/coverage-report.cjs` from `fixtures/reference.json` (the package seed) and `fixtures/clock.json`. Environment dataset coverage of the delivered reference fixture (the package seed every run starts from), not a Task history selection.

- Time zone: `America/Los_Angeles` (the account's). Window: 2025-01-01 to 2026-12-31, inclusive. Clock: `2026-11-20T09:00:00-08:00`.
- Method: Each record family's authoritative date field is read from the seed file, normalised from its UTC offset to a calendar date in America/Los_Angeles with Intl.DateTimeFormat, and counted if it falls in the window. A date with several records or families counts once in the global count. Due times stored for the future (purge_at, expires_at) are reported separately and never counted as history.
- Records: 2399 in 11 families, 2399 counted. Global distinct dates: **252**, from 2025-01-04 to 2026-11-20. Months with records: 23 of 24 (none in 2026-12).
- Provenance: Every record is synthetic. Fernshelf is one fictional person's private photo library; no real account, website or photo was collected. The records were written by hand or generated from fixed seeds by the scripts named below, and the image files were drawn by a script. Real records: 0.
- Exclusions: none; every authoritative date is present, valid and inside the window.

## Gaps and scheduled dates

No record of what happened exists after the clock (2026-11-20T09:00:00-08:00): from then to 2026-12-31 the fixture holds only stored due times. History dates in November 2026 end on 2026-11-20; December 2026 has no history record. History records dated after the clock: 0. The account was opened on 2025-01-04, so 2025-01-01 to the day before have no record.

## Not counted

- `items[].taken_at`: capture time of the photo, not when the account received it; 126 items were captured before 2025-01-01 and arrived in the phone's first sync on 2025-01-04.
- `items[].trashed_at, purged_at; members[].joined_at, ended_at; removals[].answers[].at; links[].revoked_at`: state changes; each also has its own item_history or activity row, which is counted.
- `items[].purge_at, links[].expires_at`: stored due times; past ones are counted through the purge or expiry rows, future ones are listed under after_clock.scheduled.
