# Pegboard full-window evaluation dataset

The Finch Street Café schedule and one hourly employee's Pegboard account (Noor Haddad, `pegboard-noor`) from **1 January 2025 to 31 December 2026**, for the Environment's delivered evaluation data. Everything is synthetic and fictional. It is separate from `fixtures/minimal.json`, which stays the small smoke-test fixture (four weeks in November 2026).

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `seed.json` | The App seed, as Infra passes it to `env.load_seed` (compact JSON, 1,608,682 bytes). |
| `clock.json` | The dataset clock, `2026-12-31T20:00:00-08:00`: pass it as `virtual_now` with the seed. |
| `dataset.json` | Provenance in the `pale.dataset.v1` contract: one synthetic source, the generator, its seed and the resulting counts. |
| `coverage.json`, `coverage.md` | Date coverage by record family and month, with gaps, exclusions and states at the clock. |

## Loading it

Infra loads it like any Task history: `env.load_seed` with `data` = `seed.json`, `virtual_now` = the clock above, and the package's binding (`fixtures/binding.json`, account `pegboard-noor`). The seed is valid only at that clock or later: pending handoffs and time off are judged against it.

- `node tests/dataset.integration.cjs --runtime DIR` does this on the public runtime and reads the data back through the Agent's MCP tools (every month of the window, both ends, the future weeks, handoffs, time off, notifications, hours), then makes one write and resets. The whole request is 1,608,896 bytes, inside Core's 2 MiB limit for one MCP message.
- `node examples/web.cjs --runtime DIR --dataset full-window` opens the web pages on it, at the dataset clock.

## What is in it

- **Workplace**: the café of the smoke fixture (two locations, three positions, the same policy: 10 hours' rest, a 40-hour week, a 24-hour handoff cut-off, 14 days' notice for time off, manager approval). Twelve people: Noor, nine coworkers, Rosa the manager, and two more than the fixture: Eli (barista, worked until August 2025) and Mae (cashier, from mid-September 2025). Pegboard has no employment dates, so both stay on the team list.
- **Schedule**: 107 weeks, 30 December 2024 to 17 January 2027, each posted on the Monday two weeks before it at 15:00 (3,417 shifts). Four shifts every day (two openers, the afternoon barista, the shift lead), the Quay kiosk Friday to Sunday from May to September, the evening cashier on Fridays and Saturdays and every day in July and August, a 05:30 summer open; closed on New Year's Day, Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day. About 2.5% of shifts are posted open and the manager fills most of them on Thursdays; she also adds an extra open shift most months.
- **Noor**: a student who works about 3 or 4 shifts a week (375 shifts), with a 24-hour cap. Her unavailable times change with each term (nine availability updates, three days before each term), and she changes her preferences five times (quiet hours, open-shift and email notices).
- **Handoffs**: 85, of which 50 she asked for (26 covers, 10 trades, 11 releases, 3 pickups) and 35 coworker requests to her (28 covers, 7 trades). They end in every way the App knows: approved 26, declined 29, expired at the cut-off 15, cancelled or withdrawn 7, denied by the manager 3, void after a manager change 3; at the clock one waits for a coworker and one waits for Noor.
- **Manager changes**: shift times moved, shifts cancelled (13), open shifts filled; each is a row in the shift's history.
- **Time off**: 20 requests, three to six weeks ahead (exams, trips, holidays): approved 15, denied 1, cancelled 3, and one still pending at the clock (29 to 31 January 2027).
- **Notifications and activity**: 259 notifications (schedule posted, shift changed, open shift, coworker request, handoff update, time-off decision; held in quiet hours where the App rules say so; email copies only while email notices are on) and 783 activity rows.

## Dates

Counting zone: America/Los_Angeles (the workplace and account zone). From `coverage.md`:

- Actions (the activity log: every write and event) on **333** of the 730 dates; Noor's own writes on 101 dates.
- All history (actions, postings, shift changes, handoffs, time off, notifications and the shifts that took place) on **725** of 730 dates. The 5 dates without a record are the café's closed days with no other activity: 2025-01-01, 2025-12-25, 2026-01-01, 2026-11-26 and 2026-12-25.
- The 69 shifts dated 2 to 17 January 2027 were posted before the clock: they are future schedule, not history, and are excluded from every count.

## Provenance and how it was made

Generated by `dev/dataset.cjs` with a seeded generator (mulberry32, seed 20250101): a timeline of postings, requests, answers, decisions, changes, time off and settings, each applied through the App's own tool handlers and event handlers in time order, so shift histories, handoff states, notifications and the activity log follow the App rules. Every call and event is checked against `tools.json` and `schemas/events.schema.json`, and the whole state against the seed schema and seed rules every 250 steps and at the end. Messages and reasons come from short fixed lists.

There is no real source behind it: Pegboard models one employee's view of a private workplace schedule, and no public website publishes such records, so there is no real-data anchor to collect. No record is real or mixed; none is presented as an observed fact.

## Reproduce

```
node dev/dataset.cjs --check     # rebuild from the seed and compare with seed.json, clock.json, dataset.json
node dev/coverage.cjs --check    # recount and compare with coverage.json, coverage.md
node tests/dataset.integration.cjs --runtime DIR
```

`npm run check:dataset` runs the first two; `node dev/dataset.cjs && node dev/coverage.cjs` rewrites the files.

## Limits

- Synthetic realism is a judgment for review: the schedule pattern, the rate of requests and decisions and the short message lists are invented.
- One manager decides everything; coworkers' own requests to each other, which Noor's account would not see, are not modelled.
- The dataset clock is the end of the window. A Task that starts earlier should select the history before its first session and stage later records as scenario events, not load this seed at an earlier clock.
