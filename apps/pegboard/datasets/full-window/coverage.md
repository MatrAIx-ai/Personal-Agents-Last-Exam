## Method

Each record family has one authoritative date field. Timestamps (all with a UTC offset) are converted to a local date in America/Los_Angeles. A record counts when its local date is in the window and it is not after the dataset clock; missing or unparseable dates are counted as invalid and listed. Distinct dates are counted per family and once globally (a date with several records or families counts once). Shift dates after the clock are posted future schedule, not history: they are excluded and reported under future_posted_shifts.

## Loading and queries

`node tests/dataset.integration.cjs --runtime DIR` loads `seed.json` through the public runtime with `env.load_seed` and the dataset clock, then reads it back through the App's MCP tools: every month of the window through `pegboard.shifts.list`, both ends of the window, the future posted weeks, the handoff, time-off and notification lists, and one write on the loaded data. Its report is in `docs/validation/pegboard/`.
