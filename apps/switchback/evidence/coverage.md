# Switchback dataset coverage

Generated by `node scripts/coverage-report.cjs` from the committed package files; `npm test` fails if this file or `coverage.json` differs from a fresh run. Do not edit by hand.

- **Scope:** Environment dataset coverage of the records each delivered Switchback fixture loads with env.load_seed (and the Mail seed used by the publisher), counted over represented occurrence dates.
- **Window:** 2025-01-01 to 2026-12-31, inclusive.
- **Counting time zone:** `America/Phoenix`. Facilities keep fixed UTC offsets (-06:00, -07:00 or -08:00, no daylight saving). Timestamps are normalized to America/Phoenix (UTC-07:00 all year) before taking the date; date-only fields (stay and claim nights) are facility-local calendar dates and are taken as they are. date_shift_vs_facility_offset counts records whose America/Phoenix date differs from the date in their own offset.
- **Provenance:** All records are synthetic: written by fixtures/generate.cjs from fixed seeds (claim instants drawn once from OS entropy into fixtures/evaluator/). No real facility, person, payment or Recreation.gov record is included, and none of these records is an observed fact.
- **Global deduplicated distinct dates in the window:** 424 (2025-01-02 to 2026-12-31); months with no date in any source: none.

## Authoritative date field per collection

| Collection | Field | Represents |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `reservations` | `created_at` | booking made (charge receipt time) |
| `reservations[].history` | `at` | lifecycle event after booking: check-in, completion, cancellation, no-show release, change, late-arrival notice (the "confirmed" row repeats created_at and is not counted again) |
| `reservations[].nights` | `date (date-only, facility-local)` | a night the account holder stayed or is booked to stay (cancelled stays excluded) |
| `payments` | `created_at` | charge or refund issued |
| `alerts` | `posted_at` | facility alert posted |
| `closures` | `ordered_at` | closure ordered |
| `claims` | `claimed_at` | another booker took open nights |
| `claims[].nights` | `date (date-only, facility-local)` | a night another booker holds |
| `equipment` | `updated_at` | saved equipment last edited |
| `companions` | `updated_at` | saved companion last edited |
| `messages (Mail)` | `received_at` | Mail message in the seeded inbox |

Excluded date fields:

- `reservations.updated_at`: repeats the last history row.
- `reservations.arrival, departure`: stay bounds; the nights themselves are counted.
- `payments.posts_at`: derived posting date (refunds post three days after created_at); counted once through created_at.
- `alerts.from, to, cancelled_at; alerts.revisions`: validity window and its end, not an occurrence.
- `closures.from, to`: validity window of the closure, not an occurrence.
- `wallet.cards exp_month, exp_year`: expiry deadline.
- `holds.expires_at, previews.expires_at, notices`: deadlines and run records; empty in every seed.
- `facilities, sites, policies`: catalogue records with no represented date.
- `fixtures/schedules/*.json, fixtures/evaluator/*.json`: scenario events applied during a run, not loaded history; listed separately below and not counted.

## Gaps and how each fixture covers the window

- **fixture-minimal** (clock 2027-05-20T09:00:00-07:00): 85 distinct dates, 2025-01-02 to 2026-12-30. Every month from 2025-01 to 2026-12 has dated records. Months with a single distinct date: 2025-05, 2025-08, 2025-11, 2026-02, 2026-03. 5 of its stays (res-0023, res-0024, res-0025, res-0026, res-0027) come from the generator's quiet-month pass (fillQuietMonths in fixtures/generate.cjs), which adds one stay or advance booking for each month that the main history loop left without any dated record; every other record is unchanged from the earlier generator. Its clock lies after the window, so the whole window is past activity.
- **fixture-high-country** (clock 2026-06-12T08:00:00-06:00): 299 distinct dates, 2025-01-28 to 2026-12-08. Every month from 2025-01 up to the clock has dated records. Months with a single distinct date: 2025-01, 2025-02, 2025-08, 2025-11. 3 of its stays (res-0012, res-0013, res-0014) come from the generator's quiet-month pass (fillQuietMonths in fixtures/generate.cjs), which adds one stay or advance booking for each month that the main history loop left without any dated record; every other record is unchanged from the earlier generator. After the clock (2026-07 on) the fixture holds only bookings for the future: its upcoming stays and other bookers' claimed nights in the 180-day released window, which ends 2026-12-08. Nights after 2026-12-08 are not released yet at this clock, so by rule nothing can be booked there; this end is by design, not missing data.
- **fixture-desert-rivers** (clock 2026-10-02T07:30:00-07:00): 242 distinct dates, 2025-01-03 to 2026-12-31. Every month from 2025-01 up to the clock has dated records. Months with a single distinct date: 2025-10. 3 of its stays (res-0021, res-0022, res-0023) come from the generator's quiet-month pass (fillQuietMonths in fixtures/generate.cjs), which adds one stay or advance booking for each month that the main history loop left without any dated record; every other record is unchanged from the earlier generator. After the clock (2026-11 on) the fixture holds only bookings for the future: its upcoming stays and other bookers' claimed nights in the 180-day released window, which ends 2027-03-30.

minimal is the full-window source: its clock (2027-05-20) lies after the window, so all 24 months are past activity. high-country and desert-rivers are task fixtures whose clocks lie inside the window (evidence/README.md has a demo run at the desert-rivers clock); their history runs from January 2025 to the clock, and after it they hold only bookings for the future.

## Reproduce

```sh
cd apps/switchback
node scripts/coverage-report.cjs --check   # compares with a fresh run
node scripts/coverage-report.cjs           # rewrites evidence/coverage.json and evidence/coverage.md
node fixtures/generate.cjs --check         # the fixtures themselves match the generator
```

Loading: `node tests/platform-checks.cjs` (from the repository root: `node apps/switchback/tests/platform-checks.cjs`) loads each fixture with `env.load_seed` through real Core and checks that every counted reservation, payment, alert and closure ID, with its counted date, is returned by the Agent tools `switchback.reservations.list`, `switchback.reservations.get` and `switchback.alerts.list`.
