# Notice: reference screenshots

The images in this directory are screenshots of public Recreation.gov pages (campground and campsite pages, the Rules and Reservation Policies page, and public Help Center articles at help.recreation.gov). They and everything shown in them, including photographs, maps, logos and text, belong to their respective owners.

They are included only as review comparison evidence for the Switchback App, as `docs/submission-evidence.md` asks: they let a reviewer compare Switchback's pages with the reference product's workflows. They are not covered by the package license (MIT for code, CC0 for fixtures and icons), are not runtime assets, are not loaded by any page or tool, and must not be reused for anything else.

How they were taken: `evidence/capture-reference.cjs`, headless Chromium with its default user agent, public pages only, no login, account, cart, form submission or bot-check bypass. `sources.json` records each image's source URL, final URL, page title, capture time (UTC) and viewport. No personal data was captured; the Help Center images show the reference product's own demo accounts.
