## Gaps

- Months with history: 21 of 24. Months without any history record: 2025-07, 2026-07, 2026-12.
- November 2026 holds history only up to the clock (2026-11-17 07:00): 109 records on 11 dates.
- After the clock the dataset holds no activity, by design: everything after 2026-11-17 07:00 happens during a run, through Agent writes, `env.apply_event` and `env.advance_time`. December 2026 therefore has no history record; it holds only the scheduled items below. This is a remaining gap against the 2025-01-01..2026-12-31 window; it is not filled with invented activity.
- 2025-07 and 2026-07 are the summer break: no school days, so no attendance, menus, reports or purchases, and the generated history writes no other account activity there. Those months have no record.
- Earlier school years keep attendance exceptions only (absent, late, left early), not a present mark for every school day; the hand-written 2026-27 year marks every day from 21 September.
