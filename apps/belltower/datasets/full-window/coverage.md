## Method

Each record family has one authoritative date field. Timestamps (all with a UTC offset) are converted to a local date in America/Chicago. A revision counts on the date it was received; publication times differ by minutes. A record counts when its local date is in the window and it is not after the dataset clock; missing or unparseable dates are counted as invalid and listed. Distinct dates are counted per family and once globally (a date with several records or families counts once). Removed clippings and follow rules are no longer stored: their dates appear only through the activity log.

## Loading and queries

`node tests/dataset.integration.cjs --runtime DIR` loads `seed.json` through the public runtime with `env.load_seed` and the dataset clock, then reads it back through the App's MCP tools: every month of the window through `belltower.items.list`, both ends of the window, a corrected item with its revisions and copies, the read marks, clippings, follow rules and notifications, and one write on the loaded data. Its report is in `docs/validation/belltower/`.
