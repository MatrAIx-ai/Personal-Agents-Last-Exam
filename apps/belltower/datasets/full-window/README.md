# Belltower full-window evaluation dataset

Two years of what six invented sources published about the fictional city of Halden, **1 January 2025 to 31 December 2026**, and what one reader (Dana Reyes, account `belltower-dana`) did with it, for the Environment's delivered evaluation data. Everything is synthetic and fictional. It is separate from `fixtures/minimal.json`, which stays the small smoke-test fixture (three weeks in October and November 2026).

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `seed.json` | The App seed, as Infra passes it to `env.load_seed` (compact JSON, 1,097,763 bytes). |
| `clock.json` | The dataset clock, `2026-12-31T20:00:00-06:00`: pass it as `virtual_now` with the seed. |
| `dataset.json` | Provenance in the `pale.dataset.v1` contract: one synthetic source, the generator, its seed and the resulting counts. |
| `coverage.json`, `coverage.md` | Date coverage by record family and month, with gaps, the effective periods kept apart, and states at the clock. |

## Loading it

Infra loads it like any Task history: `env.load_seed` with `data` = `seed.json`, `virtual_now` = the clock above, and the package's binding (`fixtures/binding.json`, account `belltower-dana`). Belltower's seed rules do not read the clock, but every revision in it was received by the dataset clock, so load it at that time or later.

- `node tests/dataset.integration.cjs --runtime DIR` does this on the public runtime and reads the data back through the Agent's MCP tools (every month of the window, both ends, a corrected item with its copies, read marks, clippings, follow rules, notifications), then makes one write and resets. The whole request is 1,097,977 bytes, inside Core's 2 MiB limit for one MCP message.
- `node examples/web.cjs --runtime DIR --dataset full-window` opens the web pages on it, at the dataset clock.

## What is in it

- **City and sources**: the city, areas and six sources of the smoke fixture: Halden Public Works and the City of Halden (official), The Halden Courier and the Old Quay Ledger (newsrooms), Halden Roundup and the Neighbourhood Bulletin (aggregators).
- **Items**: 869 items (546 articles, 323 public notices with effective periods) with 962 revisions: 58 updates, 28 corrections with the publisher's note, 7 retractions. 239 revisions are another source's copy or report (`based_on`). Stories follow the seasons: snow emergencies and water-main breaks in winter, street sweeping and hydrant flushing in spring and autumn, resurfacing, heat and the Harbour Festival in summer, leaf collection, flu clinics and the city election in the autumn, holiday collection changes, hearings, planning applications, library hours, bus detours, school board news, shop openings and closures, safety reports and community news all year. Two boil-water notices (one corrected because it named too wide an area), two newsroom articles withdrawn, and aggregators that withdraw their copy when the original is corrected.
- **Dana**: lives in Lower Ferry Hill, works in Old Quay and looks in on her father in Millbrook South. She sets up three follow rules in January 2025 and changes them over the two years (8 created, 8 changed including pauses, 1 removed; 7 at the clock), changes her preferences 7 times (digest time, corrections delivered immediately, email notices on and off, quiet hours), marks 345 revisions as read, and keeps clippings: 40 made, 15 changed (a newer revision, an added line), 11 removed; 29 kept at the clock.
- **Notifications and activity**: 289 notifications, written by the App's own rules from her follow rules, earlier notifications and watched clippings (immediate or in the daily digest, held in quiet hours, superseded when a newer revision arrives first; email copies only while email notices are on), and 1,686 activity rows.

## Dates

Counting zone: America/Chicago (the account's zone). A revision counts on the date it was received. From `coverage.md`:

- Actions (the activity log: every arriving revision, every account write and their effects) on **604** of the 730 dates, from 2025-01-01 to 2026-12-31; Dana's own writes on 279 dates; new items on 540 dates. The longest stretch without any record is four days.
- The effective periods of notices (closures, bans, opening hours) are what the notices announce, not history: they are excluded from every count. At the clock 17 notices are in effect and 2 start after it.

## Provenance and how it was made

Generated by `dev/dataset.cjs` with a seeded generator (mulberry32, seed 20250102): story templates for each kind of source, with seasonal rules, slots for streets, places and dates, follow-ups (updates, corrections with the right fact, retractions) and other sources' copies and reports; a script of big days (snow emergencies, boil-water notices, elections, holidays, the festival); and Dana's actions (follow rules and preferences on fixed dates; reading, clipping and tidying driven by what reaches her). Each event and call goes through the App's own handlers in time order, so revisions, read marks, clippings, notifications and the activity log follow the App rules. Every call and event is checked against `tools.json` and `schemas/events.schema.json`, and the whole state against the seed schema and seed rules every 300 steps and at the end.

There is no real source behind it: Belltower's city, sources and people are invented (the approved plan uses invented publishers and places), so no real feed or website was collected and there is no real-data anchor. No record is real or mixed; none is presented as an observed fact.

## Reproduce

```
node dev/dataset.cjs --check     # rebuild from the seed and compare with seed.json, clock.json, dataset.json
node dev/coverage.cjs --check    # recount and compare with coverage.json, coverage.md
node tests/dataset.integration.cjs --runtime DIR
```

`npm run check:dataset` runs the first two; `node dev/dataset.cjs && node dev/coverage.cjs` rewrites the files.

## Limits

- Synthetic realism is a judgment for review: the wording comes from templates with a limited set of phrasings, and the rates of publishing, corrections and retractions are chosen, not measured.
- About 1.2 items a day for a small city's six sources; the smoke fixture is denser (two a day) because it was written by hand around its test cases.
- Removed clippings and follow rules are no longer stored: their dates are only in the activity log.
- The dataset clock is the end of the window. A Task that starts earlier should select the history before its first session and stage later items as scenario events.
