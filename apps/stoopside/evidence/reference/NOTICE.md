# Reference screenshots: notice

The PNG files in this directory are screenshots of public meetup.com pages. The pages, their text, layout, images and the Meetup name and logo belong to their owners (Meetup and the groups and members who published the content). They are included only as review comparison evidence for the Stoopside delivery, as asked by `docs/submission-evidence.md` section 1. They are not covered by the Stoopside package license (MIT for code, CC0 for fixtures), are not runtime assets, are not loaded by the App, and must not be reused for any other purpose.

They were captured on 2026-11-28 (UTC) with headless Chromium from public pages, without logging in, without creating an account and without answering or bypassing any bot check or cookie prompt (the cookie banner was hidden, not accepted). Before each capture every image on the page and every person name the page shows (member profile links, "Hosted by" and "Organized by" lines, the Organizers and Attendees panels) was blurred, so no personal data is kept. `sources.json` records the source URL, capture time, HTTP status and outcome of every page tried, including the pages that were not captured, and `capture.cjs` is the script that took them.
