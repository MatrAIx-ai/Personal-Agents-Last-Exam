# Stoopside dataset coverage

Generated by [`scripts/coverage-report.cjs`](../scripts/coverage-report.cjs) from the committed fixtures; do not edit by hand. Reproduce with `cd apps/stoopside && node scripts/coverage-report.cjs --check` (fails if this file or `coverage.json` differs from a fresh run). The full numbers, including per-collection counts, are in [`coverage.json`](coverage.json).

- **Scope:** Every delivered fixture in fixtures/index.json and its companion Mail seed (the records loaded with env.load_seed and Mail 1.3.0 seeding). Environment coverage window, not a Task history count.
- **Window:** 2025-01-01 to 2026-12-31, inclusive.
- **Counting time zone:** `Etc/GMT+5`. Etc/GMT+5 is the IANA fixed-offset zone for UTC-05:00 with no daylight-saving change, the Maple St groups' offset. Riverside (UTC-06:00) and Hillcrest (UTC-08:00) timestamps are converted to it before counting, so one global deduplicated count is possible. Each source's count in its own group offset is also given.
- **Provenance:** All records in every source are synthetic (0 real, 0 mixed). No real group, person, post or message is used, nothing was crawled, and there is no real-data anchor. The fixtures come from deterministic scripts in apps/stoopside/scripts/ (a fixed-seed generator plus hand-written specs); those scripts and their text were written with AI assistance, and no language model runs at generation time. The human read-through of every member and event row is pending. See contribution/dataset.json, generation.parameters.provenance.
- **Invalid or missing dates:** none.

## Which source covers the window

Maple St is the full-window source: its history runs from January 2025 to its clock (2027-03-01), so every month of 2025 and 2026 has records. Riverside and Hillcrest are bounded by their clocks by design: each fixture is a snapshot taken at its clock (Riverside 2026-04-20, Hillcrest 2026-11-18), and a fixture has no history after its own clock. Their months after the clock hold only upcoming events (scheduled, not history) or nothing. Together the three sources cover every month of the window; the global row below is the deduplicated union.

## Gaps and reasons

- **maple-street:** no empty month. Months with fewer than 8 distinct dates: 2025-11 (7). Scheduled after the clock: 0 event starts on 0 dates in the window. Scenario: 16 staged events (0 in the window, 2027-03-01T10:00:00-05:00 to 2027-03-09T10:00:00-05:00), applied by Infra and not counted.
- **riverside:** no history in 2026-05 to 2026-12 (8 months), because the fixture's clock is 2026-04-20 and a fixture has no history after its clock. Scheduled after the clock: 7 event starts on 7 dates in the window. Scenario: 16 staged events (16 in the window, 2026-04-20T12:00:00-06:00 to 2026-05-01T10:00:00-06:00), applied by Infra and not counted.
- **hillcrest:** no history in 2026-12, because the fixture's clock is 2026-11-18 and a fixture has no history after its clock. Scheduled after the clock: 8 event starts on 8 dates in the window. Scenario: 17 staged events (17 in the window, 2026-11-18T12:00:00-08:00 to 2026-11-30T09:00:00-08:00), applied by Infra and not counted.

## Authoritative date fields

| Collection | Field | Represents |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| groups | `created_at` | group founded |
| members | `created_at` | member account created |
| memberships | `revisions[].at` | join, leave, removal or role change |
| events | `start` | event occurrence (start time) |
| events | `revisions[].at` | event posted, edited or cancelled |
| rsvps | `revisions[].at` | RSVP answered or changed |
| posts | `revisions[].at` | post, comment or announcement written, edited or deleted |
| swaps | `proposed_at` | swap proposed |
| swaps | `decided_at` | swap answered (null while pending) |
| deliveries | `at` | notification delivered to a member |
| mail.messages | `received_at` | Mail message received or sent (companion Mail seed) |

**Excluded fields**

| Collection | Field | Reason |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| events | `end` | end of the same occurrence as start |
| events | `rsvp_open, rsvp_close` | deadlines, not occurrences |
| rsvps | `created_at, updated_at` | equal to the first and last revision |
| rsvps | `waitlisted_at, offer_expires_at, reconfirm_by` | queue key and deadlines |
| posts | `created_at, updated_at` | equal to the first and last revision |
| memberships | `created_at` | equal to the first revision |
| sheets | `lock_at` | deadline; a sheet has no date of its own (its event is counted) |
| swaps | `expires_at` | deadline |
| groups | `digest_through` | bookkeeping time for the Monday digest |
| scenario events | `at` | staged events applied by Infra after the fixture clock; reported separately, never counted as history |

**Counting rules**

- A timestamp counts as history when it is at or before the fixture clock. Upcoming event starts after the clock are reported as scheduled_after_clock and are not counted as history.
- Dates are taken in the counting zone and kept only when they fall in the window; records outside it are counted as out of window.
- A timestamp that does not parse is listed under invalid and not counted.
