## Reference comparison
- [Spotify's public plan management guide](https://support.spotify.com/us/article/change-premium-plans/) maps to the plan card and action at **Overview** (01/08). [Spotify's public cancellation guide](https://support.spotify.com/us/article/cancel-premium/) maps to **Preview → Confirm → Status** (02/04/10/13) and keeping saved entries.
- Reference provenance in the attachment lists first-party public articles and the limitations of the earlier archived, unstyled help captures. No new signed-in captures: a Free account overview needs account access and the active cancellation path requires an authorized Premium account. No accounts were created, no credentials used, and no subscription purchased for this evidence. Public help articles document actions and outcomes but do not prove signed-in screen appearance.
- Intentional differences: Echo Arbor uses its own design, simulated prices, deterministic billing dates, a pre-bound fictional account, and no billing or sign-in. Withdrawal undoes a pending cancellation before the boundary; it does not resubscribe, take a payment or create an account. The UI explicitly shows the bound account name and simulated UTC date, with all display and Core boundary dates in UTC. Status lists dated subscription history and the library shows saved dates.
