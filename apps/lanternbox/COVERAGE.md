# Lanternbox data coverage

Written by `node scripts/coverage.cjs`; `node scripts/coverage.cjs --check` recomputes it and compares. Do not edit by hand.

- **Window:** 2025-01-01 to 2026-12-31, inclusive.
- **Counting time zone:** `America/New_York`. Every timestamp carries an offset and is converted to this zone before its date is taken. There are no date-only records.
- **Clock:** `2026-11-18T09:00:00-08:00`. The seed holds the history before it. What comes after it is the schedule, plus a few catalog dates in the seed that the App reads against the clock.
- **Provenance:** all synthetic, generated by `scripts/build-fixtures.cjs` from a fixed seed. No real person, title, studio or service, and no generated record is presented as an observed fact.
- **Delivered dataset:** `fixtures/minimal.json` with `fixtures/schedules/late-2026.json` is the delivered Environment evaluation dataset. The tests load the same seed; there is no separate, smaller smoke fixture.

## Gaps

- Months with no record: none.
- Months with no `profiles` record: 21 of 24.
- Months with no `titles` record: 16 of 24.
- Months with no `episodes` record: 8 of 24.
- Months with no `history` record: none.
- Months with no `progress` record: none.
- Months with no `watchlist` record: 15 of 24.
- Months with no `devices` record: 21 of 24.
- Months with no `charges` record: none.
- Months with no `notifications` record: 17 of 24.
- Months with no `activity` record: none.
- Runs of 7 or more days with no record of any type (window edges included): 
  - 10 days between 2026-06-17 and 2026-06-28
  - 9 days between 2026-09-10 and 2026-09-20
- Longest run: 10 days.

The window opens on 2025-01-01 and the account opens on 2025-01-02. `profiles` and `devices` are few by nature (4 and 5), and there is one charge a month.

## Left out

- Catalog dates before 2025-01-01 are outside the window and not counted: 45 of 60 titles (`available_from`) and 177 of 269 episodes (`released_at`). The titles themselves stay in the catalog; only their dates are left out of the counts.
- Other records dated before the window: none. Records dated after the window: none.
- `plans` and the account record have no date field and are not counted (3 plans).
- Secondary and planned dates are not counted: `available_until`, `paid_through`, charge `period_start` and `period_end`, history `recorded_at`, device `last_used_at`.
- A progress row is counted at its latest `updated_at`; a row the schedule updates is counted again at the new date.
- Records with a missing or invalid date: 0.
