# Original Parkstead facility illustrations

The six PNG files are original, fictional 320×180 facility thumbnails created with
OpenAI's built-in image generation tool on 2026-11-25 and downscaled deterministically
with macOS `sips`. They contain no copied logos, maps, screenshots, real facility
listings, or customer information. The final bundle is under 1 MiB. Integrity,
dimensions, MIME type, publication scope and provenance are generated into
`contribution/assets.catalog.json`.

Prompt set (one built-in generation call per asset):

1. Modern covered urban garage entrance near a fictional harbor; navy, teal, cream and coral flat 3D editorial style.
2. Compact downtown parking deck beneath brick arches; aubergine, sandstone, sage and amber flat 3D editorial style.
3. Open-air waterfront lot with shade structures; blue, seafoam, light concrete and yellow flat 3D editorial style.
4. Civic museum district underground entrance with accessible ramp; indigo, terracotta, stone and mint flat 3D editorial style.
5. Bright multilevel city structure with EV bays and solar canopies; cyan, deep blue, white and lime flat 3D editorial style.
6. Tree-lined neighborhood surface lot at golden hour; forest green, warm gray, rust and sky-blue flat 3D editorial style.

Every prompt required a wide landscape composition, a wholly fictional place, and
no brands, logos, readable text, maps, real landmarks or watermarks.
