## Capture integrity and reproduction

The new product images are full-page renders using **Poppler `pdftoppm` 26.11.0**, at a maximum dimension of 1,800 pixels. Rendering preserves the publisher's original screenshot and explanatory context; there is no crop, reconstruction, redaction, image generation or inserted interface content. The Diamond Resorts PDF was blank in the browser and its web screenshot retrieval timed out, but downloading the exact official public URL succeeded without credentials. Its retained page was rendered from those downloaded bytes. The Premier Inn guide was retrieved the same way.

To reproduce, download the two official PDF URLs above, verify their hashes against `pdf_source` in [sources.json](sources.json), and render the specified page:

```sh
pdftoppm -f 4 -l 4 -scale-to 1800 -png -singlefile diamond-guide.pdf diamond-special-requests-guide
pdftoppm -f 3 -l 3 -scale-to 1800 -png -singlefile premierinn-guide.pdf premierinn-add-breakfast-guide
```

The Travelodge gate and original FAQ images are unedited Chrome/CUA captures, with exact dimensions, capture timestamps and SHA-256 values per image. The original guide images remain their publishers' material and are retained as attributed review evidence; they are not runtime assets and are not claimed to be MIT-licensed. No private guest, booking credential or payment information appears in the selected pages.
