# Ribbonwell data coverage

Written by `node scripts/coverage.cjs`; `node scripts/coverage.cjs --check` recomputes it and compares. Do not edit by hand.

- **Window:** 2025-01-01 to 2026-12-31, inclusive.
- **Counting time zone:** `America/New_York`. Every timestamp carries an offset and is converted to this zone before its date is taken. There are no date-only records.
- **Clock:** `2026-11-18T09:00:00-08:00`. The seed holds everything before it; the schedule holds everything after it.
- **Provenance:** all synthetic, generated by `scripts/build-fixtures.cjs` from a fixed seed. No real person, shop or product.
- **Smoke fixture and delivered data:** the same files. `fixtures/minimal.json` is both what the tests load and the delivered seed.

## Left out

- `members`, `groups` and `settings` have no date field and are not counted (13 members, 2 groups).
- Secondary and planned dates are not counted: `updated_at`, `reserved_until`, `drawn_at`, `occasion_date`, `join_by`, `exchange_date`.
- Records with a missing or invalid date: 0.
- Records dated outside the window: 0.
