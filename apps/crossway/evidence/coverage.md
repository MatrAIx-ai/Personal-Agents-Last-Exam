# Crossway dataset coverage

Generated by `node apps/crossway/scripts/coverage-report.cjs` from the committed seed files; `--check` compares this file with a fresh run and `tests/coverage.test.cjs` runs that comparison in `npm test`.

- **Scope:** Environment coverage window, all delivered seeds (fixtures/seeds/index.json).
- **Window:** 2025-01-01 to 2026-12-31, inclusive.
- **Time zone:** America/New_York. Every timestamp is normalized to its Eastern calendar date before counting.
- **Provenance:** all records are synthetic (0 real records, no real anchor). No record is real or collected: plazas, rates, plates, people, cards and notices are fictional. The generator and story text were written with AI coding assistance; the records are its deterministic output. Human read-through: pending.
- **Totals:** 1473 records on 436 distinct dates (global, deduplicated across seeds), from 2025-01-06 to 2026-12-30. 24 of 24 months have records; months with none across all seeds: none. Excluded rows: 0.

## Loading and retrieval

`tests/platform-checks.cjs` loads every seed with `env.load_seed` through real Core and checks that the loaded state hash equals the seed file's canonical hash, so these counts are over the records the App actually holds. `node apps/crossway/scripts/coverage-report.cjs --loading` also reads `crossway.tolls.list` month by month for every seed through the Agent MCP connection and checks each month's total against the toll counts here; its output is `evidence/coverage-loading.json`.
