Brightfold Employee Handbook (excerpt)
Section 9.2: Leaving Brightfold

Notice period. Employees give 10 working days of notice. The count starts on the first working day after the day notice is given. The tenth working day is the employee's last day.

Working days. Monday to Friday, excluding company holidays.

Company holidays, November 2026 to January 2027: November 26 and 27, December 24 and 25, January 1 and January 18.

Notice is given in writing to the employee's manager.
