# Environment Track proposal

**Proposed PR title:** `[Environment Proposal] [Travel & commuting] Fueltrail Gas Prices and Fuel Log`

Fueltrail is an independently branded synthetic fuel-price finder and vehicle fuel-log service. Its workflows are informed by GasBuddy's public station-search, vehicle-profile, alert, and fuel-log experiences, but the implementation will not copy GasBuddy branding, source code, screenshots, station data, user data, or price feeds.

## 2. What you plan to build

**✍️ Your answer (required):** Build a resettable browser App and MCP business interface for a fictional fuel-price and vehicle-record service. The initial version will support four complete workflows:

1. **Find station prices:** search synthetic stations around a named area or saved place for a selected vehicle/fuel grade and time. Filter by search radius, fuel type, cash/credit price, brand, open status, and amenities; sort by price, distance, or a deterministic estimated trip cost. Results show price, units, reported time, freshness, distance, hours, accepted payment-price type, and missing/stale-price status. Unsupported fuel grades, invalid locations, closed stations, and no matching results produce explicit outcomes.
2. **Create and handle fuel-price alerts:** create, update, pause, resume, or delete an alert for an area, radius, fuel grade, cash/credit preference, and either a below-price threshold or a price-rise threshold. Task-scheduled price events evaluate active alerts and create one in-App notification per newly satisfied condition. Alerts record the triggering station/market, observed price, prior price where relevant, and event time. Replayed price events or an unchanged condition do not duplicate notifications, and an alert never buys fuel or changes a vehicle/log.
3. **Manage vehicle preferences:** add, edit, select, or archive synthetic vehicle profiles with label, year/make/model text, compatible fuel grade, tank capacity, distance/volume units, optional fuel-economy baseline, preferred search radius, cash/credit preference, brands, and required amenities. The active vehicle supplies search defaults but every search can override them explicitly. VINs, license plates, recall data, telematics, and live location tracking are excluded.
4. **Maintain a fuel log:** create a fill-up record with vehicle, date/time, station, odometer, volume, unit price, total cost, fuel grade, cash/credit type, and full/partial/missed-fill status. Edit or delete a record with expected-version checks and revision/tombstone history. Fuel economy is calculated only across eligible consecutive complete fill-ups with increasing odometers; partial or missed fill-ups break the calculation chain and remain visible. Duplicate records, inconsistent totals beyond the stated rounding tolerance, decreasing odometers, incompatible fuel, and edits to archived vehicles are rejected without partial changes.

The environment is useful for personal-agent evaluation because a safe choice depends on price freshness, fuel compatibility, distance, station status, user preferences, alert deduplication, and longitudinal vehicle records. It supports tasks such as finding the cheapest compatible station that is still open, alerting only when a meaningful threshold is crossed, correcting a mistaken fill-up, or comparing recent fuel economy without treating a partial fill as a full tank.

The GUI will expose the same business state and actions as MCP through station search/results/detail, alert management, vehicle profiles, fuel-log history/editor, and fuel-economy summary views. It will not be a generic state inspector.

## 3. Potential app connections

- **Internal personal apps (required):** Mail 1.3.0 is optional in v1.
  - **Mail 1.3.0, optional:** Fueltrail will publish triggered price alerts and selected fuel-log correction notices through Mail's implemented `channel.email` receive contract in [`examples/apps/mail/contribution/contribution.json`](../../examples/apps/mail/contribution/contribution.json). Connected validation will verify the actual destination Mail record and Messaging receipt. Fueltrail remains independently runnable without Mail because every alert and revision is retained in the App. Failed or unavailable delivery is reported separately and does not roll back a price event or fuel-log commit; v1 does not claim an atomic outbox or automatic queued retry.
- **Other apps and websites (required):** None required for v1. Future tasks may coordinate Fueltrail with car-rental, parking, toll, maps, budgeting, or maintenance environments, but no external App is a package or acceptance dependency. Station prices, status, and market changes are deterministic fixtures/task events rather than live GasBuddy, map, retailer, or payment services.

### Integration details

- **Operations and access (required):** Agent-facing MCP and GUI operations will cover station search/read, alert list/create/update/pause/resume/delete, vehicle list/create/read/update/archive/select, fuel-log list/read/preview/create/update/delete, and fuel-economy summary. Representative names are `station.search`, `station.get`, `price_alert.list`, `price_alert.create`, `price_alert.update`, `price_alert.pause`, `price_alert.resume`, `price_alert.delete`, `vehicle.list`, `vehicle.create`, `vehicle.update`, `vehicle.archive`, `vehicle.select`, `fuel_log.preview`, `fuel_log.create`, `fuel_log.update`, `fuel_log.delete`, and `fuel_economy.get`; exact schemas will be declared in `tools.json`. Infra-only `env.load_seed`, `env.apply_event`, `env.advance_time`, `env.snapshot`, and `env.reset` load histories, inject price/station changes, advance freshness windows, inspect results, and restore a clean run. Setup, reset, time control, and event injection are not Agent operations.
- **State and lifecycle (required):** Each App instance represents one user's Fueltrail account and is isolated by Core's `run_id` and `app_id`. Synthetic seed data includes places, stations, hours/amenities, price observations, vehicle profiles/preferences, alerts/notifications, and fuel-log histories/revisions. Price observations are immutable and have source/effective/received times; search derives the latest valid observation and labels stale or missing data. Monetary totals use integer cents, pump prices use integer mills per volume unit, volume uses thousandths, and documented deterministic rounding reconciles totals. Fuel-log previews expire and bind vehicle/log versions. Writes use idempotency keys and expected versions; duplicate successful retries return the prior result and stale writes fail without partial changes. Reset restores the fixture baseline, audit/receipts, task-loaded events, and virtual-time-dependent results; snapshots are review evidence, not an Agent restore capability.
- **Events and notifications (required):** The App accepts task/Infra events for `fuel.price_observed`, `fuel.station_status_changed`, and `fuel.station_details_changed`. Events include stable event/station IDs, effective virtual time, fuel grade and payment-price type where applicable, normalized price/units, and bounded source/reason metadata. They are idempotent and reject malformed units, impossible values, or unknown stations. A price observation evaluates active alerts after committing the observation; duplicate/replayed event IDs do not retrigger them. Fueltrail optionally publishes compatible `channel.email` CloudEvents for the notices above. An alert identifies the Fueltrail station/alert as `source_object`, includes price, grade, area, observed time, and freshness, and excludes location history or payment credentials. Sender commit, receiver commit, and delivery acknowledgement remain separate outcomes.
- **Shared data and dependencies (required):** Shared references include `person_id`, `fuel_account_id`, `place_id`, `station_id`, `price_observation_id`, `vehicle_id`, `alert_id`, `notification_id`, `fuel_log_id`, `fuel_log_revision_id`, `preview_id`, and `event_id`. All places, stations, vehicles, prices, and logs are synthetic. No VIN, license plate, bank/card credential, live coordinate trace, or receipt image is stored. The initial App requires no attachments. The package runs independently with the repository's pinned public Core/runtime and Node.js 18+ using only built-ins. Mail 1.3.0 is optional in run assembly for notification evidence and is not required for core fuel workflows.
