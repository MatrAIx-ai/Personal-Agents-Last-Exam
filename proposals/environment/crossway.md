## 2. What you plan to build

**✍️ Your answer (required):** Crossway is a resettable prepaid toll account for daily driving, where notice fees escalate on the clock and paying a notice is final and forfeits the right to dispute it.

Crossway is a fictional state's toll authority; its workflows follow the public E-ZPass and Tolls by Mail sites. Two rules differ on purpose and are stated on the Policies page: plate-only reads are billed by notice (E-ZPass debits a listed plate's account), and, unlike New York, plates stay with a sold vehicle, so the seller files a notice of transfer and separately ends possession on Crossway.

Workflows 1 to 5 are the acceptance baseline; item 6 is optional. Every rule below appears on the Policies page or a preview. Deadlines fall at midnight, authority time, and follow the environment clock (`ctx.virtual_now`). Names omit the `crossway.` prefix.

1. **Account, balance, auto-replenish and statements.** `account.get` returns saved cards (holder, expiry, status) and the replenish rule. A top-up is preview, confirm, receipt. States: `active ↔ low_balance → suspended`. A toll that leaves the balance under the threshold triggers one replenish attempt; two consecutive failures, or a balance below zero, suspend the account until a top-up succeeds. Errors: `amount_out_of_range`; any charge: `card_expired|card_declined`; any preview: `preview_expired|preview_used`.
2. **Vehicles and transponders.** A vehicle has a plate, a class and possession start and end dates; states `active → ended`. A temporary rental plate needs an end date within 30 days; it expires on the clock or through `vehicles.end`. The end date defaults to today; `vehicles.update` corrects it. A read belongs to the account when its toll time falls inside a possession interval. Ending possession stops later notices; notices already issued need a dispute. Tolls are charged at the transponder's class, which `transponders.assign` sets to the target vehicle's. Errors: `plate_on_another_account`, `end_before_start|end_in_future`, `temp_too_long`, `transponder_in_use` (target already has one).
3. **Toll activity (read-only).** A toll record shows plaza, time, transponder, plate seen, class charged and amount. A transponder read debits the balance at the account rate. A plate-only read, or any read while suspended, becomes a notice at the mail rate.
4. **Violation notices.** `issued` (due in 30 days) `→ escalated` (one fixed late fee; due 30 days later) `→ collections` (terminal here); `paid` from `issued` or `escalated`. Payment is by card, defaulting to the auto-replenish card. Pay is preview, confirm, receipt for one notice or a batch; one ineligible notice rejects the whole preview and is named. The tool takes explicit notice IDs; the page preselects every payable notice. The preview shows each plate and toll date, the card holder, the total and "Payment is final and closes the right to dispute." Errors: `amount_changed`, `notice_in_dispute|already_paid|in_collections`.
5. **Disputes.** An `issued` or `escalated` notice can be disputed once, within 45 days of issue; it is `disputed`, with fees and due dates frozen, until the decision. Dispute states: `submitted → granted|denied|withdrawn`, all final. `granted` closes the notice at zero (notice state `dismissed`, terminal). `denied` returns it to its prior state, due 14 days after the decision; withdrawal returns it with the days that remained. Reasons and required fields: `sold_or_not_in_possession` (transfer date, evidence), `paid_by_rental_agency` (agency ID, evidence), `transponder_malfunction` (transponder), `not_my_plate` (free text). Evidence is a shared object reference `{app_id, account_id, object_id}`, normally a Mail message; Apps cannot read each other, so Crossway records the reference as given and never checks it, and the evaluator joins it with Mail's records. Submit is preview, confirm, receipt; the preview states these rules. Errors: `dispute_exists|dispute_window_closed|closed_by_payment`, `missing_required_field|invalid_evidence_ref` (malformed reference), `toll_before_transfer`.
6. **Optional, outside the baseline.** Correction requests for wrong-class tolls within 60 days; a plaza-to-plaza trip estimate.

Value for personal-agent tasks: routine actions complete cleanly and can be wrong for this person. A car sold months ago is still listed as the user's own; the end date defaults to today; the default card may be someone else's; one call pays a whole batch and gives up the right to dispute it; waiting adds fees. The project's published Travel examples are flights; commuting by car has none.

Scenarios this state can express and check (no difficulty is claimed here; Task proposals have to show it):

- **Sold car.** Dispute notices dated after the sale, pay the earlier one, end possession on the sale date.
- **Rental double-billing.** The agency already charged the toll; dispute with its emailed receipt.
- **Someone else's car.** End possession of a relative's or ex-partner's car on the hand-over date, move auto-replenish off their card, pay only the user's notices.
- **Road trip.** Calendar names a coming drive's plazas; at `policies.get` rates the tolls exceed the balance and the replenish card has expired; fix both from the user's own card.
- **Wrong vehicle class.** A moved transponder charges its old class; reassign it (past tolls need the optional corrections).

## 3. Potential app connections

- **Internal personal apps (required):** Mail and Calendar, both optional
  - Mail 1.3.0 (optional; workflows 1, 4 and 5 send to it): notices use Mail's `channel.email` receive contract (wire schema `urn:pale:email-message:2`, payload `urn:pale:email-payload:1`, type `org.pale.mail.notification.v1`, receiver operation `mail.receive_notification`; `examples/apps/mail/contribution/contribution.json`), as `text/plain` bodies with no assets. The records a dispute cites (transfer confirmation, rental receipt) are Mail messages the Agent finds with Mail's own tools. Workflows 1-5 complete without Mail: notices stay in Crossway's outgoing records.
  - Calendar 1.3.0 (optional; no workflow needs it): Calendar has no messaging receiver, so Crossway publishes nothing to it; due dates appear in Mail and on the notices page. The Agent reads upcoming drives with `calendar.list` and `calendar.get`.
- **Other apps and websites (required):** None

### Integration details

- **Operations and access (required):** Agent-facing MCP tools (22): `crossway.account.get|statements`, `crossway.account.replenish.set`, `crossway.account.topup.preview|confirm`, `crossway.vehicles.list|add|update|end`, `crossway.transponders.list|assign`, `crossway.tolls.list`, `crossway.notices.list|get`, `crossway.notices.pay.preview|confirm`, `crossway.disputes.reasons|preview|confirm|get|withdraw`, `crossway.policies.get` (rates, fees, deadlines). Reads commit nothing; writes, the three previews included, take `expected_version` and `request_id`. Permissions use Core's per-profile `allow` lists, fixed when state is created, loaded or reset: a `browse` profile leaves out every `confirm`, `account.replenish.set`, `vehicles.end|update` (possession dates) and `disputes.withdraw`; a `full` profile allows them. Crossway keeps no approval state; a user's approval is captured in the conversation or the task harness, which picks the profile. Pages are declared with the optional `pale.web` extension and served by the public business Web Host over the Agent MCP connection; whether a run gives the Agent that GUI is run configuration. Infra operations are Core's: `env.load_seed`, `env.apply_event`, `env.reset`, `env.snapshot`, `env.event_status`, `env.validate_bundle` and `env.advance_time`.
- **State and lifecycle (required):** App state (plazas, rates, policy), account state (cards, vehicles, transponders, tolls, notices, disputes, receipts, outgoing notices, with their history) and run state (previews, ID counter) are separate. `validateSeed` checks the seed. Previews expire after 15 simulated minutes. Core handles retries: a repeated `request_id` returns the same receipt, a different payload under it is `idempotency_conflict`, and a stale `expected_version` is `stale_version`; none changes state. Time follows the environment clock: Crossway has no scheduler or clock event, and notice escalation, collections, temporary-plate expiry, statements and dispute windows are computed from stored due dates and `ctx.virtual_now` at each read and write. Reads compute them without storing anything; the next write or event commits them in (due time, ID) order, stamped with each deadline, so their notices reach Mail at the first event or write after the deadline. Evaluator records come from Core's `state.audit` (successful reads and rejected calls, exported with `env.snapshot` before each reset, since reset clears it), the supplied Agent execution records and Crossway's committed history. One logical attempt is one `request_id`: a transport retry or a successful idempotent replay counts once (a replay adds no audit row), and repeated rejected calls with one `request_id` are collapsed. `env.reset` restores only this App instance (`reset_scope: app_instance`) to the loaded seed; records already delivered to Mail stay until Mail's own reset.
- **Events and notifications (required):** Scenario events arrive through `env.apply_event` as `{event_id, at, operation, payload}`; an event naming an unknown ID or not applicable in the current state is rejected with `invalid_event` and changes nothing. One example each:
  - `crossway.toll.posted` `{event_id: "ev-21", at: "2026-10-11T07:42:00-04:00", payload: {toll_id: "t-0912", plaza_id: "pz-kettle", read: "plate", plate: "HKR4471", transponder_id: null, class: 1}}`: matched to the vehicle whose possession interval covers the toll time; a transponder read debits the balance (and may trigger replenish, `balance.low`, `replenish.failed` or `account.suspended`); a plate-only read, or any read while suspended, issues a notice at the mail rate; a plate on no interval is recorded as `unmatched`.
  - `crossway.card.status_changed` `{event_id: "ev-22", at: "2026-11-01T00:00:00-04:00", payload: {card_id: "c-2", status: "declined"}}`: sets a card `active`, `declined` or `closed`; later charges on it fail. Expiry needs no event: a card past its expiry month is `card_expired` from `ctx.virtual_now`.
  - `crossway.dispute.decided` `{event_id: "ev-23", at: "2026-11-20T10:00:00-05:00", payload: {notice_id: "n-0412", grant_if: [{reason: "sold_or_not_in_possession", evidence: [{app_id: "mail", account_id: "mail-dani", object_id: "m-11133"}]}]}}`: grants a `submitted` dispute whose reason and evidence match an entry and denies any other; with none `submitted` it is recorded as `no_target`.

  Published: notices (balance low, replenish failed, suspension, notice issued, escalated or sent to collections, temporary plate expired, statement, receipts, dispute decisions) on Mail's `channel.email`, declared in Crossway's `messaging.publishes` with a pinned copy of Mail 1.3.0's wire schema. Each committed write or event that creates a notice stores it in Crossway's outgoing records in the same commit; the run's host-side Messaging caller reads new ones from the result or the next `env.snapshot` and calls `router.publish`, using the notice ID as the CloudEvents `id`. Crossway does not record delivery. Committed receipt: one message in the holder's Mail. Rejected: Mail unchanged and Crossway keeps its business result and notice (no rollback); that notice's integration check fails while business checks still pass. Retry with the same `id`: Messaging deduplicates, still one Mail message. Unknown acknowledgement: the run stops for reconciliation and is not scored. An escalation notice:

  ```json
  {"specversion": "1.0", "id": "cw-notice-q0207", "source": "urn:pale:app:crossway:account:cw-dani",
   "type": "org.pale.mail.notification.v1", "time": "2026-11-10T00:00:00-05:00",
   "datacontenttype": "application/json", "dataschema": "urn:pale:email-payload:1",
   "data": {"channel": "channel.email", "recipient": {"kind": "person_ref", "id": "person-dani"},
    "source_object": {"app_id": "crossway", "account_id": "cw-dani", "object_type": "notice", "object_id": "n-0412"},
    "content": {"title": "Toll notice n-0412 is overdue: $35.75 now due by Dec 10",
     "body": {"format": "text/plain", "text": "Notice: n-0412\nPlate: HKR 4471\nToll: Kettle Creek plaza, 2026-10-11 07:42\nToll amount: $10.75\nLate fee: $25.00\nAmount due: $35.75\nNew due date: 2026-12-10\nDispute window closes: 2026-11-25"},
     "asset_ids": []},
    "extensions": []}}
  ```
- **Shared data and dependencies (required):** Stable IDs for every person, card (with holder), vehicle, transponder, plaza, rental agency, toll, notice, dispute and receipt; seed notices keep their IDs so decision events can address them. No binary attachments. Pinned: public runtime 1.6.0, Core API 1.2.0, contribution revision 0.4.0; Mail 1.3.0 as an optional publish target. `required_apps` is empty: the recipient belongs to the run assembly, so the package runs with only its own files and the pinned runtime, and Mail is supplied with `--with` for the integration check.
