## 2. What you plan to build

**✍️ Your answer (required):** Crossway is a resettable prepaid toll account for daily driving, where notice fees escalate on the clock and paying a notice is final and forfeits the right to dispute it.

Crossway is a fictional state's toll authority; its workflows follow the public E-ZPass and Tolls by Mail sites. Two rules differ on purpose and are stated on the Policies page: plate-only reads are billed by notice (E-ZPass debits a listed plate's account), and, unlike New York, plates stay with a sold vehicle, so the seller files a notice of transfer and separately ends possession on Crossway.

Workflows 1 to 5 are the acceptance baseline; item 6 is optional. Every rule below appears on the Policies page or a preview. Deadlines fall at midnight, authority time. Names omit the `crossway.` prefix.

1. **Account, balance, auto-replenish and statements.** `account.get` returns saved cards (holder, expiry, status) and the replenish rule. A top-up is preview, confirm, receipt. States: `active ↔ low_balance → suspended`. A toll that leaves the balance under the threshold triggers one replenish attempt; two consecutive failures, or a balance below zero, suspend the account until a top-up succeeds. Errors: `amount_out_of_range`; any charge: `card_expired|card_declined`; any preview: `preview_expired|preview_used`.
2. **Vehicles and transponders.** A vehicle has a plate, a class and possession start and end dates; states `active → ended`. A temporary rental plate needs an end date within 30 days; it expires on the clock or through `vehicles.end`. The end date defaults to today; `vehicles.update` corrects it. A read belongs to the account when its toll time falls inside a possession interval. Ending possession stops later notices; notices already issued need a dispute. Tolls are charged at the transponder's class, which `transponders.assign` sets to the target vehicle's. Errors: `plate_on_another_account`, `end_before_start|end_in_future`, `temp_too_long`, `transponder_in_use` (target already has one).
3. **Toll activity (read-only).** A toll record shows plaza, time, transponder, plate seen, class charged and amount. A transponder read debits the balance at the account rate. A plate-only read, or any read while suspended, becomes a notice at the mail rate.
4. **Violation notices.** `issued` (due in 30 days) `→ escalated` (one fixed late fee; due 30 days later) `→ collections` (terminal here); `paid` from `issued` or `escalated`. Payment is by card, defaulting to the auto-replenish card. Pay is preview, confirm, receipt for one notice or a batch; one ineligible notice rejects the whole preview and is named. The tool takes explicit notice IDs; the page preselects every payable notice. The preview shows each plate and toll date, the card holder, the total and "Payment is final and closes the right to dispute." Errors: `amount_changed`, `notice_in_dispute|already_paid|in_collections`.
5. **Disputes.** An `issued` or `escalated` notice can be disputed once, within 45 days of issue; it is `disputed`, with fees and due dates frozen, until the decision. Dispute states: `submitted → granted|denied|withdrawn`, all final. `granted` closes the notice at zero (notice state `dismissed`, terminal). `denied` returns it to its prior state, due 14 days after the decision; withdrawal returns it with the days that remained. Reasons and required fields: `sold_or_not_in_possession` (transfer date, evidence), `paid_by_rental_agency` (agency ID, evidence), `transponder_malfunction` (transponder), `not_my_plate` (free text). Evidence is a reference `{kind, id}` to a Mail message; Crossway never reads its content, and checks that the ID resolves only if the binding allows it (question 3). Submit is preview, confirm, receipt; the preview states these rules. Errors: `dispute_exists|dispute_window_closed|closed_by_payment`, `missing_required_field|evidence_not_found`, `toll_before_transfer`.
6. **Optional, outside the baseline.** Correction requests for wrong-class tolls within 60 days; a plaza-to-plaza trip estimate.

Value for personal-agent tasks: routine actions complete cleanly and can be wrong for this person. A car sold months ago is still listed as the user's own; the end date defaults to today; the default card may be someone else's; one call pays a whole batch and gives up the right to dispute it; waiting adds fees. The project's published Travel examples are flights; commuting by car has none.

Scenarios this state can express and check (no difficulty is claimed here; Task proposals have to show it):

- **Sold car.** Dispute notices dated after the sale, pay the earlier one, end possession on the sale date.
- **Rental double-billing.** The agency already charged the toll; dispute with its emailed receipt.
- **Someone else's car.** End possession of a relative's or ex-partner's car on the hand-over date, move auto-replenish off their card, pay only the user's notices.
- **Road trip.** Calendar names a coming drive's plazas; at `policies.get` rates the tolls exceed the balance and the replenish card has expired; fix both from the user's own card.
- **Wrong vehicle class.** A moved transponder charges its old class; reassign it (past tolls need the optional corrections).

## 3. Potential app connections

- **Internal personal apps (required):** Mail and Calendar only
  - Mail: notifications, and the records (transfer confirmation, rental receipt) a dispute cites by message ID.
  - Calendar: notice due dates and plate expiry; the agent reads upcoming drives there.
  - Fallbacks: if apps cannot write to Calendar, due dates appear only in Mail and on the notices page. If Mail IDs cannot be resolved through the binding, evidence is recorded as given and tasks check the cited ID.
- **Other apps and websites (required):** None

### Integration details

- **Operations and access (required):** Agent-facing MCP tools (22): `crossway.account.get|statements`, `crossway.account.replenish.set`, `crossway.account.topup.preview|confirm`, `crossway.vehicles.list|add|update|end`, `crossway.transponders.list|assign`, `crossway.tolls.list`, `crossway.notices.list|get`, `crossway.notices.pay.preview|confirm`, `crossway.disputes.reasons|preview|confirm|get|withdraw`, `crossway.policies.get` (rates, fees, deadlines). Reads commit nothing; writes, the three previews included, take `expected_version` and `request_id`. Approval-gateable: every `confirm`, `account.replenish.set`, `vehicles.end|update` (possession dates) and `disputes.withdraw`. A GUI renderer shows the same records. Platform operations: `env.reset|load_seed|snapshot|apply_event`. The business model is separate from a thin interface layer, so it ports if the first infrastructure release differs from the interface proposed in #12 (open, not adopted).
- **State and lifecycle (required):** App state, account state and run state (last clock time seen, previews, applied event and request IDs, ID counter) are separate. `validateSeed` checks the seed; reset reloads it and clears run state; a snapshot captures account and run state. Previews expire after 15 simulated minutes. A repeated `request_id` returns the same receipt, or `request_id_conflict` if the payload differs; a stale `expected_version` applies nothing. IDs come from the counter and times from the platform clock.
- **Events and notifications (required):** Events use `{event_id, at, operation, payload}`. Scripted by the task: `crossway.toll.posted`, `crossway.card.status_changed` (expiry follows the clock), `crossway.dispute.decided`, `crossway.clock.advance`. `dispute.decided {notice_id, grant_if: [{reason, evidence_ids}]}` grants a `submitted` dispute whose reason and evidence match an entry and denies any other; with none `submitted` it is recorded as `no_target`. `toll.posted` can raise `balance.low`, `replenish.failed`, `account.suspended`. The deadline step raises `notice.escalated`, `notice.sent_to_collections`, `vehicle.temp_expired`, `statement.issued`. It runs before every write and event, in (time, ID) order until nothing is due, and stamps results with the deadline time; reads compute status from stored dates and the clock. A scenario schedules `clock.advance` at each midnight and before the grading snapshot, so notifications never depend on agent call timing. Published: text emails to Mail (`channel.email` in #12) for these, receipts and decisions, for example `notice.escalated {notice_id, amount_due, due_date}`; Calendar entries (section 3).
- **Shared data and dependencies (required):** Stable IDs for every person, card (with holder), vehicle, transponder, plaza, rental agency, toll, notice, dispute and receipt; seed notices keep their IDs so decision events can address them. No binary attachments. Needs shared Mail, Calendar and the platform clock.
