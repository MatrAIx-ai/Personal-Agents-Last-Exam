# Environment Track proposal

Proposed PR title: `[Environment Proposal] [Public service & law] CivicDesk public comments, service requests and records (Regulations.gov- and 311-inspired)`

## 1. App and domains

- **Authors / GitHub handles (required):** Sunny Yuan ([@ZichenYuan](https://github.com/ZichenYuan)).
- **Reference app or website / URL (required):** CivicDesk, an independently branded synthetic government services portal; patterns reference Regulations.gov (https://www.regulations.gov/) for public comments on proposed rules, municipal 311 portals for service requests (311 is the non-emergency city-services telephone number used across the US and Canada, the counterpart of 911; a city's 311 portal is the web or app form where residents report issues such as potholes or missed trash pickup and track the city's response), and public-records request portals. No government code, assets or real dockets are reproduced.
- **Primary domain (required):** Public service & law
- **Secondary domains (optional):** None

## 2. What you plan to build

**✍️ Your answer (required):** A resettable civic services portal where a user browses proposed rules with open, extended or closed comment periods, drafts and submits public comments (named or anonymous) behind a required acknowledgment and receives a tracking number and posting status; files and tracks service requests with status timelines; submits public records requests that return clarification questions, fee estimates needing approval and documents; and, as optional workflows, books appointments at a city office and subscribes to docket and meeting notices delivered to Mail. Deadlines, extensions, corrections and status changes run on the simulated clock.

Workflows 1 to 3 form the initial scope. Workflows 4 and 5 are optional: they are not part of the acceptance baseline and will be built only if design review asks for them. Each delivered workflow is complete end to end, including modification, cancellation, confirmation and realistic errors:

1. **Proposed rules and public comments.** Search dockets; a docket page with summary, supporting documents, comment period (opens and closes at a time in a stated time zone) and status (open, extended, closed). Draft a comment with validation (length, required fields, attachment types); choose named or anonymous; a required acknowledgment checkbox; submit to receive a receipt with a tracking number; status moves from received to posted on a schedule, or is returned with a reason. A second distinct submission on the same docket triggers a duplicate warning; a supplemental comment is a separate flow. Errors: closed period, missing acknowledgment, oversized attachment. Submitted comments cannot be withdrawn, as in the reference.
2. **Service requests (311-style).** "311" is the non-emergency telephone number that many US and Canadian cities use for municipal services, the counterpart of 911 for emergencies. By extension, a city's 311 portal is the web or app form where residents report non-emergency problems such as a pothole, a missed trash pickup or a broken streetlight, and then track what the city does about it. In this environment: create a request (category, location, description, photo); status timeline (received, assigned, in progress, resolved, closed); add a comment; duplicate detection against open requests nearby; reopen a closed request.
3. **Public records requests.** Create a request; clerk clarification questions arrive as events and need a reply; a fee estimate requires approval before processing; statutory response deadlines are computed from the clock; documents are delivered; the user can withdraw.
4. **Appointments (optional).** City office services with limited slots; book, reschedule and cancel; a required-documents checklist; reminders.
5. **Subscriptions and meetings (optional).** Subscribe to docket updates and topics; council meeting agendas with public-comment sign-up deadlines; notices delivered to Mail.

Value for personal-agent tasks: civic tasks are deadline-bound, often irreversible, require the user's own reasons and identity choices, and change under the user's feet through extensions and corrections. The author's reviewed PersonalAgentEval cases supply a downstream task on this environment, submitting a public comment from the user's own reasons and closing it out correctly (submitted alongside this proposal as a linked Task PR); further tasks planned on it are a records request with a fee approval, a service request that duplicates an open one, and, if the optional workflows are built, a meeting sign-up before the cutoff.

## 3. Potential app connections

- **Internal personal apps (required):** Mail: receipts, status changes, clarification requests and extension notices arrive here, and newsletters in Mail link to dockets. Calendar: comment and sign-up deadlines and appointments. Files: the user's notes that hold their reasons, and attachments to comments and requests. Contacts: None.
- **Other apps and websites (required):** None in the initial scope. A news or information app could link dockets later (TBD).

### Integration details

- **Operations and access (required):** Agent-facing MCP operations: `dockets.search|get|documents`, `comments.draft|validate|submit|receipt|status|list_mine`, `requests.create|get|list|comment|attach|reopen`, `records.create|get|list|reply|fee.approve|withdraw`, and, for the optional workflows, `appointments.slots|book|reschedule|cancel|list`, `subscriptions.create|list|delete`, `meetings.list|signup|cancel_signup`. `comments.submit` and `records.fee.approve` are approval-gated writes, as are `appointments.book` and `meetings.signup` if built; `comments.submit` requires `acknowledged: true` and rejects otherwise. A GUI renderer presents docket search, docket, comment form, review, receipt, my comments, request form and status, and records request pages for computer-use agents, plus appointments and subscriptions pages if the optional workflows are built. Platform-facing operations: `env.reset`, `env.load_seed`, `env.snapshot`, `env.inject_event`.
- **State and lifecycle (required):** App state (dockets, documents, fee tables and, for the optional workflows, offices and meeting calendars) is separated from account state (comments, requests, records requests and, optionally, appointments and subscriptions) and run state (clock, drafts, event log). Reset reloads the seed and clears drafts; submissions return receipts with tracking numbers; repeating a submit with the same draft ID returns the existing receipt (idempotent), while a second distinct submission on the same docket is flagged as a possible duplicate; statutory deadlines and period closings are computed from the clock in the docket's stated time zone.
- **Events and notifications (required):** Accepted events: `docket.period_extended`, `docket.correction_published`, `docket.closed`, `comment.status_changed`, `request.status_changed`, `records.clarification_requested`, `records.fee_estimated`, `records.delivered`, and, for the optional workflows, `appointment.slot_released` and `meeting.agenda_published`. Published notifications: text-email notifications to Mail for receipts, status changes, clarification requests, extensions and agenda notices, and calendar updates for deadlines and appointments, following the shared Mail and Calendar apps' formats. Task scenarios decide when events occur.
- **Shared data and dependencies (required):** Stable IDs for the person, account, docket, comment, request, records request, appointment and meeting; attachments (text PDFs and images) addressed by asset ID. Depends on the shared Mail, Calendar and Files apps and the platform clock; no external service at run time.
