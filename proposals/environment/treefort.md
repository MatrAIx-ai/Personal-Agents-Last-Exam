## 2. What you plan to build

**✍️ Your answer (required):** Treefort is a resettable family-settings app for a game platform: guardians set each child's play-time schedule, content ceiling and spending rules, and decide the requests a child sends for more time, a purchase or a title above the ceiling. The app enforces who may decide what and whose money pays, while the family's own rules, which can be stricter or looser than the settings, live elsewhere in the person's history. Four workflows are complete in the first version.

**Entities.** Family (time zone, guardians with permissions, children), child settings (content ceiling, purchase rule, caps), weekday schedule and dated exceptions, play session, request (`extra_time`, `purchase`, `content_exception`) with its decision, wallet (allowance and gift-credit buckets), guardian card with a monthly cap, ledger entry, allowance rule, gift, notice.

**Simplifications.** One App instance holds one family, and the Agent acts as one bound guardian. Children never use Treefort: their requests arrive as events. Treefort never buys anything: it records decisions and money holds, and the store reports the outcome by event. Money is integer cents in one currency. Ratings are a four-step ladder (`everyone`, `everyone_10`, `teen`, `mature`). Days are local dates in the family's time zone.

- **W1 Family and child settings.** Read the family, each guardian's permissions and each child's settings and status for today; change a child's content ceiling, purchase rule (`always_ask`, `ask_above_cap`, `blocked`) and caps; pause and resume play. Paths: modify = `children.update`; cancel = `play.resume` ends a pause; errors = unknown child (`not_found`), a guardian without the permission (`not_permitted`), an invalid cap (`invalid_amount`). Confirm: not applicable in W1 and W2, because a setting spends nothing and another write undoes it; in W3 and W4 a decision or top-up is one explicit call that names the request or amount and the funding source, and its result states what was held or charged. Whether the Agent must ask the person first is the task's rule; Treefort records which call was made.
- **W2 Play-time schedule.** Read the weekly schedule and today's position (limit, minutes played, minutes left, allowed window, bedtime, pause). Set a weekday's limit, window and bedtime; add, change or remove a dated exception such as "no limit on the first day of the holidays" or "30 minutes less this Thursday". Paths: modify = `schedule.set_day`, `schedule.exceptions.update`; cancel = `schedule.exceptions.remove`; errors = a window that ends before it starts or a limit over 24 hours (`invalid_schedule`), a second exception for the same child and date (`exception_conflict`), changing a past date (`invalid_state`).
- **W3 Requests and decisions.** Requests arrive as events and wait for a decision until they expire. The Agent lists and reads them (a bundle shows each component and its rating), approves, denies, or revokes an approved purchase that the store has not completed. Paths: modify = approve extra time for fewer minutes than asked; cancel = `requests.revoke`; errors = deciding a closed request (`invalid_state`), an expired one (`request_expired`), an item above the ceiling without an explicit exception (`rating_above_ceiling`), over the per-item cap (`over_item_cap`), not enough money in the chosen source (`insufficient_funds`, `over_monthly_cap`), and a guardian who may not make that kind of decision (`not_permitted`).
- **W4 Money.** Read each child's wallet, ledger and a monthly statement that separates allowance, gift credit and the guardian's card; set, change or pause the allowance rule; add money once from the guardian's card. Paths: modify = `allowance.set`; cancel = pause the rule; errors = `not_permitted`, `invalid_amount`, `over_monthly_cap`.

Rules:

- Authority. Each guardian holds a subset of six permissions: `manage_settings`, `manage_schedule`, `decide_time`, `decide_purchases`, `decide_content` and `manage_money`. A call outside the bound guardian's permissions returns `not_permitted` and changes nothing. A family may require a second guardian's consent for chosen kinds of decision; the other guardian answers in their own session, which reaches Treefort as an event.
- Play time. A child's limit for a local day is the weekday rule, replaced or adjusted by that date's exception, plus extra time approved for that day. Play is possible only inside the allowed window, before bedtime and while not paused; whichever comes first ends it. Minutes played come from recorded sessions.
- Requests. A request closes at `expires_at`; a purchase request also closes when its offer ends. A denial is final for that request. Approved extra time may be less than asked and applies to the request's local day only.
- Purchases. The approval names the source of money: `allowance`, `gift_credit` or `guardian_card`. Checks run in this order: permission; the request is still open; the item's effective rating, which is the highest of its own rating and its components' ratings, against the child's ceiling (above it needs `allow_above_ceiling: true`, the `decide_content` permission and any required second consent); the per-item cap; then funds, which means the bucket's balance or the card's monthly cap counting captured and held amounts in the family's calendar month. Approval places a hold; settlement captures or releases it.
- Money. Every balance change is a ledger entry naming the bucket, the cause and the request, gift or rule behind it. Gift credit keeps who gave it and their note. Allowance top-ups run at the rule's local time on the virtual clock.

**Value for personal-agent tasks.** A request that the app would let through is not thereby one the family wants approved. Whose money it is, who may say yes to what, and which evenings are exceptions are agreed between people and recorded in notes and messages, not in the settings; a child's request arrives while the guardian is at work; and the other guardian may need to agree first. Treefort makes the outcome checkable: which request was decided, by whom, from which bucket, what was held or released, and what each guardian was told. Distinct task situations (separate Task proposals): (1) a bundle is affordable from gift credit, but one component is rated above what this parent approves without being asked (Task #112 plans this on #11 with family settings); (2) a child asks for more time on a school night and the family's standing rule depends on homework that only a message confirms; (3) set holiday exceptions for a week and restore the normal schedule afterwards; (4) a title above the ceiling needs the co-parent's consent while they are travelling, before the offer ends; (5) answer "what did the games cost me this month" across allowance, gift credit and the card; (6) a child sends the same request again after a denial. Fixtures are plain seed data, so each task loads its own family, schedule, requests, wallets and history.

**Boundaries and overlap.** Proposal list checked on 2026-11-19: no other family-settings or parental-control environment. #11 (Beam, same domain) is the game store: catalog, checkout, gifts, library, wallet and refunds, with family accounts and spending limits listed as a later extension. Treefort is the family side only and has no catalog or checkout. The two share no state: a task that uses both seeds consistent items and schedules each App's own events. Task #112 plans to use #11 for a child profile and family settings, with a small in-task store as a fallback; I will ask both authors in this PR whether Treefort's request and wallet model fits. #85 (Kinthread) is a family chat and could hold the family's own rules; it needs no connection.

## 3. Potential app connections

- **Internal personal apps (required):** Mail only, optional and send-only, used by W3 and W4 notices: when `email_notices` is on for the bound guardian, each notice is also built as a plain-text message for the existing receive declaration of Mail 1.3.0 in `examples/apps/mail` (channel `channel.email`, wire `urn:pale:email-message:2`, payload `urn:pale:email-payload:1`, type `org.pale.mail.notification.v1`, receiver operation `mail.receive_notification`), reviewed at `main` commit `428c757` ([wire schema](https://github.com/MatrAIx-ai/Personal-Agents-Last-Exam/blob/428c757/examples/apps/mail/schemas/router-message.schema.json)). Treefort declares the matching `messaging.publishes` entry and builds no inbox. Calendar, Contacts and Messages: none. No workflow needs the Agent to call another App's tools, and all four workflows complete with Treefort alone.
- **Other apps and websites (required):** None required. Optional pairing with #11 happens through task-supplied seeds and events, not through an App-to-App channel: Treefort stores the store's item reference and order reference as opaque values. No external website or payment service is called at run time.

### Integration details

- **Operations and access (required):** 22 Agent-facing MCP operations over stdio (`service.cjs --role agent`), registered in `tools.json` as `treefort.<name>`. Read (10): `family.get`, `children.get`, `schedule.get`, `activity.list`, `requests.list`, `requests.get`, `wallet.get`, `wallet.ledger`, `statement.get`, `notices.list`. Write (12): `children.update`, `play.pause`, `play.resume`, `schedule.set_day`, `schedule.exceptions.add`, `schedule.exceptions.update`, `schedule.exceptions.remove`, `requests.approve`, `requests.deny`, `requests.revoke`, `allowance.set`, `wallet.topup`. Every write takes Core's `request_id` and `expected_version` and returns the object's `id` with the committed version. The target object is always the argument `id` and a time window is always `start` / `end`; the reference host's audit projection at `428c757` keeps `id`, `start` and `end` among the few inputs it records, so its audit rows show which child or request each call touched, reads included. The Agent acts as the bound guardian set by Infra, never through an argument; a task can narrow tools, objects and fields with `policies.json`. App errors: `not_found`, `not_permitted`, `invalid_state`, `request_expired`, `rating_above_ceiling`, `over_item_cap`, `insufficient_funds`, `over_monthly_cap`, `invalid_schedule`, `exception_conflict`, `invalid_amount`. Core adds `invalid_arguments`, `permission_denied`, `stale_version` and `idempotency_conflict`. Infra-only operations: `env.load_seed`, `env.apply_event`, `env.reset`, `env.snapshot`, `env.event_status`, `env.validate_bundle`; they are not listed on the Agent connection and Core returns `permission_denied` if the Agent calls them. The first version has no GUI and no reviewer console.
- **State and lifecycle (required):** State is separate per run and App instance, and one instance holds one family seen through one guardian's account (`isolation_keys: run_id, app_id`). The seed schema carries the family (guardians, permissions, children, settings, schedules and exceptions) and its history (sessions, requests with decisions, wallets, ledger, gifts, allowance rules, notices, activity rows), so a task can load months of play, earlier denials and a part-spent month. `validateSeed` rejects duplicate IDs, dangling references, timestamps without a UTC offset, a ledger that does not add up to the stored balances, a request whose status contradicts its decision, and an account that the binding does not declare (`invalid_seed`). Writes follow Core: an exact repeat of a committed `request_id` or `event_id` returns the stored receipt marked `duplicate`; the same ID with different input returns `idempotency_conflict`; a stale `expected_version` returns `stale_version`; a failed call leaves data, version, balances and notices unchanged and Core appends a denial audit row. Reads never change business data: a child's status for today, a request's remaining time and month-to-date totals are computed from stored records and `virtual_now`. Reset is Core's: it restores the loaded seed and the time loaded with it (`reset_scope: app_instance`) and touches no other App; messages already delivered to Mail stay there. Evaluator records come from three places: (a) App state in the snapshot (each request's decision with guardian, time, source of money and note; ledger entries; activity rows with sequence, virtual time, actor `agent`, `event` or `timer`, action and object ID for every committed write, event and timer effect; notices); (b) Core audit rows for every allowed or denied call with virtual time and the projected `id`; (c) full arguments and results in the supplied Agent execution trace. They join on `request_id`, `event_id` and version. Take the snapshot before a reset, because reset clears the audit.
- **Events and notifications (required):** Seven events through `env.apply_event`, in Core's envelope. The clock moves only through events. For an event at time T, Treefort first runs timers due at or before T in due-time then ID order (expire requests, end timed pauses, pay allowances), then applies the event, then writes notices. Core rejects an event dated before the App's current time (`invalid_event`); Treefort also rejects a payload timestamp later than the event's `at`, an unknown child or request, a duplicate request, session or gift ID, a withdrawal or consent for a request that is not waiting for it, and a settlement for a request that is not `approved` (`invalid_event`, state unchanged).

- **Shared data and dependencies (required):** Stable IDs for the family, guardians, children, schedules, exceptions, sessions, requests, gifts, ledger entries, allowance rules and notices. The bound guardian's `account_id` and `owner_person_id` are the shared values of the run's `binding.json`; other guardians, children and gift givers carry a shared person ID only where the task binds one, so the same person can appear in Mail or a chat App. Treefort is the source of truth for settings, decisions, holds and balances and keeps no copy of another App's records; `store_ref` and `store_order_ref` are opaque references it never dereferences. Missing data is explicit: an unknown ID returns `not_found`; a day without sessions reports zero minutes; a child without an allowance rule returns `allowance: null`. All records are JSON; there are no images, audio, video or attachments (`capabilities.resources: false`, 0 of the profile's 64 assets). Runtime needs: the public Core/Messaging runtime 1.5.0 (Core API 1.2.0) at `main` commit `428c757`; Mail is optional; `dependencies.required_apps` is empty; no external service. Local dates use the family's IANA time zone through Node's built-in `Intl`, so no package is installed.
