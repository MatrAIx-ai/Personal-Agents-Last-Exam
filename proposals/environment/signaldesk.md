## 2. What you plan to build

**✍️ Your answer (required):** A resettable browser App and MCP business interface for one fictional mobile carrier. A household has three existing mobile lines. An agent can inspect usage, compare three household plans, schedule or cancel a plan change, manage two types of international data pass, and verify the resulting charges and service status. Five complete workflows share one authoritative business state.

| Workflow | First-version behavior | Observable outcome |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Account and usage inspection | Read the household, line owners, actor role, current billing cycle, active and pending plans, domestic shared-pool usage, per-line hotspot usage, roaming balances, and recent activity. | Account and line records identify whose service is affected and which allowances apply now. |
| Plan comparison | Compare three fixed plans, including household monthly price, shared domestic data, per-line hotspot ceiling, included roaming destinations and allowances, and next-cycle effective date. | A comparison exposes all decision-relevant terms and the impact on all three lines. |
| Plan change | Preview a next-cycle household plan change; confirm or abandon it; inspect its receipt; cancel or replace the change before it takes effect. | The active plan remains unchanged until the boundary; exactly one pending change can exist. |
| Roaming-pass management | Preview and confirm a pass for one line, a supported destination set, and a future UTC start time. Read its status; reschedule or cancel it before activation; reject overlapping passes on the same line. | The pass is scheduled, active, expired, or cancelled with explicit coverage, validity, remaining data, and charge status. |
| Charges and notifications | Inspect a simple carrier charge ledger, monthly statements, itemized pass charges, and in-App notices for usage thresholds, pending changes, activation, and expiry. | Every simulated charge has a source record; service state, receipts, and notices agree. |

**First-version scale.** One carrier; one household and three lines per fixture; three plan definitions; two pass products; six destinations (Canada, Mexico, France, Germany, Japan, and South Korea); five principal browser views corresponding to the workflows above; and six deterministic fixture snapshots. The two pass products provide different durations and data allowances. Their coverage sets, fixed prices, and all plan entitlements will be published in the versioned catalog during M1. Task authors can replace household history and usage through the seed schema without changing the App implementation.

**Business rules to agree in design review.**

- Each household uses one plan for all three lines. Plan changes take effect only at the next billing-cycle boundary. There is no mid-cycle upgrade or proration in v1. A cancellation of a pending change retains the active plan. Replacing a pending change requires a new preview and confirmation.
- The domestic pool is shared. Hotspot usage also consumes that pool and is subject to a per-line ceiling. Roaming balances are separate. Every usage record identifies its line, type, amount, and occurrence time; units and rounding are documented. Exhaustion changes the displayed service allowance/status and generates a notice; it never silently purchases extra data.
- A roaming pass belongs to exactly one line and has a half-open validity interval `[start, end)` in UTC. It consumes included roaming data first, then eligible active-pass data, using a documented deterministic order. Usage outside coverage or available balances records the limitation without an automatic purchase or unlisted overage fee.
- Passes may be modified or cancelled only before activation. Rescheduling is an atomic previewed update that rechecks overlap and coverage. Active or expired passes cannot be cancelled or refunded in v1; the App explains this before confirmation and returns a visible error for invalid requests. Buying an overlapping pass is rejected without a charge or partial mutation.
- A fixed charge becomes due at pass activation. At a billing boundary the new plan takes effect and its monthly charge becomes due. Reads show these amounts immediately. The next successful write or business event posts them once, as described in §3. Statements reference the same charges. Payment collection, taxes, discounts and device financing are outside v1.
- All consequential writes use preview/confirm/abandon with an explicit target, current version, effective time, terms, and total cost. A preview is not a completed change. Stale confirmations fail without the requested business mutation. Repeating the same confirmed request returns its existing receipt; a reused request ID with different input is rejected. Core commits and version handling are reused where available.

**Value for personal-agent tasks.** The same request to reduce a bill can require different choices depending on a household member's study needs, hotspot reliance, or upcoming travel. A pass bought for the wrong family member, a cheaper plan that starts after departure, or a previously scheduled downgrade can undermine an otherwise plausible plan. A task could ask an agent to preserve a child's connectivity while adapting a parent's roaming arrangements after a trip changes. The App supplies discoverable terms and consequences; task authors supply personal history, instructions, authorization, event timing, and grading. It will not embed a preferred plan, a task solution, or hidden user preferences.

## 3. Potential app connections

### Integration details

- **Operations and access (required):** Propose `apps/signaldesk/`, subject to maintainer location review, with versioned business schemas, fixtures, tests, and documented MCP operations. Reads cover account/line details, usage, the catalog, comparisons, pending changes, passes, charges, receipts, and notices. Writes cover previews, confirmations, abandonment, and valid modification/cancellation paths. Platform management uses the public runtime's separate Infra connection for seed/history loading, event injection, reset, and snapshots; Agent connections must neither discover nor invoke those operations. Use lowercase `signaldesk.<name>` tools. Every write requires `request_id` and `expected_version`. SignalDesk supplies the business page and controller under `gui/`. The [shared Web Host](https://github.com/MatrAIx-ai/Personal-Agents-Last-Exam/blob/main/runtime/web/README.md) serves them over its fixed Agent MCP connection. Use the Host's shared stylesheet and confirm any additional styling with Infra at M1.

- **State and lifecycle (required):** Separate immutable catalog data from household/account records and run-local state. Each account and line has a stable synthetic ID; all records, previews, passes, usage entries, statements, and notices resolve within the authorized account. An owner may manage all household lines; a member may inspect only their own line and the terms applicable to it, and cannot mutate the household plan or buy a pass. The seed identifies the acting person and role. The member fixture's policy allow-list hides write tools, and handlers enforce account and line scope. Account access is distinct from the task's current authorization to act; previews and API availability do not create consent. Independent runs must not share mutable records. Load fixtures/history through the seed contract; use Core's committed state and revision checks for writes. A failed action leaves its business state unchanged, and concurrent confirmations cannot overwrite a newer plan/pass choice. Reset reconstructs the loaded baseline, including Core's seeded virtual time, usage, pending changes, pass statuses, charges, notices, and seeded history. Core clears run receipts and audit on reset. Export evidence before reset. Infra snapshots are inspection evidence; arbitrary snapshot restoration or whole-agent reset is not promised.

- **Events and notifications (required):** Infra advances Core time with `env.advance_time`. SignalDesk uses `ctx.virtual_now` as its sole clock. Advancing time calls no App handler. At that instant a read derives the effective pass status, billing cycle, plan and charges due without modifying stored records or creating notices. On the next successful write or business event, the handler applies all due boundaries in chronological order before applying the requested action. Both parts commit together. For coincident boundaries, apply the cycle and plan change first, expire ending passes, then activate starting passes. Identify each monthly charge by account and cycle, and each activation charge by pass. Issue each charge and its linked notice once, retaining the original effective time even if the commit is later. Failed writes leave the effects outstanding. [Runtime time contract](https://github.com/MatrAIx-ai/Personal-Agents-Last-Exam/blob/main/docs/runtime-time.md)

  Infra submits the following events on the household instance. The Task sets the order of events with the same timestamp. Core rejects events earlier than the current clock and handles duplicate event IDs.

  ```json
  {
    "event_id": "usage-001",
    "at": "2026-11-02T10:00:00Z",
    "operation": "signaldesk.post_usage",
    "payload": {
      "line_id": "line-02",
      "kind": "domestic_data",
      "amount_mb": 250,
      "occurred_at": "2026-11-02T10:00:00Z"
    }
  }
  ```

  For a seeded line with at least 250 MB remaining, this records one 250 MB usage entry against the line and reduces the household's remaining domestic allowance by 250 MB. Replaying the event leaves both values unchanged.

  ```json
  {
    "event_id": "account-update-001",
    "at": "2026-11-02T10:05:00Z",
    "operation": "signaldesk.account_update",
    "payload": {
      "actor_person_id": "person-owner",
      "action": "cancel_pending_plan_change",
      "plan_change_id": "change-01"
    }
  }
  ```

  Given a seeded owner and a pending change scheduled for the next cycle, this records the owner's cancellation, clears that pending change and issues an in-App notice linked to it. The active plan stays in effect. Reject a foreign target, an unauthorized actor or a change already in effect. In-App notices identify the affected account or line, effective time and source record.

- **Shared data and dependencies (required):** Use synthetic `person_id`, `account_id`, `line_id`, `plan_id`, `pass_id`, and receipt references with a binding fixture. The task assembly supplies trusted identity/account bindings. Initial/history records, catalog data, events, and statements are small UTF-8 JSON; the GUI presents accessible HTML and the shared Host stylesheet. No attachment bytes, product photographs, maps, OCR, or copied carrier assets are needed. Required platform dependency: the public [Core runtime](https://github.com/MatrAIx-ai/Personal-Agents-Last-Exam/blob/main/runtime/README.md), using the public reference profile documented on `main`. Pin an exact repository commit and compatible contracts at M1 and record them in delivery evidence; do not target a floating version during verification. No other App is required. Implementation will follow the [App delivery guide](https://github.com/MatrAIx-ai/Personal-Agents-Last-Exam/blob/main/docs/app-contribution.md), [integration guide](https://github.com/MatrAIx-ai/Personal-Agents-Last-Exam/blob/main/docs/app-integrations.md), and [architecture boundaries](https://github.com/MatrAIx-ai/Personal-Agents-Last-Exam/blob/main/docs/environment-architecture.md).
