# Environment Track proposal

**Proposed PR title:** `[Environment Proposal] [Money & insurance + Social & communities] Ledgerleaf Shared Expenses`

Ledgerleaf is an independently branded synthetic shared-expense ledger for households, trips, and other groups. Its workflows are informed by Splitwise's public group, expense, balance, and settlement experience, but the implementation will not copy Splitwise branding, source code, screenshots, user data, or policy text.

## 2. What you plan to build

**✍️ Your answer (required):** Build a resettable browser App and MCP business interface for a fictional group expense ledger. The initial version will support four complete workflows:

1. **Create and manage a group:** create a household, trip, couple, or general group; choose one currency; add synthetic member profiles; inspect membership and balances; rename or archive the group; and remove a member when policy permits. Archived groups remain readable but reject new expenses. Member removal preserves historical attribution and either requires a zero balance or leaves the former member visible until outstanding balances are settled.
2. **Record a shared expense with flexible rules:** add an expense with description, date, category, notes, one or more payers, and participating members. Split the owed amount equally, by exact amounts, by percentages, or by integer shares. The App validates that paid and owed shares each equal the expense total, performs deterministic minor-unit rounding, and previews resulting balance changes before commit. Unsupported currency, nonmembers, invalid totals, stale group state, and duplicate submissions fail without partial ledger changes.
3. **Review balances and settle debts:** derive per-member net balances and pairwise obligations from the complete group ledger; optionally preview a simplified settlement plan that reduces the number of payments while preserving every member's net balance; and record an external cash, bank, or payment-app settlement. Ledgerleaf records settlement evidence but never moves money. Settlements are immutable; mistakes are corrected by an explicit reversal linked to the original record. Overpayment, self-payment, duplicate settlement, and stale-balance confirmation are rejected.
4. **Modify or remove a bill safely:** update description, date, category, notes, payers, participants, amount, or split rule with an expected expense version and a full replacement preview. Each accepted edit creates a revision containing before/after shares and balance deltas. Deletion creates a tombstone rather than erasing history. Only the expense creator or a seeded group administrator may edit/delete; external-member edits arrive as task events. Existing settlement records are never rewritten when an expense changes, so any resulting balance remains visible for follow-up.

The environment is useful for personal-agent evaluation because correct group accounting depends on who paid, who benefited, the group's agreed split rule, prior edits, settlements, rounding, membership, and explicit authority. It supports tasks such as correcting a vacation bill after one traveler leaves, splitting utilities using unequal shares, reconciling several people who paid different amounts, or recording a settlement without accidentally duplicating it.

The GUI will expose the same business state and actions as MCP through group list/detail, expense editor and revision history, member balances, settlement preview, and activity views. It will not be a generic state inspector.

**Related scope and added value:** [PR #68 PalePay](https://github.com/MatrAIx-ai/Personal-Agents-Last-Exam/pull/68) includes a Venmo-inspired single-expense split that creates payment requests, alongside person search, payments, social visibility, balance funding, and bank transfers. Ledgerleaf instead provides a long-lived group accounting ledger: multiple payers, four split-rule types, balances across many expenses, debt simplification, external settlement records, and editable bills with revision history. It has no payment rail, bank balance, social feed, or payment-request lifecycle. The two environments are complementary; a future task could use Ledgerleaf to calculate an obligation and PalePay to pay it, but neither depends on the other in v1.

## 3. Potential app connections

- **Internal personal apps (required):** Mail 1.3.0 is optional in v1.
  - **Mail 1.3.0, optional:** Ledgerleaf will publish group-invitation, expense-added/changed, and settlement-recorded notices through Mail's implemented `channel.email` receive contract in [`examples/apps/mail/contribution/contribution.json`](../../examples/apps/mail/contribution/contribution.json). Connected validation will verify the actual destination Mail record and Messaging receipt. Ledgerleaf remains independently runnable without Mail because all activity is retained in the App. Failed or unavailable delivery is reported separately and does not roll back a ledger commit; v1 does not claim an atomic outbox or automatic queued retry.
- **Other apps and websites (required):** PalePay PR #68 is related but not required. A future Agent may separately read a Ledgerleaf settlement obligation and initiate a PalePay payment, with independent confirmation and failure semantics. v1 has no direct integration or shared state with PalePay, banks, cards, or payment processors. Other members' actions are deterministic task/Infra events rather than live accounts.

### Integration details

- **Operations and access (required):** Agent-facing MCP and GUI operations will cover group list/create/read/update/archive, member list/add/remove, expense list/read/preview/create/update/delete, balances/obligations read, simplified-plan preview, settlement list/preview/record/reverse, and activity/revision reads. Representative names are `group.list`, `group.create`, `group.get`, `group.update`, `group.archive`, `group.member.add`, `group.member.remove`, `expense.preview`, `expense.create`, `expense.update`, `expense.delete`, `group.balances.get`, `settlement.plan.preview`, `settlement.preview`, `settlement.record`, `settlement.reverse`, and `group.activity.list`; exact schemas will be declared in `tools.json`. Infra-only `env.load_seed`, `env.apply_event`, `env.advance_time`, `env.snapshot`, and `env.reset` load histories, inject other-member actions, inspect outcomes, and restore a clean run. Setup, reset, time control, and event injection are not Agent operations.
- **State and lifecycle (required):** Each App instance represents one user's Ledgerleaf account and is isolated by Core's `run_id` and `app_id`. Synthetic seed data includes member profiles, groups/roles, expenses and revisions, settlements/reversals, and activity history. Amounts use integer minor units in one currency per group. Balances and obligations are derived from the current nondeleted expense revisions plus settlement/reversal records; they are never independently mutable. Previews are immutable, expire at a declared virtual time, and bind the referenced group/expense versions. Writes use idempotency keys and expected versions. Duplicate successful retries return the prior result, stale writes fail without partial changes, and commits return object status plus Core epoch/revision. Reset restores the fixture baseline, including audit/receipts and task-loaded events; snapshots are review evidence, not an Agent restore capability.
- **Events and notifications (required):** The App accepts task/Infra events for `group.member_joined`, `group.member_left`, `expense.created_by_member`, `expense.updated_by_member`, `expense.deleted_by_member`, and `settlement.recorded_by_member`. Events include stable event/group/member/object IDs, effective virtual time, expected object version where applicable, and complete shares or settlement data; they are idempotent and reject invalid membership, authorization, totals, or lifecycle transitions. Other-member events use the same ledger invariants as Agent writes and cannot silently modify unrelated records. Ledgerleaf optionally publishes compatible `channel.email` CloudEvents for the notices above. A notice identifies the Ledgerleaf object as `source_object`, summarizes the change and group, and excludes private payment credentials because none are stored. Sender commit, receiver commit, and delivery acknowledgement remain separate outcomes.
- **Shared data and dependencies (required):** Shared references include `person_id`, `ledger_account_id`, `group_id`, `member_id`, `expense_id`, `expense_revision_id`, `preview_id`, `settlement_id`, `reversal_id`, and `event_id`. Profiles, notes, amounts, and settlement methods are synthetic. No bank/card credentials or actual transaction tokens are stored. The initial App requires no attachments or receipt images. The package runs independently with the repository's pinned public Core/runtime and Node.js 18+ using only built-ins. Mail 1.3.0 is optional in run assembly for notification evidence; PalePay is only a related future workflow and not a package dependency.
