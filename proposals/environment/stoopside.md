## 2. What you plan to build

**✍️ Your answer (required):** Stoopside is a resettable community-groups app (capped events, audience-scoped posts, sign-up sheets) that records who was shown or sent what, so tasks about who learns what can be checked without a judge.

One user account is bound per instance; other members act through scenario events. Group, role, event, RSVP and waitlist structure follow the reference; the claim window, post audiences, child counts, sign-up sheets, member export and view-as are Stoopside's own.

Workflows 1 to 5 are the acceptance baseline, each complete end to end with modification, cancellation, confirmation and realistic errors where relevant. Optional, outside the baseline: A dues and fees, B photo albums, C organizer handover. A write that notifies several members or commits another person (post, event change, RSVP) runs as preview (listing recipients), confirm, receipt, idempotent on `request_id`; other writes are single calls. Every rule below appears on the help page or a preview. Times use the group's time zone and follow the environment clock (`ctx.virtual_now`); a call at a deadline fails.

1. **Groups and membership.** `pending -> member|declined`; `member -> left|removed`. Roles: organizer, co-organizer, event host, member. A display name can differ from the name the user uses for that person. Members see `member_id`, display name, bio and roles; emails appear only in `members.export`, a co-organizer read. Per-group privacy flags: `hide_from_member_list` (search and directory, for non-organizers), `hide_rsvps` (guest lists, for non-hosts). Search matches substrings of display name and bio only. Leaving withdraws a pending request, or sets future RSVPs to `no` and releases slots. Errors: `not_member`, `role_required`, `approval_pending`, `sole_organizer`, `sole_host`.
2. **Events and RSVPs.** Hosts create, edit and cancel events (`published -> cancelled|past`) with capacity, guest limit and RSVP open and close times; every group member sees them. An RSVP carries guest and child counts, so one answer can commit other people. `rsvps.preview` pre-fills the counts from the user's latest RSVP in that group and shows them, their date and the place or waitlist outcome. States: `yes|no|waitlist`; `waitlist -> offered -> yes|expired`; `yes -> needs_reconfirm -> yes|no`. A freed place is offered for 24 hours, or until RSVPs close, to the earliest waitlisted RSVP that fits, then to the next. Editing date, time or venue requires a notify choice (`guests|group`) and flips each `yes` to `needs_reconfirm`, which holds its place for 48 hours, then becomes `no`. Cancelling notifies every open RSVP, locks attached sheets and cannot be undone. Errors: `rsvp_not_open`, `rsvp_closed`, `guest_limit_exceeded`, `claim_expired`, `capacity_below_yes`.
3. **Posts, comments and announcements.** `posts.preview` takes `kind` (`post|comment|announcement`), an audience, and `parent_id` or `edit_of`; `published -> edited|deleted`. The audience is required (`audience_required`): `group`, `event_guests` (RSVP `yes`: at publish for notification, at read time for visibility) or `hosts_only`. Comments inherit it. An edit changes text only and notifies nobody; deleting recalls nothing delivered. An announcement (co-organizers and above) is emailed to every member at once, one per group per calendar day (`announcement_limit_reached`). Announcements, and posts on a past or cancelled event, cannot be edited or deleted (`post_locked`). A digest of the last seven days of surviving `group` posts falls due Mondays at 08:00 and is created at the first write or event after that time.
4. **Sign-up sheets.** A fixture-defined sheet attaches to an event; its labeled slots (potluck, carpool, snack rota) have a quantity and a lock time, and are claimed and released until then. A swap needs the other member's acceptance: `proposed -> accepted|declined|withdrawn|expired` (48 hours or the lock). Event edits leave sheets unchanged. Errors: `slot_taken`, `sheet_locked`, `not_your_slot`, `swap_pending`.
5. **View-as and the visibility record.** `visibility.view_as` returns a group, event, post or sheet as a named member sees it now, not what was already delivered; anything the user cannot see is `not_found`. Stoopside's committed history keeps every delivery (recipient, object, channel, time) and every revision of memberships, RSVPs, audiences and posts. The visibility record is computed from that history by one documented function: for each object and member, the intervals in which it was visible; any non-zero interval counts as shown. The same function answers `view_as` for the current time. Nothing is written when an object is read.

Value for personal-agent tasks: a routine action (a search by the name the user says, year-old pre-filled counts, a `group` post, an edit or delete after delivery) can complete without an error and still be wrong for this person; RSVP state and the visibility record show which happened. This describes what is checkable, not model difficulty. Downstream tasks:

- Share a member's send-off plans with the guests of a planning event she has not joined; the user knows her by a nickname. Graded on what she was sent or shown.
- RSVP to a school-parents party on a weekend since traded to the other parent; graded on the confirmed child count.
- A waitlist offer for two arrives while the user is unreachable and the partner has other plans; the claim window is running.
- Move a venue, notify only the guests, release the user's carpool slot from the old venue and answer a pending swap.

## 3. Potential app connections

- **Internal personal apps (required):** Mail and Calendar, both optional
  - Mail 1.3.0 (optional; workflows 2-4 send to it): the user's own notifications use Mail's `channel.email` receive contract (wire schema `urn:pale:email-message:2`, payload `urn:pale:email-payload:1`, type `org.pale.mail.notification.v1`, receiver operation `mail.receive_notification`; `examples/apps/mail/contribution/contribution.json`), as `text/plain` bodies with no assets. Deliveries to other members stay in Stoopside's history and the visibility record; they are not sent anywhere. Workflows 1-5 complete without Mail.
  - Calendar 1.3.0 (optional; no workflow needs it): Calendar has no messaging receiver, so Stoopside publishes nothing to it. RSVP receipts carry start, end and venue on labelled lines, and the Agent records an event with `calendar.create` if a task asks; scenarios can seed entries with Calendar's own `calendar.upsert` event.
  - No Contacts, Messages or Files: every aliased member has an agent-visible name link (the export's email, or a seeded post or Mail message using both names).
- **Other apps and websites (required):** None

### Integration details

- **Operations and access (required):** Agent-facing MCP tools (25, prefixed `stoopside.`): `groups.list|get|join|leave`, `members.search|export`, `profile.get|update`, `events.list|get|preview_change|confirm_change`, `rsvps.preview|confirm`, `posts.list|preview|publish|delete`, `signups.get|claim|release|swap_propose|swap_answer`, `visibility.view_as`, `help.get`. `events.preview_change` covers create, edit and cancel; `rsvps.confirm` also claims an offered place and reconfirms; `swap_answer` takes `accept|decline|withdraw`. Writes, the three previews included, take `expected_version` and `request_id`. Permissions use Core's per-profile `allow` lists, fixed when state is created, loaded or reset: a `browse` profile leaves out `posts.publish`, `events.confirm_change`, `rsvps.confirm` and `members.export`, a `full` profile allows them. Stoopside keeps no approval state; a user's approval is captured in the conversation or the task harness, which picks the profile. Pages are declared with the optional `pale.web` extension and served by the public business Web Host over the Agent MCP connection; whether a run gives the Agent that GUI is run configuration. Infra operations are Core's: `env.load_seed`, `env.apply_event`, `env.reset`, `env.snapshot`, `env.event_status`, `env.validate_bundle` and `env.advance_time`.
- **State and lifecycle (required):** App state (groups, members, events, sheets and their history, including deliveries to other members), account state (the user's memberships, RSVPs, posts, claims, outgoing notices) and run state (previews, receipts) are separate; the visibility record is derived from history, is in `env.snapshot` and in no agent tool. Seeds pass `validateSeed`. Run-time IDs come from a counter and list order from a documented key. Time follows the environment clock: Stoopside has no scheduler or clock event, and RSVP close, claim and reconfirm expiry, sheet lock, swap expiry and the digest are computed from stored due times and `ctx.virtual_now` at each read and write. Reads compute them without storing anything; the next write or event commits them, so a notice that falls due (an expired claim offered to the next member, the digest) is created at the first event or write after its deadline. A preview expires after 15 simulated minutes (`preview_expired`); confirm fails `preview_stale` if an object the preview read has changed, and a stale `expected_version` is Core's `stale_version`, which changes nothing. Evaluator records come from Core's `state.audit` (successful reads and rejected calls, exported with `env.snapshot` before each reset, since reset clears it), the supplied Agent execution records (tool arguments and results, such as search strings and `view_as` targets) and Stoopside's committed history. One logical attempt is one `request_id`: a transport retry or a successful idempotent replay counts once (a replay adds no audit row), and repeated rejected calls with one `request_id` are collapsed. `env.reset` restores only this App instance (`reset_scope: app_instance`) to the loaded seed; records already delivered to Mail stay until Mail's own reset.
- **Events and notifications (required):** Scenario events arrive through `env.apply_event` as `{event_id, at, operation, payload}`, prefixed `stoopside.`; an event naming an unknown ID or not applicable in the current state is rejected with `invalid_event` and changes nothing. One example each (IDs are fixture IDs):
  - `member.joined` `{event_id: "ev-01", at: "2027-03-02T10:00:00-05:00", payload: {group_id: "g-parents", member_id: "m-041", display_name: "Bea R."}}`: adds a `member`; she sees every `group` post from now on. `member.removed` `{event_id: "ev-02", ..., payload: {group_id: "g-parents", member_id: "m-041"}}`: `member -> removed`, her future RSVPs become `no` and their places are offered on; nothing she can see stays visible.
  - `membership.decided` `{event_id: "ev-03", at: "2027-03-02T12:00:00-05:00", payload: {group_id: "g-runclub", decision: "member"}}`: decides the user's own `pending` request (`member` or `declined`); a notice goes to the user.
  - `rsvp.changed_by_other` `{event_id: "ev-04", at: "2027-03-05T18:20:00-05:00", payload: {event_id: "e-117", member_id: "m-009", answer: "no", guests: 0, children: 0}}`: another member's RSVP; a `yes -> no` frees places and starts the 24-hour offer to the earliest waitlisted RSVP that fits.
  - `event.changed_by_cohost` `{event_id: "ev-05", at: "2027-03-06T09:00:00-05:00", payload: {event_id: "e-117", change: "edit", venue: "Linden Park pavilion", notify: "guests"}}`: same rules as a host edit in workflow 2 (each `yes` becomes `needs_reconfirm`); `change: "cancel"` cancels and locks sheets.
  - `signup.claimed_by_other` `{event_id: "ev-06", at: "2027-03-06T10:00:00-05:00", payload: {sheet_id: "s-22", slot_id: "carpool-2", member_id: "m-013"}}` and `signup.released_by_other` (same payload): take or free one unit of a slot before its lock; a claim on a full slot is rejected.
  - `swap.proposed_by_other` `{event_id: "ev-07", at: "2027-03-06T11:00:00-05:00", payload: {swap_id: "sw-5", sheet_id: "s-22", from_member: "m-013", give_slot: "snacks", want_slot: "carpool-1"}}`: a `proposed` swap the user can answer for 48 hours or until the lock. `swap.answered` `{event_id: "ev-08", ..., payload: {swap_id: "sw-3", answer: "accepted"}}`: the other member's answer to a swap the user proposed; `accepted` exchanges the two claims.
  - `post.created_by_other` `{event_id: "ev-09", at: "2027-03-07T08:30:00-05:00", payload: {post_id: "p-310", group_id: "g-parents", member_id: "m-002", kind: "post", audience: "event_guests", event_ref: "e-117", text}}`: a post with the stated audience; the visibility record follows workflow 5.

  Published: the user's own notices (waitlist offers, event changes and cancellations, announcements, the digest, write receipts) on Mail's `channel.email`, declared in Stoopside's `messaging.publishes` with a pinned copy of Mail 1.3.0's wire schema. Each committed write or event that creates a notice stores it in the user's outgoing notices in the same commit; the run's host-side Messaging caller reads new ones from the result or the next `env.snapshot` and calls `router.publish`, using the notice ID as the CloudEvents `id`. Stoopside does not record delivery. Committed receipt: one message in the user's Mail. Rejected: Mail unchanged and Stoopside keeps its business result and notice (no rollback); that notice's integration check fails while business checks still pass. Retry with the same `id`: Messaging deduplicates, still one Mail message. Unknown acknowledgement: the run stops for reconciliation and is not scored. A waitlist offer:

- **Shared data and dependencies (required):** Stable IDs for people, members and every object. `binding.json` follows #12 (people, accounts, objects); the seed maps each `member_id` to a `person_id` and to an address in the Mail seed.
