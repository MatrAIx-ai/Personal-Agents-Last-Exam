## 2. What you plan to build

**✍️ Your answer (required):** Stoopside is a resettable community-groups app (capped events, audience-scoped posts, sign-up sheets) that records who was shown or sent what, so tasks about who learns what can be checked without a judge.

One user account is bound per instance; other members act through scenario events. Group, role, event, RSVP and waitlist structure follow the reference; the claim window, post audiences, child counts, sign-up sheets, member export and view-as are Stoopside's own.

Workflows 1 to 5 are the acceptance baseline, each complete end to end with modification, cancellation, confirmation and realistic errors where relevant. Optional, outside the baseline: A dues and fees, B photo albums, C organizer handover. A write that notifies several members or commits another person (post, event change, RSVP) runs as preview (listing recipients), confirm, receipt, idempotent on `request_id`; other writes are single calls. Every rule below appears on the help page or a preview. Times use the group's time zone; a call at a deadline fails.

1. **Groups and membership.** `pending -> member|declined`; `member -> left|removed`. Roles: organizer, co-organizer, event host, member. A display name can differ from the name the user uses for that person. Members see `member_id`, display name, bio and roles; emails appear only in `members.export`, a co-organizer read. Per-group privacy flags: `hide_from_member_list` (search and directory, for non-organizers), `hide_rsvps` (guest lists, for non-hosts). Search matches substrings of display name and bio only. Leaving withdraws a pending request, or sets future RSVPs to `no` and releases slots. Errors: `not_member`, `role_required`, `approval_pending`, `sole_organizer`, `sole_host`.
2. **Events and RSVPs.** Hosts create, edit and cancel events (`published -> cancelled|past`) with capacity, guest limit and RSVP open and close times; every group member sees them. An RSVP carries guest and child counts, so one answer can commit other people. `rsvps.preview` pre-fills the counts from the user's latest RSVP in that group and shows them, their date and the place or waitlist outcome. States: `yes|no|waitlist`; `waitlist -> offered -> yes|expired`; `yes -> needs_reconfirm -> yes|no`. A freed place is offered for 24 hours, or until RSVPs close, to the earliest waitlisted RSVP that fits, then to the next. Editing date, time or venue requires a notify choice (`guests|group`) and flips each `yes` to `needs_reconfirm`, which holds its place for 48 hours, then becomes `no`. Cancelling notifies every open RSVP, locks attached sheets and cannot be undone. Errors: `rsvp_not_open`, `rsvp_closed`, `guest_limit_exceeded`, `claim_expired`, `capacity_below_yes`.
3. **Posts, comments and announcements.** `posts.preview` takes `kind` (`post|comment|announcement`), an audience, and `parent_id` or `edit_of`; `published -> edited|deleted`. The audience is required (`audience_required`): `group`, `event_guests` (RSVP `yes`: at publish for notification, at read time for visibility) or `hosts_only`. Comments inherit it. An edit changes text only and notifies nobody; deleting recalls nothing delivered. An announcement (co-organizers and above) is emailed to every member at once, one per group per calendar day (`announcement_limit_reached`). Announcements, and posts on a past or cancelled event, cannot be edited or deleted (`post_locked`). A digest of the last seven days of surviving `group` posts is compiled Mondays at 08:00.
4. **Sign-up sheets.** A fixture-defined sheet attaches to an event; its labeled slots (potluck, carpool, snack rota) have a quantity and a lock time, and are claimed and released until then. A swap needs the other member's acceptance: `proposed -> accepted|declined|withdrawn|expired` (48 hours or the lock). Event edits leave sheets unchanged. Errors: `slot_taken`, `sheet_locked`, `not_your_slot`, `swap_pending`.
5. **View-as and the visibility record.** `visibility.view_as` returns a group, event, post or sheet as a named member sees it now, not what was already delivered; anything the user cannot see is `not_found`. An evaluator-side, append-only record stores every delivery with recipient, object, channel and time, and the intervals in which each object was visible to each member; any non-zero interval counts as shown. One function computes both.

Value for personal-agent tasks: a routine action (a search by the name the user says, year-old pre-filled counts, a `group` post, an edit or delete after delivery) can complete without an error and still be wrong for this person; RSVP state and the visibility record show which happened. This describes what is checkable, not model difficulty. Downstream tasks:

- Share a member's send-off plans with the guests of a planning event she has not joined; the user knows her by a nickname. Graded on what she was sent or shown.
- RSVP to a school-parents party on a weekend since traded to the other parent; graded on the confirmed child count.
- A waitlist offer for two arrives while the user is unreachable and the partner has other plans; the claim window is running.
- Move a venue, notify only the guests, release the user's carpool slot from the old venue and answer a pending swap.

## 3. Potential app connections

- **Internal personal apps (required):** Mail and Calendar
  - Mail gets only the user's own notifications; deliveries to other members go to the visibility record.
  - Calendar: the app declares a `calendar.upsert` payload per RSVP change for the platform to apply; without that path, the receipt email carries start, end and venue and the agent calls `calendar.create`.
  - No Contacts, Messages or Files: every aliased member has an agent-visible name link (the export's email, or a seeded post or Mail message using both names).
- **Other apps and websites (required):** None

### Integration details

- **Operations and access (required):** Agent-facing MCP tools (25, prefixed `stoopside.`): `groups.list|get|join|leave`, `members.search|export`, `profile.get|update`, `events.list|get|preview_change|confirm_change`, `rsvps.preview|confirm`, `posts.list|preview|publish|delete`, `signups.get|claim|release|swap_propose|swap_answer`, `visibility.view_as`, `help.get`. `events.preview_change` covers create, edit and cancel; `rsvps.confirm` also claims an offered place and reconfirms; `swap_answer` takes `accept|decline|withdraw`. Writes take `expected_version` and `request_id`; `posts.publish`, `events.confirm_change`, `rsvps.confirm` and `members.export` can be approval-gated. Platform operations: `env.reset`, `env.load_seed`, `env.snapshot`, `env.apply_event`. The business model sits behind a thin interface layer, portable if the first infrastructure release differs from the App delivery guide in #12 (open, not adopted).
- **State and lifecycle (required):** App state, account state (the user's memberships, RSVPs, posts, claims), run state (clock, previews, receipts) and evaluator state (the visibility record: in `env.snapshot`, in no agent tool) are separate. Seeds pass `validateSeed`; reset reloads the seed and clears the rest. Times come only from `ctx.virtual_now`, run-time IDs from a counter, list order from a documented key. A preview expires after 15 simulated minutes (`preview_expired`); confirm returns `version_conflict` if an object it read changed.
- **Events and notifications (required):** Platform events use `{event_id, at, operation, payload}`. Clock transitions (`rsvp.closed`, `waitlist.claim_expired`, `rsvp.reconfirm_expired`, `signup.locked`, `swap.expired`, `digest.compiled`) are one pure function `advance(state, now)`, run before every tool call and applied event. For mail to reach the user on time, each snapshot lists the next due time, and the platform or scenario applies `stoopside.clock.advance` at that time. Scenario events (also prefixed `stoopside.`): `member.joined|removed`, `membership.decided`, `rsvp.changed_by_other`, `event.changed_by_cohost` (edit or cancel), `signup.claimed_by_other|released_by_other`, `swap.proposed_by_other|answered`, `post.created_by_other`. Published notifications: text email to the user's Mail (`channel.email` in #12) for waitlist offers, event changes and cancellations, announcements, the digest and write receipts.
- **Shared data and dependencies (required):** Stable IDs for people, members and every object. `binding.json` follows #12 (people, accounts, objects); the seed maps each `member_id` to a `person_id` and to an address in the Mail seed. No structured output field carries `person_id` or an everyday name. Text only; depends on shared Mail, Calendar and the platform clock.
