# Environment Track proposal

Proposed PR title: `[Environment Proposal] [Health & care + Money & insurance] Northlake Patient Portal (MyChart-inspired)`

## 1. App and domains

- **Authors / GitHub handles (required):** Sunny Yuan ([@ZichenYuan](https://github.com/ZichenYuan)).
- **Reference app or website / URL (required):** Northlake Patient Portal, an independently branded synthetic patient portal for one fictional health system; interaction patterns reference MyChart (https://www.mychart.org/) and public patient-portal help pages only. No Epic code, assets or data are used.
- **Primary domain (required):** Health & care
- **Secondary domains (optional):** Money & insurance

## 2. What you plan to build

**✍️ Your answer (required):** A resettable patient portal where a user reads and sends secure messages to clinic staff, reviews visit billing (billed amount, insurance adjustment, patient responsibility, payments and balance), downloads itemized statements, finds in-network providers and books, cancels or reschedules appointments, manages prescriptions and the pharmacy on file, and receives clinic notices, test results and care instructions. Staff replies, billing postings, result releases and notices arrive on the platform's simulated clock, so tasks can span days and sessions, and every money- or identity-related action needs an explicit confirmation step.

Initial workflows, each complete end to end including modification, cancellation, confirmation and realistic errors:

1. **Secure messaging.** Inbox of threads grouped by recipient (clinic, nurse line, billing office). Read, reply, start a new thread, attach a document, mark read or unread, export a thread. Staff replies are injected events with realistic delays, partial answers and late details (for example a pharmacy named several messages after "pick it up ASAP"). Staff can close a thread; a closed thread rejects replies with a clear error.
2. **Billing.** Ledger of visits and lab claims showing billed amount, insurance adjustment, patient responsibility, payments and balance, so the same charge appears as two numbers before and after insurance. Statements by period; itemized statement download (text-based PDF); pay a balance as preview, confirm, receipt; ask a question about a charge (opens a billing thread); refund and fee-waiver status; errors for paying more than the balance or paying a claim still in adjudication.
3. **Appointments.** Provider directory with specialty, locations, in-network status per insurance plan, rating and review count, and next available slot. Slots are not held until confirmed and can disappear between selection and confirmation (injected event). Booking includes a patient-identity step (date of birth) reserved for the user, so the agent must hand off and say so. Cancel, reschedule, join a waitlist for an earlier slot; confirmations and reminders are published as notifications.
4. **Prescriptions and pharmacy.** Active prescriptions with status (sent, ready for pickup, blocked because an enrollment credential is missing), pharmacy on file, change pharmacy, refill request with scheduled status changes.
5. **Notices, results and records.** Clinic notices (a provider leaving, a clinic closing on a date), test results released on a schedule, visit summaries, care instructions with multi-step protocols and waiting periods, medication list and insurance card on file.

Value for personal-agent tasks: health is the domain where an agent must read the user's own records before acting, cross-check two parts of the same app (messages versus billing), quote long threads exactly, respect approval boundaries (payments, identity data, sending to third parties) and handle delays and silences across days. The author's reviewed PersonalAgentEval cases supply four downstream tasks on this environment: an evidence-backed billing grievance (submitted alongside this proposal as a linked Task PR), finding and booking an in-network specialist under rating, distance and timing constraints, obtaining an itemized statement for a benefits reimbursement claim, and reconciling a prescription decision with the record.

## 3. Potential app connections

- **Internal personal apps (required):** Mail: receives portal notifications (new message, statement ready, appointment confirmation and reminder, result released) and is where the user drafts grievance or appeal emails with portal exports attached. Calendar: appointments and care-protocol waiting periods are created, moved and cancelled. Files: downloaded statements and thread exports. Contacts: clinic and pharmacy entries, read only, TBD pending a Contacts app.
- **Other apps and websites (required):** A benefits (FSA/HSA) provider app would consume the itemized statement for reimbursement claims; a bank app (#6) could show portal payments as transactions. The insurer's in-network directory is bundled inside the portal's provider search for the initial scope; a standalone insurer app is deferred.

### Integration details

- **Operations and access (required):** Agent-facing operations exposed as MCP tools in the style of the reference packages in #12: `portal.messages.list|get|send|reply|export`, `portal.billing.list|get_claim|statement|pay.preview|pay.confirm|question`, `portal.providers.search|get`, `portal.appointments.slots|preview|confirm|cancel|reschedule|list|waitlist`, `portal.prescriptions.list|refill`, `portal.pharmacy.get|set`, `portal.notices.list`, `portal.results.list|get`, `portal.records.get`, `portal.insurance.get`. Each declares a read or write effect; `pay.confirm`, `appointments.confirm`, `pharmacy.set` and `messages.send` are writes the platform can gate behind user approval. A GUI renderer presents the same records as pages (inbox, thread, billing, claim, statement, provider search, booking, prescriptions, notices) for computer-use agents. Platform-facing operations: `env.reset`, `env.load_seed`, `env.snapshot`, `env.inject_event`.
- **State and lifecycle (required):** App state (health system, clinics, providers, slot calendars, fee schedules) is separated from account state (the patient's threads, claims, appointments, prescriptions, notices) and run state (clock, pending previews, delivered events). Initial data comes from a seed checked by `validateSeed`; reset reloads the seed and clears previews, receipts and scheduled events; snapshots capture account and run state. Every write returns the resulting record and a receipt ID; repeating a confirm with the same receipt ID is idempotent; a preview expires after a configured simulated interval; a slot taken by an injected event between preview and confirm returns a conflict error instead of booking.
- **Events and notifications (required):** Accepted events: `portal.staff_reply`, `portal.thread_closed`, `portal.billing.post` (new claim, adjustment, refund or waiver decision), `portal.notice.publish`, `portal.result.release`, `portal.appointment.update` (clinic cancels or moves a visit), `portal.prescription.update`, `portal.slot.taken`, `portal.page.interstitial` (a privacy or session interstitial blocks one page load until acknowledged). Published notifications: text-email notifications to Mail for new messages, statements, confirmations, reminders and results, and calendar updates for appointments; example payloads will follow the Mail email-payload and Calendar `calendar.upsert` formats in #12. Task scenarios decide when events occur.
- **Shared data and dependencies (required):** Stable IDs for the person (patient), the account (one patient per instance in the initial scope), providers, clinics, claims, threads and appointments; attachments are text PDF statements and thread exports addressed by asset ID in the asset catalog. Depends on the shared Mail and Calendar apps for notifications and on the platform clock for scheduled events; no external service is needed at run time.
