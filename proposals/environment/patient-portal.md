# Environment Track proposal

Proposed PR title: `[Environment Proposal] [Health & care + Money & insurance] Northlake Patient Portal (MyChart-inspired)`

## 1. App and domains

- **Authors / GitHub handles (required):** Sunny Yuan ([@ZichenYuan](https://github.com/ZichenYuan)).
- **Reference app or website / URL (required):** Northlake Patient Portal, an independently branded synthetic patient portal for one fictional health system; interaction patterns reference MyChart (https://www.mychart.org/) and public patient-portal help pages only. No Epic code, assets or data are used.
- **Primary domain (required):** Health & care
- **Secondary domains (optional):** Money & insurance

## 2. What you plan to build

**✍️ Your answer (required):** A resettable patient portal where a user reads and sends secure messages to clinic staff, reviews visit billing (billed amount, insurance adjustment, patient responsibility, payments and balance), downloads itemized statements, finds in-network providers and books, cancels or reschedules appointments, and, as optional workflows, manages prescriptions and the pharmacy on file and receives clinic notices, test results and care instructions. Staff replies, billing postings, result releases and notices arrive as events on the platform's virtual clock, so tasks can span days and sessions, and every money- or identity-related write is a preview followed by a confirm that the task policy can withhold from the agent.

Workflows 1 to 3 form the first version and the acceptance baseline. Workflows 4 and 5 are optional: they are outside the baseline and will be built only if design review asks for them. Each delivered workflow is complete end to end, including modification, cancellation, confirmation and realistic errors:

1. **Secure messaging.** Inbox of threads grouped by recipient (clinic, nurse line, billing office). Read, reply, start a new thread, attach a document to a message, mark a message read or unread, export a thread as text. A thread is `open` (awaiting staff or awaiting the patient) or `closed`; a message is `unread` or `read`. Staff replies are events with realistic delays, partial answers and late details (for example a pharmacy named several messages after "pick it up ASAP"). A closed thread rejects replies with `thread_closed`; an attachment outside the accepted formats or size is rejected with `attachment_invalid`.
2. **Billing.** Ledger of visits and lab claims showing billed amount, insurance adjustment, patient responsibility, payments and balance, so the same charge appears as two numbers before and after insurance. A claim is `in_adjudication`, then `paid` or `denied` with a reason code (for example `no_referral_on_file`, `duplicate_claim`); a denied claim can enter `reprocessing` and end `paid` or `denied` again; the patient balance is derived from these states and from posted payments. Statements by period as text-based PDF, returned as bytes the agent can save or attach; pay a balance as preview, confirm, receipt; ask a question about a charge (opens a billing thread); request a refund or a fee waiver, each `submitted` and then `granted` or `declined`, where a granted waiver zeroes the fee's patient responsibility and a granted refund posts a refund line. Errors: paying more than the balance (`exceeds_balance`), paying a claim still in adjudication (`claim_not_final`), a preview that expired or no longer matches the ledger (`preview_stale`).
3. **Appointments.** Provider directory with specialty, locations, in-network status per insurance plan, rating and review count, and next available slot. Slots are not held: a slot taken by an event between preview and confirm returns `slot_unavailable` with the nearest alternatives. Booking a new visit requires a patient-identity step (date of birth) that the agent cannot perform: `portal.appointments.confirm` returns `identity_required` until the task applies a `user.identity_verified` event for that preview, so the agent must hand off and say so. An appointment is `booked`, then `completed`, `cancelled_by_patient`, `cancelled_by_clinic` or `rescheduled` (linked to its replacement). Cancel, reschedule, join a waitlist for an earlier slot: a waitlist entry is `waiting`, `offered` when an earlier slot opens, then `booked`, `declined` or `expired`, or `left` if the patient leaves it. Confirmations, clinic-side changes, waitlist offers and reminders are published as Mail notices; reminders are events the task schedules, because nothing fires on its own when time advances.
4. **Prescriptions and pharmacy (optional, outside the first version).** Active prescriptions with status (sent to the pharmacy, blocked because an enrollment credential is missing, renewal requested, renewal decided), pharmacy on file, change pharmacy, refill request with scheduled status changes.
5. **Notices, results and records (optional, outside the first version).** Clinic notices (a provider leaving, a clinic closing on a date), test results released on a schedule, visit summaries, care instructions with multi-step protocols and waiting periods, medication list and insurance card on file.

Value for personal-agent tasks: health is the domain where an agent must read the user's own records before acting, cross-check two parts of the same app (messages versus billing), quote long threads exactly, respect approval boundaries (payments, identity data, sending to third parties) and handle delays and silences across days. The author's reviewed PersonalAgentEval cases supply downstream tasks on this environment: an evidence-backed billing grievance (the linked Task PR #17), finding and booking an in-network specialist under rating, distance and timing constraints, and obtaining an itemized statement for a benefits reimbursement claim; a prescription-decision task would follow if the optional prescriptions workflow is built. Task #114, by another author, also plans to use the portal for scheduling, reminders and visit notes.

## 3. Potential app connections

- **Internal personal apps (required):** Mail 1.3.0, optional, used by workflows 1 to 3: the portal publishes text notices (new message, statement ready, refund or waiver decision, appointment confirmation, clinic-side change, waitlist offer, reminder) on `channel.email`, wire `urn:pale:email-message:2`, payload `urn:pale:email-payload:1`, received by Mail's `mail.receive_notification`; the notices carry no files, so the `channel.email.attachments` contract is not used. On the agent's side, the linked Task #17 has the agent create a grievance draft with `mail.draft.create` and attach the statement with `mail.attachment.add`, which takes the bytes, MIME type and SHA-256 that `portal.billing.statement` returns. Files 1.0.0, optional, used by workflows 1 and 2 on the agent's side only: Files has no messaging receiver and the portal has no client for other Apps, so the agent saves a statement or a thread export with `files.put`, passing the bytes, MIME type and SHA-256 the portal returned. Calendar 1.3.0, optional, used by workflow 3 on the agent's side only: Calendar has no messaging receiver, so the agent copies an appointment into Calendar with `calendar.create`, `calendar.update` and `calendar.cancel` and checks other commitments with `calendar.freebusy` or `calendar.list` when the task grants Calendar; the portal is the source of truth for its appointments and publishes nothing to Calendar. Contacts: None; clinic and pharmacy details are portal records read through `portal.providers.get`, and no workflow reads Contacts.
- **Other apps and websites (required):** None in the first version. A benefits (FSA/HSA) App consuming the itemized statement and a bank App showing portal payments are future links and are outside the first version. The insurer's in-network directory is bundled inside the portal's provider search; insurer adjudication and explanations of benefits belong to a health-plan App such as #64 (see Milestones), and the portal only shows the adjudication results the clinic's billing office posts.

### Integration details

- **Operations and access (required):** Agent-facing MCP operations, each declared read or write in `tools.json`. Reads: `portal.account.get` (patient, insurance on file, interstitial state), `portal.messages.list`, `portal.messages.get`, `portal.messages.export` (one thread as UTF-8 text: filename, `text/plain`, base64 bytes, SHA-256), `portal.messages.attachment.get` (a message attachment's bytes), `portal.billing.list`, `portal.billing.get_claim` (with claim history), `portal.billing.statement` (a statement as text-based PDF: filename, `application/pdf`, base64 bytes, SHA-256, deterministic for the same ledger state), `portal.billing.requests.list`, `portal.providers.search`, `portal.providers.get`, `portal.appointments.slots`, `portal.appointments.list`, `portal.appointments.get`, `portal.waitlist.list`. Writes: `portal.messages.send` and `portal.messages.reply` (both accept an optional `attachments` array of files with filename, MIME type, base64 bytes and SHA-256), `portal.messages.flags` (read or unread), `portal.billing.pay.preview` (stores a preview with amount, claims covered and an expiry), `portal.billing.pay.confirm`, `portal.billing.question`, `portal.billing.requests.create` (refund or fee waiver), `portal.appointments.preview` (stores a preview with slot, provider, network status and an expiry), `portal.appointments.confirm`, `portal.appointments.cancel`, `portal.appointments.reschedule`, `portal.waitlist.join`, `portal.waitlist.leave`, `portal.waitlist.accept`, `portal.waitlist.decline`, `portal.interstitial.acknowledge`. That is 31 core operations; the optional workflows would add `portal.prescriptions.list|refill`, `portal.pharmacy.get|set`, `portal.notices.list`, `portal.results.list|get` and `portal.records.get`. Gated writes (`portal.billing.pay.confirm`, `portal.appointments.confirm`, `portal.messages.send`, `portal.messages.reply`, `portal.billing.requests.create`, `portal.waitlist.accept`, and `portal.pharmacy.set` if built) require a current preview or offer ID where one exists; whether the agent may call each is decided by the task policy's allow-list and per-tool `ids` and `fields` constraints, which Core enforces. A fixture flag `require_user_confirmation` (default off) additionally makes a gated write return `approval_required` until the task applies a `user.confirmed` event through Infra carrying the preview or offer ID, so the package runs with no user harness; a platform-level approval step, if one is added, belongs to Infra and this flag is the fallback. The identity step uses the same pattern with its own event: `portal.appointments.confirm` returns `identity_required` until a `user.identity_verified` event names the preview. Agent-facing pages (inbox, thread, billing, claim, statement, providers, booking, appointments, account) are App-owned `gui/index.html` and `gui/src/app.js`, declared through the optional `pale.web` extension in `contribution/gui.json` and served by the public business Web Host over the Agent MCP connection; GUI actions call the same business tools under the same policy, and the model-side computer-use adapter is a platform component. Platform-facing operations, never listed on Agent connections: `env.load_seed`, `env.apply_event`, `env.reset`, `env.snapshot`, and the host-provided `env.advance_time`.
- **State and lifecycle (required):** App state (health system, clinics, providers, slot calendars, fee schedules, network status per plan) is separated from account state (the patient's threads and messages with flags and attachments, claims with history, payments, refund and waiver requests, statements, appointments, waitlist entries, insurance on file, interstitial state) and run state (pending previews and offers, receipts, event log). The clock is Core's `ctx.virtual_now`; preview expiry, offer expiry and "completed" appointments are evaluated when an operation runs, and the App keeps no clock of its own. `validateSeed` rejects a claim whose billed amount does not equal insurance adjustment plus insurance payment plus patient responsibility, a payment larger than its claim's patient responsibility, a message in an unknown thread or dated before the thread opened, an appointment on a provider without a slot calendar, and duplicate IDs; a claim with no statement period appears on no statement and is flagged `unstatemented`, a provider without reviews shows "no ratings yet", and a thread without a recipient category is listed under the clinic. Every write carries Core's `request_id` and the caller's `expected_version`: a repeated `request_id` returns the stored receipt with no second effect, and a stale `expected_version` is rejected before the handler runs, so the agent re-reads and retries. In addition, a confirm whose preview has expired or whose claims or slot changed since the preview returns `preview_stale` with the current figures, and a slot taken since the preview returns `slot_unavailable`. Reads cannot change business state, and a refused write has no business effect. Reset restores this App instance's seed and clears previews, offers, receipts and the event log; business data is byte-identical after a reset round-trip, while Core's epoch and audit are Core's own; notices already delivered to Mail and files the agent saved in Files stay where they are.
- **Events and notifications (required):** Accepted events, applied by Infra with `env.apply_event`, with the handler reading the event's `at` as the current time; effects and one example each are in the table below. Published notifications: send-only on `channel.email` to Mail 1.3.0 as described in the example notice below; `messaging.receives` is empty. The portal commits its own change first and constructs the notice afterwards; Messaging delivers it as a separate commit. A rejected delivery leaves no Mail record and leaves the portal's committed change unchanged, recorded as `notice_status: rejected` with the receipt in the record's notice history; an unknown acknowledgement halts the run for reconciliation as the runtime specifies; the portal does not retry on its own because the runtime has no outbox, and nothing is rolled back. Reminders are not timers: the task schedules a `portal.appointment.reminder` event, because `env.advance_time` runs no handler. Task scenarios decide when events occur.
- **Shared data and dependencies (required):** Stable IDs for the person (the run binding's `person_id`; one patient per instance), account, clinic, provider, slot, appointment, waitlist entry, thread, message, attachment, claim, payment, request, statement, preview, offer and receipt. Clinic and pharmacy details are portal-owned records. Statements (text-based PDF, `application/pdf`) and thread exports (UTF-8 `text/plain`) are generated at read time from the ledger and the thread, each under 200 KB, and are returned as filename, MIME type, base64 bytes and SHA-256 in the shape `files.put` and `mail.attachment.add` accept; they are not fixture assets. Documents the user attaches to a message are PDF, PNG, JPEG or plain text, at most 2 per message and 512 KiB each, held in account state. The fixture asset bundle holds only script-drawn UI icons: PNG, at most 12 assets, under 1 MiB decoded. The package runs on its own with only the shared runtime (Core and Messaging) and its own fixtures: `app.json.dependencies` is empty, and Mail 1.3.0, Files 1.0.0 and Calendar 1.3.0 are optional run-assembly connections, not package dependencies. The linked Task #17 expects portal notices in Mail and the statement attached to a Mail draft, so its run assembly enables Mail and Files and grants `channel.email` from the portal to the user's Mail account. No external service at run time.
