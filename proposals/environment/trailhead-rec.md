## 2. What you plan to build

**✍️ Your answer (required):** Trailhead Parks & Rec is a resettable, independently branded municipal recreation and community-facility simulator where an enrollment or booking can succeed cleanly and still violate a household's real rules. Everyday municipal recreation portals govern seasonal swim lessons, youth sports leagues, adult fitness clinics, lap-swim punch passes and tennis/pickleball court reservations with residency-tier pricing (`resident` vs `non_resident`), strict age and skill-prerequisite checks, medical/allergy waiver consent flags, time-limited waitlist offers (e.g., 24-hour virtual-time acceptance windows), prorated withdrawal-refund cutoffs, and weather or air-quality facility closures. A personal agent must verify who in the household is eligible, respect withdrawn medical or spending consent, avoid Calendar conflicts, and act before waitlist offers or refund windows expire.

Core reusable workflows (entities, state transitions, reads/writes, and failure/control paths):

1. **Search programs, leagues and facility courts (`program`: `open | waitlist_only | closed | cancelled_by_facility`; `court_slot`: `available | reserved | closed_weather`):**
   - *Reads:* search seasonal programs and athletic court slots by activity, facility, age range, skill prerequisite, resident/non-resident fee, schedule and seat availability; inspect refund-cutoff schedule and waiver requirements.
   - *Failure/control:* unknown `program_id` or `slot_id` returns `NOT_FOUND`; invalid age/date filters return `VALIDATION_ERROR`.
2. **Manage household roster, eligibility and pass balances (`member_profile`, `pass`: `active -> depleted | expired`):**
   - *Reads/writes:* inspect household member profiles (linked via shared `person_ref`, birthdate/age, verified skill prerequisites such as `swim_level_2`, residency tier, emergency-contact/allergy-waiver status `signed | pending | withdrawn`), update non-restricted emergency notes, or view punch-pass/seasonal-pass balances.
   - *Failure/control:* attempting to silently flip a `withdrawn` medical waiver without explicit guardian confirmation flags fails with `WAIVER_CONSENT_REQUIRED`; stale `expected_version` fails with `VERSION_CONFLICT`.
3. **Enroll, accept/decline waitlist offers, transfer and withdraw (`enrollment`: `waitlisted -> offer_pending -> enrolled -> transferred | withdrawn_refunded | withdrawn_credit | offer_expired`):**
   - *Reads/writes:* preview exact fee (applying residency tier and pass/credit balance), enroll directly or join a waitlist, accept or decline an `offer_pending` waitlist spot before its virtual-time `offer_expires_at`, transfer to another section of the same program, or withdraw with deterministic refund/credit computation (`full_refund` before cutoff A, `household_credit` between cutoffs A and B, `no_refund` after cutoff B).
   - *Confirm/modify/cancel & error paths:* enrolling an ineligible age or missing prerequisite fails with `ELIGIBILITY_FAILED`; enrolling with `waiver_status: withdrawn` fails with `WAIVER_CONSENT_REQUIRED`; accepting a waitlist offer after `offer_expires_at` under virtual time fails with `OFFER_EXPIRED`; duplicate submission with the same `request_id` replays the committed receipt without double-charging.
4. **Reserve, modify and cancel athletic courts and handle closures (`court_booking`: `reserved -> modified -> cancelled_refunded | cancelled_forfeited | facility_closed_credited`):**
   - *Reads/writes:* book a court slot (using fee or punch pass), reschedule to another open slot, or cancel before the virtual-time free-cancellation cutoff.
   - *Failure/control:* booking an already-taken or weather-closed slot fails with `SLOT_UNAVAILABLE`; cancelling after the cutoff forfeits the pass punch (`cancelled_forfeited`) rather than silently refunding.

Reuse across distinct task situations: (a) a waitlist spot opens for a child's Saturday swim class with a 24-hour virtual-time offer window, and the agent must check Calendar for conflicts, verify `swim_level_2` prerequisite and allergy waiver status, and either accept or decline before expiry; (b) an adult runner/swimmer wants to withdraw from an evening clinic after an injury note in Messages/Health and transfer remaining household credit to a morning lap-swim pass before the prorated refund cutoff; (c) an outdoor pickleball reservation is cancelled by a `facility.weather_closure` event, issuing an automatic credit that the agent reuses to book an indoor court for the same household group. Every task seeds its own facilities, programs, household members, waivers and event timeline.

### Integration details

- **Operations and access (required):**
  - **Agent-facing business operations** (exposed via both `agent-mcp` stdio tools and the App-owned Web GUI reading/writing the same authoritative state):
    - `program.search` / `program.get` (`MCP` + `GUI`, `read`): search programs/sections and inspect prerequisites, residency fees, waiver requirements and refund cutoffs.
    - `household.get` (`MCP` + `GUI`, `read`) and `household.update_emergency_note` (`MCP` + `GUI`, `write`): read household member eligibility, passes, credit balance and waiver flags; update non-restricted emergency contact notes.
    - `enrollment.list` / `enrollment.preview` (`MCP` + `GUI`, `read`) and `enrollment.create` / `enrollment.respond_offer` / `enrollment.transfer` / `enrollment.withdraw` (`MCP` + `GUI`, `write`): full enrollment, waitlist-offer and withdrawal lifecycle.
    - `court.search_slots` / `court.booking.list` (`MCP` + `GUI`, `read`) and `court.booking.create` / `court.booking.modify` / `court.booking.cancel` (`MCP` + `GUI`, `write`): athletic court reservation lifecycle.
  - **Infra-only privileged operations** (strictly on `infra-mcp`, unavailable to the Agent and absent from the Agent Web GUI): `env.load_seed`, `env.apply_event`, `env.reset`, `env.snapshot`, `env.event_status`, `env.validate_bundle`, `env.advance_time`.
  - **Reviewer console:** None inside the Agent GUI; inspection uses Core's administrative snapshot/audit interface.
- **State and lifecycle (required):**
  - **State separation:** App-level municipal catalog (`facilities[]`, `programs[]`, `court_slots[]`, fee/refund policy tables), account-level household state (`account_id`, `residency_tier`, `household_credit_cents`, `members[]`, `passes[]`, `enrollments[]`, `court_bookings[]`), and run-level Core isolation (`run_id`, `app_id`, virtual clock, receipts and audit log). Each task loads its own initial programs, household members, waivers and enrollment history via `env.load_seed` against `schemas/seed.schema.json`.
  - **Commit evidence, retries and concurrency:** Every write requires `request_id` and `expected_version`, returns `{committed: true, receipt_id, entity_id, version, state_epoch}` on commit, replays the identical receipt on `request_id` retry (preventing duplicate enrollment charges or double-deducted pass punches), and rejects stale `expected_version` with `VERSION_CONFLICT`. Reads never mutate business state; rejected writes preserve business state and record a denial row in Core `state.audit`. Evaluator correlation joins App retained enrollment/booking/credit revision history with Core `state.audit` and supplied Agent execution records; snapshots are retained prior to reset.
  - **Reset and restore:** `env.reset` enforces `reset_scope: app_instance`, returning only this Trailhead instance to its loaded baseline seed without touching Mail or other Apps. Snapshots are inspection evidence, not cross-App rollback.
- **Events and notifications (required):**
  - **Accepted Infra events** (task scenarios choose timing and payload; Trailhead defines deterministic effects):
    - `waitlist.spot_opened`: transitions a `waitlisted` enrollment to `offer_pending` and sets `offer_expires_at`. Example: `{"event_id": "evt-rec-001", "at": "2026-11-18T15:00:00+00:00", "operation": "waitlist.spot_opened", "payload": {"enrollment_id": "enr-205", "offer_expires_at": "2026-11-19T15:00:00+00:00"}}`.
    - `facility.weather_closure`: marks a facility's outdoor court slots or program sessions closed for a virtual-time window and credits affected reservations (`facility_closed_credited`). Example: `{"event_id": "evt-rec-002", "at": "2026-11-19T06:30:00+00:00", "operation": "facility.weather_closure", "payload": {"facility_id": "fac-meadow-courts", "closed_from": "2026-11-19T08:00:00+00:00", "closed_until": "2026-11-19T20:00:00+00:00", "reason": "Air quality advisory (AQI > 160)"}}`.
    - `member.waiver_status_changed`: updates a household member's medical/allergy waiver status (`signed | pending | withdrawn`). Example: `{"event_id": "evt-rec-003", "at": "2026-11-19T11:00:00+00:00", "operation": "member.waiver_status_changed", "payload": {"person_ref": "person-leo", "waiver_type": "aquatics_allergy", "status": "withdrawn"}}`.
    - `program.schedule_changed`: shifts a program's meeting time or facility, opening a penalty-free withdrawal window. Example: `{"event_id": "evt-rec-004", "at": "2026-11-20T09:00:00+00:00", "operation": "program.schedule_changed", "payload": {"program_id": "prg-swim-l3-sat", "new_time_slot": "Sun 09:00-09:45", "penalty_free_until": "2026-11-23T23:59:59+00:00"}}`.
  - **Virtual-time business rules:** Waitlist offer expiration (`offer_expires_at`), withdrawal refund cutoffs, court free-cancellation deadlines and pass expiration dates all evaluate strictly against Core's supplied virtual time (`env.advance_time` / event `at`), never host wall-clock time.
  - **Messaging directions:** `publishes`: `channel.email` (send-only, CloudEvents 1.0 wire envelope matching `urn:pale:email-message:2` / `urn:pale:email-payload:1` / `org.pale.mail.notification.v1` -> Mail `mail.receive_notification`). `receives`: `None`. Wire-message payload example (matches `examples/apps/mail/schemas/email-payload.schema.json`, `asset_ids: []`):
    `{"channel": "channel.email", "recipient": {"kind": "person_ref", "id": "person-elena"}, "source_object": {"app_id": "trailhead-rec", "account_id": "acct-elena", "object_type": "enrollment", "object_id": "enr-205"}, "content": {"title": "Trailhead Waitlist Offer: Youth Swim Level 3", "body": {"format": "text/plain", "text": "A spot opened for enr-205. Respond before 2026-11-19T15:00:00+00:00."}, "asset_ids": []}, "extensions": []}`.
    Sender commit and Mail receiver commit are separate; delivery rejection leaves Mail state unchanged and records the failed delivery receipt without rolling back the local enrollment/booking.
- **Shared data and dependencies (required):**
  - Household members and accounts are referenced by shared IDs (`person_ref`, `account_id`) via `fixtures/binding.json`; Trailhead does not duplicate Contacts or Mail accounts.
  - Assets and media fit PALE's reference profile: synthetic PNG facility-map cards and UTF-8 JSON/text program policies, at most 10 bundled assets and <= 200 KiB total decoded bytes (strictly within the 64-asset / 1 MiB reference-profile ceiling). Missing optional facility-map assets degrade cleanly to structured text metadata.
  - Standalone package boundary: `apps/trailhead-rec/` runs independently using only Node 18+, pinned Core API `1.2.0` / public runtime `1.6.0`, and its own package files. `examples/apps/mail` (`v1.3.0`) is an optional integration target supplied via `app:verify --with examples/apps/mail` during integration checks.
