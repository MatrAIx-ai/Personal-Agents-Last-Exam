### Domain names

The App manages a household's storage service. Rent, payment methods and AutoPay are the operator's customer-account functions; no separate banking, insurance or legal service is implemented.

## 2. What you plan to build

**✍️ Your answer (required):** A resettable storage customer portal with Public Storage-like entry points: **Find Storage → Reserve → Complete eRental → My Account / Storage Units / Payments → Move Out**. A personal agent can select a suitable unit, complete online rental, manage routine account/access settings, pay rent, and finish move-out. The five workflows connect shopping for storage to continuing customer-account service.

| Workflow | First-version actions and visible outcome | Changes, cancellation and realistic errors |
| --- | --- | --- |
| W1 — Find storage | Search three fictional facilities by local city/postal zone; compare size, indoor/drive-up, floor/lift, climate-control label, opening hours, available unit types and online rate. Open an itemized move-in estimate. | Show first-period versus regular rent, setup/lock charges, selected offer and expiry. No assumption that a smaller unit is cheaper or that a climate-control label guarantees a temperature. No map or packing solver required. |
| W2 — Reserve and complete eRental | Make a free reservation for facility/unit type and move-in date; view, amend or cancel it. Complete eRental by reviewing the assigned available unit, customer/contact facts, synthetic agreement, coverage-on-file status, payment method and total; preview/confirm or abandon. | A reservation preserves its disclosed quote terms until expiry but does not guarantee an individual unit. Availability is rechecked at checkout. A failed payment or missing required field leaves the reservation open and creates no rental/charge. Successful checkout creates one rental agreement, paid move-in receipt and exact unit assignment. |
| W3 — My Account and storage access | Read storage-unit details, agreement text, facility hours and move-in instructions; update contact details; add/remove authorized people. Inspect access status and issue a simulated app gate-open request for the tenant's unit/facility. | Authorized-person records do not grant payment or rental-management authority. Outside-hours, not-yet-started, closed or explicitly restricted access is denied visibly. A gate-open command acknowledges a simulated command, not a person's physical entry or an emptied unit. |
| W4 — Payments and AutoPay | Inspect current balance, itemized charges/credits and payment history. Pay the current unreserved balance with a seeded tokenized method; turn AutoPay on/off or change its selected method. Read pending, posted or failed outcomes. | A submitted payment is not yet posted; pending amounts are shown separately and reserved against duplicate payment. Changing AutoPay does not refund charges or cancel an already submitted attempt. A failed attempt leaves the balance due and permits an explicit retry. |
| W5 — Move out | Read the next rent date and move-out checklist; notify a planned date with at least seven days' notice; change/cancel that notice; report the unit emptied/cleaned and lock removed; follow the operator's completion record. | Notice or the tenant's report is not final closure. An operator finding belongings/lock still present keeps the rental open with a reason. Completion ends future rental/access/AutoPay scheduling, while previously due charges or submitted payments remain in history. No automatic prorated refund after a rent boundary. |

**Business rules.**

- Facilities publish approximate dimensions, access hours, online quote terms and all included/excluded charges. No real addresses or current Public Storage rates are imported. A reservation selects a facility and unit type, lasts through its declared move-in cutoff and is free. Amendments atomically replace the reservation's terms; if no alternative quote is available, the original remains. A quoted rate is not an exclusive hold on an individual physical unit. Only successful eRental assigns a free exact unit; competing checkout has one winner.
- Rental review shows move-in date, exact unit, first rent, regular rent, setup/lock charges, any introductory offer, next bill and any first-month credit. There is no default enrollment in AutoPay. Required coverage is represented by a seeded coverage-on-file fact; buying insurance and handling claims are excluded. The original synthetic agreement and accepted quote revision are retained.
- Initial eRental checkout uses a synchronous local simulator decision based on the chosen seeded valid/declined method. Success atomically records a rental, agreement, move-in charge and posted payment; a decline changes none of those records. A future-dated rental is scheduled until the published facility-local move-in opening time; there is no artificial restriction to dates 1–28. Cancellation before that time follows the quote's disclosed full-credit rule, preserves its original receipt and releases the unit. Once active, W5 applies.
- This synthetic operator bills on the first of each month in `America/Los_Angeles`. At checkout, the first rent is paid in full. For a start on local day `d` in a month with `D` days, month two receives a credit of `floor(first_rent_cents * (d - 1) / D)`. Month two's regular charge and that credit are separate rows. A first-day start has no such credit. The quote shows both initial and next-bill totals; this is a documented synthetic formula inspired by the reference's second-month-credit explanation, not a claim to reproduce its accounting exactly. No taxes or insurance charges are modeled.
- Regular rates remain fixed under the accepted terms in v1; first-period offers end at their stated boundary. No rent increase, retention negotiation or mid-rental unit transfer is implemented. Each rental/month has exactly one rent charge; statements reference those records and do not charge again. Closure before the next boundary prevents that period; closure at or after it does not refund the full-period charge. A previous month-two credit is posted only if the rental remains open for that bill and never creates an undisclosed cash refund.
- Subsequent payments are asynchronous simulated attempts. A manual payment allocates a positive amount to stated unpaid ledger rows; pending allocations cannot be paid again. On a rent boundary, enabled AutoPay submits one attempt per rental/bill for its unreserved due amount, using the method selected at that time. Disabling it later leaves submitted attempts unchanged; a failure releases the allocation but never schedules blind retries. Turning it on after a missed boundary affects future bills and does not silently collect old debt. Only an explicit payment-result event posts or fails an attempt. No real banking is involved.
- Authorized people are an add/remove list on the storage unit, following the reference's account workflow. Their presence does not grant a management login. The simulator does not model possession/recovery of keys or secure revocation of previously shared access information. Tenant app gate requests use the trusted actor/account and current rental/hours/restriction state, never caller-supplied identity. Command acknowledgment is not entry evidence. Public opening hours use each facility's IANA zone and DST-aware conversion.
- Move-out notice selects a local date at least seven local calendar days ahead; the UI shows the next rent boundary. The tenant can edit/cancel until reporting completion. The report stores the checklist assertions, not an authoritative vacancy. An operator event records completion or a remaining-belongings/lock reason. A rejection returns the notice to an actionable state. Successful completion closes at the event time, releases the unit and stops future access and recurring payment scheduling; existing debts and already submitted attempts remain. V1 requires promptly scheduled completion events and does not promise retroactive correction of operator delay.
- Core's `ctx.virtual_now` is the only current-time source. Reads purely project start/expiry, due charges/credits, pending AutoPay attempts and current access. The next successful write/event materializes due business boundaries chronologically and applies the requested action atomically. For a coincident bill/closure, post rent/credit and create the due AutoPay attempt before applying closure. Use stable rental/month/attempt keys, preserve effective versus recorded times and test large-jump equivalence. Infra owns the clock and event order; no App scheduler.

**Personal-agent value.** A household moving home may need a ground-floor unit with access before work, a low first-month quote whose later price is affordable, and an eRental actually completed before arrival. Later requests include changing an expired AutoPay method, checking whether rent posted, or ending storage before another full month. These are reusable customer workflows. The Task supplies the user's move dates, budget, accessibility needs and permission to act; the App does not choose a unit or encode a task solution.

### Integration details

- **Operations and access (required):** Proposed `apps/unitharbor/` package. Lowercase tools cover facility/unit search/detail; reservation and rental reads; account/unit/authorized-people reads; statement/payment history; versioned preview/confirm/abandon for reservation, eRental, contact/people, payment, AutoPay and move-out changes; and simulated tenant gate-open requests. Every write requires `request_id` and `expected_version`. Trusted seed identity/policy defines tenant and read-only access; names in Authorized People do not gain financial authority. Supply `pale.web` version 1 page/controller declarations and an App registry; the shared Web Host and stylesheet serve five product areas over the same Agent MCP state. No browser business store or custom bridge. Infra's separate connection owns seed/history/reset/snapshot/events/time; those operations are neither visible nor callable by the Agent.
- **State and lifecycle (required):** Separate public facilities/types/offers from account-bound reservations, exact assigned rentals, original agreements, authorized people, contact revisions, bills/credits/payments, AutoPay settings and move-out reports. Top-level `account_id` and stable person/rental IDs support account/run isolation. Core provides atomic writes, stale-version rejection and request receipts. An accepted request replays its original receipt; changed input conflicts. Reusing a consumed eRental quote cannot create another rental under a new request ID. Failed operations leave business records unchanged. Reset restores loaded records, public revisions and virtual time, clearing run receipts/audit. Export evidence first; snapshots do not imply arbitrary restoration.
- **Events and notifications (required):** Declare four operations consistently in both App and contribution manifests; a whole-event schema covers every variant. Infra supplies order/timing. Notices identify source record, effective time and recorded time. No cross-App messaging in v1.

  ```json
  {"event_id":"payment-001","at":"2026-11-28T18:00:00Z","operation":"unitharbor.payment_result","payload":{"payment_attempt_id":"payment-01","outcome":"posted","provider_reference":"sim-pay-001"}}
  ```

  Post an existing submitted attempt against its allocated charges once. A failed outcome releases its pending allocation and records a reason without reducing the due balance. Repeated provider references cannot post twice; conflicting final outcomes fail with a business code.

  ```json
  {"event_id":"moveout-001","at":"2026-11-30T23:00:00-08:00","operation":"unitharbor.moveout_result","payload":{"rental_id":"rental-01","completion_report_id":"report-01","outcome":"not_complete","reason":"Tenant lock is still attached"}}
  ```

  For an active rental with a tenant completion report, retain the rental and show why action is needed. A later report and `outcome: "completed"` close the rental, release its unit and stop future scheduling; it does not erase debt or previously submitted attempts. The Agent cannot supply its own operator outcome.

  ```json
  {"event_id":"inventory-001","at":"2026-11-28T09:00:00Z","operation":"unitharbor.inventory_update","payload":{"facility_id":"facility-02","unit_type_id":"type-5x10-indoor","expected_revision":1,"new_revision":2,"available_unit_ids":[]}}
  ```

  Update availability for a facility/type and record the revision. A reservation stays readable with its quoted rate, but eRental cannot assign an unavailable unit and must offer a fresh choice. Updates cannot release or take a unit already assigned to this account's scheduled/active rental or reveal another customer's identity.

  ```json
  {"event_id":"access-001","at":"2026-11-28T08:00:00-08:00","operation":"unitharbor.access_update","payload":{"facility_id":"facility-02","expected_revision":1,"new_revision":2,"restriction":{"starts_at":"2026-11-28T08:00:00-08:00","ends_at":"2026-11-28T12:00:00-08:00","reason":"Gate maintenance"}}}
  ```

  Publish a temporary access restriction and affected-account notice without changing rent, authorized people or vacancy. Deny tenant gate commands during the interval; a successful command afterward still does not prove entry. Unknown IDs, invalid intervals and revision conflicts fail atomically.
