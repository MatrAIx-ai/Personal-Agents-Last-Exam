## 2. What you plan to build

**✍️ Your answer (required):** A small subscription-management app with one fictional service, one individual plan, and one synthetic account per scenario. Three views cover four workflows: (1) read current plan, renewal status, fixed informational renewal amount, period end, and retained content; (2) preview cancellation at period end, then confirm or abandon it; (3) inspect the resulting status and effective date; (4) preview and confirm or abandon revocation of a cancellation that has not yet taken effect. Cancelled access becomes free at period end; the account and its three synthetic saved-content entries are retained. This is service-side entitlement management, not a banking, budgeting, purchasing, refund, or content-discovery environment.

The only dynamic world event is one scheduled period boundary. If cancellation is pending, access changes from paid to free. Otherwise the active period advances to the next predeclared date, without charging money or creating a ledger. Both outcomes invalidate older action previews. The visible simulated clock, current period, and status are authoritative: a confirmation at or after the boundary applies the event first, rejects a stale preview with no action effect, and requires a fresh preview. A revocation after cancellation has taken effect cannot restore paid access; resubscription is out of scope. Duplicate confirmations return the original receipt, not a second mutation. Unknown account IDs, read-only actor attempts, already-free accounts, stale versions, and invalid revocations produce explicit errors or documented idempotent outcomes.

This supports multi-session tasks where a person wants to stop renewal but retain access until a particular date, or later changes their mind before cancellation takes effect. A generic agent can confuse a preview with completion, report access as ended too early, silently reactivate renewal, or act on a stale date. Task authors supply the person's instructions through the official user channel; the app does not invent permission or secretly choose the preferred outcome. All policy facts needed to act are visible. Existing proposals #4, #5, #7 cover research records, repair appointments, and item-return requests; #6 covers bank/brokerage operations and #11 covers game commerce. None is a dependency. This proposal focuses on persistent subscription entitlement and deferred cancellation, with no shared financial state.

## 3. Potential app connections

- **Internal personal apps (required):** None in the MVP. User intent is supplied by the scenario; clock and status are local. Mail notices and Calendar reminders are deferred rather than simulated as extra integrations.
- **Other apps and websites (required):** None in the MVP. No live Spotify account, payment processor, bank, app store, streaming endpoint, or third-party support agent. Every observation and action uses the same local subscription state.

### Integration details

- **Operations and access (required):** Proposed access is the three browser views and a same-state action/observation surface for plan lookup, cancellation/revocation preview, confirm/abandon, and status/receipt inspection. Platform-only setup chooses the existing synthetic snapshot, resets it, and advances the clock through the period boundary. Read-only actors cannot perform writes. Exact MCP/API methods and official setup/reset/event hooks are TBD after release and design agreement; no payment, playback, or resubscription API is added.
- **State and lifecycle (required):** Separate the fixed service/plan definition from each run's synthetic account/role, entitlement, saved entries, period dates, previews, receipts, clock, and event cursor. Reset restores that full local state; any platform snapshot must preserve the same boundary. Confirmation returns a receipt and observable state, whereas preview/abandon does not change entitlement. Duplicate confirmations return the existing receipt. Concurrent changes or the period boundary invalidate older previews; stale confirmation fails without an action mutation, and saved content remains unchanged. Exact state-version and snapshot formats remain TBD.
- **Events and notifications (required):** Task scenarios schedule the existing single period-boundary event. Illustrative input facts are account_id=account-1 and simulated_time=period_end; output is free entitlement if cancellation was pending, otherwise an active plan with the next predeclared period, plus invalidation of older previews. Event processing precedes a confirmation at that time, as already specified. There is no charge or ledger entry. Status/receipt changes appear only through local UI/action observations; no Mail, Calendar, or external notification service. Official event schema and hooks are TBD.
- **Shared data and dependencies (required):** Synthetic account, plan, saved-entry, preview, and receipt IDs connect the local entitlement state. Dates, status, and the nominal informational renewal amount are explicit fixture values; exact serialization will be settled in design review. The three saved entries are text-only; no audio, images, attachments, real profiles, or payment credentials are used. UI and the proposed adapter share one local state store. No live streaming/billing service or other environment proposal is required.
