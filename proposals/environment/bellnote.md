# Environment Track proposal

Proposed PR title: `[Environment Proposal] [Learning & research + Health & care] Bellnote school–family portal: absences, permission forms, pickup authorization and lunch accounts`

## 1. App and domains

- **Authors / GitHub handles (required):** Junnan Li (@lijrjyan)
- **Reference app or website / URL (required):** Bellnote, an independently branded synthetic school–family portal of a fictional school district, seen from one parent or guardian's account (attendance and absence reports, permission forms with fees, the people allowed to collect each child and lunch accounts). Workflow references: ParentSquare (https://www.parentsquare.com/) for forms and school notices, and the PowerSchool parent portal (https://www.powerschool.com/) for attendance, guardian records and meal balances. They inform only the shape of the workflows; no data, text, branding or icons are reused, and the district, schools, staff, families and policies are invented.
- **Primary domain (required):** Learning & research
- **Secondary domains (optional):** Health & care. Two of the four workflows carry a child's health record: the allergy and dietary flags on the lunch account and menu, and the medication and medical-consent forms a guardian signs.

## 2. What you plan to build

**✍️ Your answer (required):** Bellnote is a resettable school–family portal, seen from one guardian's account, in which the school accepts whatever a permitted guardian submits before its cut-off, so an action the portal accepts can still be wrong for this family: the wrong adult at the gate, a signature the other parent was meant to give, a trip lunch for a child with a nut allergy, a top-up past what the household agreed. The account holds two or more children who may attend different schools, and for each child the guardian's rights (collect, sign, pay), the standing list of people allowed to collect the child, any restriction the school office holds on file, the lunch account and the forms addressed to that child. School staff, the cafeteria and the other guardian act through task-supplied events, and every time-based state follows the environment clock. Four workflows are complete in the first version.

**Entities.** District (time zone and a policy of six values), school (bell times, the absence and dismissal-change cut-offs, a calendar of closed and early-dismissal days), child (school, grade, homeroom, meal restrictions), guardian link (one per guardian and child: `can_pickup`, `can_sign`, `can_pay`, `custodial`), pickup person (a non-guardian adult on a child's standing list), pickup restriction (a person the office must not release the child to; read-only), dismissal change (one child, one day), attendance record (one child, one day), absence report, form and form response (one per form and child), lunch account and lunch transaction, menu day with allergen tags, payment method and payment, notification, account preferences.

**Simplifications.** One App instance holds one guardian account in one district; the other guardian is not an account, and what they do arrives as events. Staff are not accounts either. There are no grades, assignments, report cards, timetables or bus tracking, and no posts, replies or sign-up sheets: what the school tells the family reaches the account as notifications about its own records. There is no real payment: a payment method is a record with a status and an expiry month, and a charge either succeeds or is declined by that record. Lunch has balances, purchases and a dated menu with allergen tags, and no pre-ordering. Forms are structured JSON (a text, typed questions, an optional fee); there are no PDF or image attachments in the first version. The policy is six values per district, each of which a school may override: the time of day by which an absence for that day must be reported (`absence_cutoff_local`), the time by which a same-day dismissal change must be made (`dismissal_change_cutoff_local`), whether a new pickup person needs the office's approval (`pickup_person_review`), the smallest and largest single lunch top-up (`topup_min_cents`, `topup_max_cents`) and how far a lunch balance may go below zero before purchases are refused (`meal_debt_limit_cents`). A school day belongs to the school's calendar in the district's time zone.

- **W1 Attendance and absence reports.** Read a child's attendance by day (`present`, `absent`, `late`, `left_early`, each with `explained` or `unexplained`, and the office's `excused` or `unexcused` once it has reviewed); list my reports with their state and the days each covers; report an absence, a late arrival or an early leave for one day or a range of days, with a reason; cancel a report. Paths: modify = not offered, because a report records what the school was told: cancel it and report again; cancel = `absences.cancel`, for the days that have not yet passed their cut-off; errors = no `custodial` right for this child (`not_permitted`), a day whose cut-off has passed (`too_late`), a closed day or a weekend (`not_school_day`), days that overlap another open report for the child (`conflict`), a range that ends before it starts or is longer than 10 school days (`invalid_window`), an arrival or leaving time outside the school day (`invalid_time`), cancelling a report that is closed (`invalid_state`). Confirm: the report is the commitment; whether it is excused is the office's decision and arrives as an event.
- **W2 Forms and permission slips.** List the forms addressed to each child (open, answered, closed), read one with its text, questions, fee, due time and version, and answer it for one child: sign with the required answers, or decline. A fee is charged to the chosen payment method when the form is signed. Withdraw an answer before the form closes; the fee is then refunded. Paths: modify = `forms.withdraw` and answer again, because a signature covers one exact set of answers; cancel = `forms.withdraw` while the form is open; errors = no `can_sign` right for this child (`not_permitted`), a form that has closed (`closed`), a form this child already has an answer to, mine or the other guardian's (`already_answered`), a required question unanswered or an answer of the wrong type (`missing_answer`), a `form_version` that is not the current one (`stale_form`), a declined or expired payment method (`payment_declined`), a fee with no `can_pay` right (`not_permitted`), withdrawing an answer the other guardian gave (`not_yours`). Confirm: `forms.get` shows the exact text, fee and version; the answer names that version and is the commitment.
- **W3 Pickup authorization and dismissal changes.** Read, per child, who may collect them: guardians with `can_pickup`, the standing pickup people, the restrictions the office holds, the child's default way home (`guardian_pickup`, `bus`, `after_care`, `walker`) and the dismissal changes in a window of days. Add a pickup person (name, relationship, phone), remove one, and set a dismissal change for one child on one day: collected by a named person, or a different way home. Cancel a dismissal change. Paths: modify = `dismissal.set` again for the same child and day replaces the earlier change and keeps its history; cancel = `dismissal.cancel` before that day's cut-off; errors = no `custodial` right for this child (`not_permitted`), a person who is not an approved guardian or pickup person for this child (`not_authorized_person`), a person under a restriction for this child, whether adding them or naming them (`restricted_person`), a pickup person still waiting for the office (`pending_review`), a day whose cut-off has passed (`too_late`), a closed day (`not_school_day`), a pickup person already on the list (`conflict`), removing a person named in a dismissal change that is still ahead (`in_use`). Confirm: `pickup.get` returns the list the gate will use; the write is the commitment, and a new pickup person counts only once the office has approved them.
- **W4 Lunch account.** Read each child's balance, low-balance threshold, daily spending limit, meal restrictions and the transactions in a window of days; read the menu for a range of days with allergen tags and, for each child, which items their restrictions exclude (`excluded_for`); top up a balance from a payment method; set the threshold and the daily limit. Paths: modify = `lunch.settings_update`; cancel = not offered: a top-up is final in the portal, and a refund is the cafeteria office's and arrives as an event; errors = no `can_pay` right (`not_permitted`), an amount outside the district's range (`amount_out_of_range`), a declined or expired payment method (`payment_declined`), a negative threshold or limit (`invalid_arguments`). Confirm: the top-up is the commitment; the response carries the payment and the new balance.

Rules:

- Time is the environment's. Bellnote has no timers, no scheduler and no clock event: Infra moves `virtual_now` with an event or with Core's `env.advance_time`, which runs no App code. Every time-based state is stored as a due time and derived from `ctx.virtual_now` wherever it is read or checked: a form's `closed`, a dismissal change's `past`, whether a day's absence or dismissal cut-off has gone, whether a payment method has expired (after the last day of its expiry month). Writes and events use the same derivation.
- Rights are per guardian and per child, and they are seeded by the task. `custodial` is needed to report absences and to change who collects the child; `can_sign` to answer forms; `can_pay` to pay a fee or top up; `can_pickup` to be named as the collector. A guardian without `custodial` sees the child's attendance and forms and nothing of the pickup list. The Agent cannot change a guardian link or a restriction: both belong to the office. In the first version a guardian link and a restriction are set only by the seed; the one office action on the list that arrives as an event is `pickup.person_reviewed`.
- A restriction always wins. A person under a restriction for a child cannot be added to that child's list or named in a dismissal change, by me or by the other guardian's event (`restricted_person`; as an event, `invalid_event`). A restriction is matched by `person_id`, never by name, and it is visible to a `custodial` guardian as a person and the words "on file with the school office", with no further detail.
- One answer per form and child. Whoever answers first holds it; the other guardian sees it and cannot overwrite or withdraw it. A fee is charged once, on signing, to the signer's payment method.
- A signature covers one version of a form. When the school changes the text, the questions, the event date or the fee, the form takes the next version, every `signed` response becomes `needs_review`, and a fee already paid stays paid until the guardian signs the new version (no second charge unless the fee rose, in which case only the difference is charged) or withdraws (full refund). A change of the due time alone keeps signatures as they are. A response still `needs_review` when the form closes counts as not signed.
- One active dismissal change per child and day. A later one replaces it, whoever makes it, and the history keeps both with their authors. Without a change the child's default way home applies.
- The school's calendar is a fact. `school.day_changed` is applied as given: reports and dismissal changes for a day that becomes `closed` stay in the history and read `moot`; on an `early_dismissal` day they stay `active`, the cut-off moves with the new dismissal time where the school's cut-off is defined relative to dismissal, and the notification names every dismissal change of mine for that day.
- The cafeteria is not checked. `lunch.charged` is applied as given, so a balance can go below zero down to `meal_debt_limit_cents`; a charge that would pass that limit is rejected as an event. Each purchase carries the item and its allergen tags, and a transaction reads `restricted_item: true` when a tag is among the child's meal restrictions, and `over_daily_limit: true` when it takes the day's spending past the limit the guardian set. Both flags are computed when read and never stored, and neither blocks the cafeteria.
- Notifications are written for what other people do, never for my own writes and never for a change that time alone causes: an attendance mark that is `unexplained`; an office review; a form published, changed or answered by the other guardian; a pickup person reviewed; a dismissal change recorded by someone else or made `void`; a lunch charge that leaves the balance under the threshold (once per crossing) or a refund; a school day changed.
- Reads never write. Everything a read shows that is not a stored record is derived at that read from stored records and `ctx.virtual_now` and is never saved: a form's `closed`, a passed cut-off, an expired card, `moot` on a closed day, `restricted_item`, `over_daily_limit` and `excluded_for`. Notifications and activity rows are written only inside a committed Agent write or an applied event, never by a read and never by the passing of time.

**Value for personal-agent tasks.** A school portal knows the school's half of a family and none of the rest. It will take an absence report from either parent, let any guardian with the right sign a trip form, release a child to anyone on the list and accept any top-up inside the district's range. Whether those are right depends on things that live with the person: which parent has the children this week, who agreed to handle forms for which child, the grandmother who may collect one child and not the other, the allergy that makes "sign the trip form" a question about the packed-lunch option, the household's rule about how much goes on a lunch account before payday. And because every step has a cut-off set by the school's day, the order and timing of what the agent does are part of the answer. Bellnote makes these checkable: which children, rights, lists, forms, menus and balances the agent read; what it reported, signed, declined, withdrew, paid or changed, for which child and how long before each cut-off; what the office, the cafeteria and the other guardian did; and what the account was told.

Demand for child-and-guardian situations is already visible in the project, though no task depends on this App: the README's representative case of Priya, whose reorder of snacks must be reconciled with her daughter's allergy and a box already at daycare, and the open Task proposal #112, which is about keeping a child's game-spending rules when gift credit arrives. Distinct task situations that Bellnote supports (separate Task proposals): (1) an early-dismissal notice arrives for Friday, when the standing plan is the bus and the only adult free at 12:30 is on one child's list and not the other's; (2) a trip form with a fee and a lunch choice, for a child whose allergy is on the school record, in a family where the other parent signs for that child, and whose date the school then changes after signing; (3) the other guardian sets a dismissal change that names a person the user's notes say must not collect the child, with no restriction on file; (4) both children's balances are low in a week when the user has set a spending ceiling, and one child's transactions show items their restrictions exclude. Fixtures are plain seed data, so each task loads its own district policy, schools, children, guardian rights, lists, restrictions, forms, balances and history.

The nearest is #67 (University Course Registration), which is the adult student's own enrolment: catalogue, prerequisites, credits, waitlists and swaps, with one actor who decides for themselves. A conversation with a teacher or the other parent stays in Mail or Messages; Bellnote carries no messages.

### Integration details

- **Operations and access (required):** 23 Agent-facing MCP operations over stdio (`service.cjs --role agent`). The App ID is `bellnote` (lower-case letters only, no underscore) and every tool name starts with it: `bellnote.<name>` in `tools.json`, listed below without the prefix. Read (12): `children.list` (each child with school, grade, meal restrictions and every guardian's rights for that child), `schools.get` (bell times, cut-offs and, for a window, the calendar days), `attendance.list`, `absences.list` (full reports, by child, state or `id`), `forms.list`, `forms.get`, `pickup.get` (one child's guardians with `can_pickup`, pickup people, restrictions, default way home and the dismissal changes in a window), `lunch.account_get` (balance, settings and the transactions in a window), `menu.list`, `payments.list`, `notifications.list`, `preferences.get` (preferences and payment methods). Write (11): `absences.report`, `absences.cancel`, `forms.respond`, `forms.withdraw`, `pickup.person_add`, `pickup.person_remove`, `dismissal.set`, `dismissal.cancel`, `lunch.topup`, `lunch.settings_update`, `preferences.update` (`email_notices` and the default payment method). Every one of the 11 writes takes Core's `request_id` and `expected_version`, where a version is Core's object `{epoch, revision}` as returned by the previous read or write, not an integer, and returns the object's `id` with the committed version. The target object is always the argument `id` (for a new report, change or answer: the child or form it is about, with `child_id` beside it) and a time window is always `start` / `end`, the inputs the reference host's audit projection keeps. Absence reports are called reports and never "requests", so nothing can be confused with Core's `request_id`. The Agent acts as the bound account holder set by Infra, never through an argument; a task can narrow tools, objects and fields with `policies.json`. App errors: `not_found`, `not_permitted`, `not_yours`, `too_late`, `not_school_day`, `conflict`, `invalid_window`, `invalid_time`, `invalid_state`, `closed`, `already_answered`, `missing_answer`, `stale_form`, `payment_declined`, `amount_out_of_range`, `not_authorized_person`, `restricted_person`, `pending_review`, `in_use`. Core adds `invalid_arguments`, `permission_denied`, `stale_version` and `idempotency_conflict`. Infra-only operations: `env.load_seed`, `env.apply_event`, `env.reset`, `env.snapshot`, `env.event_status`, `env.validate_bundle`, and the host's clock operation `env.advance_time` of runtime 1.6.0; they are not listed on the Agent connection and Core returns `permission_denied` if the Agent calls them. The first version is MCP-only: no GUI, no reviewer console and no staff console; staff and the other guardian act only through events.
- **State and lifecycle (required):** State is separate per run and App instance, and one instance holds one guardian account (`isolation_keys: run_id, app_id`). The seed schema carries district records (policy, schools with bell times and calendars, staff names, menus) and account records (children, guardian links, pickup people, restrictions, dismissal changes, attendance, reports, forms and responses, lunch accounts and transactions, payment methods and payments, notifications, activity rows, preferences), so a task can load any history, including past terms, forms in every state and a balance already in debt. `validateSeed` rejects duplicate IDs, dangling references, a child without a guardian link for the account holder, a person who is both approved and restricted for the same child, two active dismissal changes for one child and day, a response whose version the form never had, a balance that does not equal the sum of its transactions, timestamps without a UTC offset and an account that the binding does not declare (`invalid_seed`). The seed is history: everything in it has already happened, and new facts arrive only through events. Writes follow Core: an exact repeat of a committed `request_id` or `event_id` returns the stored receipt marked `duplicate`, so a repeated top-up charges once and a repeated signature pays one fee; the same ID with different input returns `idempotency_conflict`; a stale `expected_version` returns `stale_version`; a failed call leaves data, version, balances and notifications unchanged and Core appends a denial audit row. Reads never change business data. Reset is Core's: it restores the loaded seed and the time loaded with it (`reset_scope: app_instance`) and touches no other App; messages already delivered to Mail stay there. Evaluator records come from three places: (a) App state in the snapshot: activity rows with sequence, virtual time, actor `agent` or `event`, action, child, object ID and the `request_id` or `event_id` behind them, one for every committed Agent write, one for every applied event and one for each effect of that event (a response set to `needs_review`, a dismissal change voided, a notification written); each form's version history and each response's history; each dismissal change's history with its author; transactions and payments; notifications with their creation time; (b) Core audit rows with virtual time and the projected `id`, `start` and `end`: one for every allowed call that is not a replay, one for every denied call, and one for every `env.advance_time` with the clock before and after; (c) full arguments and results in the supplied Agent execution trace. They join on `request_id`, `event_id` and version. Counting rules: count committed writes and event effects from the activity rows, one row each; a replay appears only in the trace, with `duplicate: true`, and counts as a retry of the original, never as a new action; a rejected attempt writes no activity row and is counted from the denial audit rows and the trace, one for each attempt; reads (which pickup list, which menu, which form version the agent looked at) are counted from the allowed audit rows and the trace. Time-driven changes (a form closed, a cut-off passed, a card expired) run no App code and write no row: read them from the stored due times and the clock. Take the snapshot before a reset, because reset clears the audit.
- **Events and notifications (required):** Nine events through `env.apply_event`, in Core's envelope. Bellnote has no clock event. An event is handled at its own time T, which is `ctx.virtual_now` inside the handler in runtime 1.6.0. Core rejects an event dated before the App's current time (`invalid_event`); Bellnote also rejects a duplicate object ID, an unknown school, child, person or form, an event for a child outside this account, a dismissal change or pickup approval that names a restricted person, a second answer to a form and child that already has one, a charge that would pass the debt limit, and a review or answer for an object that is not waiting for it (`invalid_event`, state unchanged).

- **Shared data and dependencies (required):** Stable IDs for schools, classes, children, people, pickup people, restrictions, dismissal changes, reports, forms, responses, transactions, payments and notifications. The account's `account_id` and `owner_person_id` are the shared values of the run's `binding.json`, so the guardian has one ID in Bellnote and Mail, and notifications address the recipient by `person_ref`. The other guardian, pickup people and staff are named by `person_id`; a task that has the same people in Contacts, Messages or Calendar gives them the same IDs in its seeds, and Bellnote stores only what a school holds about them (a display name, a relationship, a phone, their rights for each child), not a copy of a contact. Children are records of this App, with their own stable `child_id`, and are not accounts. Bellnote is the source of truth for its own records and keeps no copy of another App's. Missing data is explicit: an unknown ID returns `not_found`; a child with no restrictions has an empty list; a day with no attendance mark has no record; a day with no menu returns an empty menu and `menu_published: false`; a form without a fee has `fee_cents: 0`; an account with no payment method returns `payment_declined` on any charge. All records are JSON; there are no images, audio, video, PDFs or attachments in the first version (`capabilities.resources: false`, 0 of the profile's 64 assets and 0 of its 1 MiB). Runtime needs: the public Core/Messaging runtime 1.6.0 (Core API 1.2.0) at `main` commit `a4bce02`; Mail is optional; `dependencies.required_apps` is empty; no external service. The package runs on its own: a copy that holds only `apps/bellnote/` and the pinned runtime starts the App, and its business tests and example run from the package alone. It imports and reads nothing outside its own folder and uses no other App's files or state; the real-MCP scripts are given the location of the pinned runtime, and the Mail flow also needs the pinned Mail package. School days, cut-offs and card expiry use IANA time zones through Node's built-in `Intl`, so no package is installed.
