## 2. What you plan to build

Briefly describe the main workflows and why this environment would be useful for personal-agent tasks.

**✍️ Your answer (required):** A resettable graduate-degree portal, seen from one doctoral student's account, that carries the last stretch of a degree: forming the committee, scheduling the defense, getting the committee copy in on time, passing format review and depositing the final dissertation. The portal accepts whatever is structurally valid before each cutoff, so an action it accepts can still be wrong for this student.

Workflows:

1. **Committee:** propose a committee (chair, members, one external member), withdraw the proposal before the office decides, propose again after a rejection, and propose a changed committee later. Member responses and the office decision arrive as Infra events.
2. **Defense:** request a defense date, time, room and mode; reschedule or cancel it. The portal derives the later deadlines from the defense date.
3. **Committee copy:** submit a document version as the committee copy, and ask the chair for a short delay before the deadline. The chair's decision arrives as an Infra event.
4. **Format review and deposit:** submit a version for format review, read the reviewer's required changes, resubmit, and deposit the approved version with an embargo choice after the defense result is recorded.
5. **Notices:** Regalia records four notices for the student: defense request received, committee decision, delay decision and format review result. When Mail is enabled, the host delivers them to the student's inbox (Section 3).

Business rules that make the right action depend on the person:

- **Deadlines follow the defense date.** The seed sets the university's IANA time zone, and day counts are calendar-date differences in that zone. A defense must be requested at least 21 days ahead: a defense on Apr 6 can be requested through Mar 16 and not on Mar 17. The committee copy is due at 23:59 on the 14th day before the defense, Mar 23 in this example. Rescheduling moves both deadlines and voids any open or approved delay.
- **A submission is a snapshot.** A committee copy or a format submission records the exact document version. A later version does not replace it; a correction is a new submission, and for a committee copy it is accepted only before its deadline. A committee copy belongs to the defense request it was submitted under. The current committee copy is the latest snapshot for the active request, and it stays current after a reschedule. After a cancellation or a result, earlier copies stay readable and a new request starts with none. Missing the committee copy has no automatic effect; the Task decides what the chair does.
- **Approval is bound to a version.** Format approval covers one version. Only that version can be deposited; a newer version needs a new review, and a version that received `changes_required` cannot be submitted again. For example, if v8 is approved on Apr 20 and v9 is added on Apr 22, a deposit of v9 is rejected until v9 passes review.
- **A delay is short and asked in advance.** A delay may run to 23:59 on the third business day after the deadline date, and must be requested before the deadline derived from the current defense date. A business day is Monday to Friday minus the term calendar's closure days. One delay request may be open at a time; if a second one is approved later, the latest approval sets the deadline. An approved delay is stored as an instant and is not recomputed when closure days change.
- **The committee locks.** A committee is a chair plus at least three other members, exactly one of whom is external. A changed committee can be proposed while one is approved; the approved one stays in force until the office approves the new one. From the 21st day before a requested defense, a new proposal and an office approval are both rejected.
- **Deposit follows a pass and is final.** `passed` and `passed_with_revisions` both open the deposit; revisions are carried by a new version that must pass format review like any other. The portal accepts any format-approved version for deposit and does not check that it was added after the defense; whether the revisions are in it is for the Task to judge. The deposited version and the embargo (none, 6, 12 or 24 months) cannot be changed afterwards, and the deposit closes at the term's deposit deadline.

Entities: student account, document and its immutable versions, committee proposal and member responses, defense request, submission (committee copy or format submission, an immutable snapshot), delay request, format review result, deposit, term calendar and outbound notice.

Statuses. Committee proposal: `proposed` → `approved` or `rejected`, or `withdrawn`; `approved` → `superseded` when a later proposal is approved. Defense request: `requested` → `held` once a result is recorded, or `cancelled`; a request is active only while `requested`. After a `failed` result the student may make a new request; after either pass, a new request is rejected. Delay request (belongs to one defense request): `requested` → `approved` or `declined`; `requested` or `approved` → `voided` when that defense is rescheduled, cancelled or has a result recorded. Format submission: `in_review` → `approved` or `changes_required`. Deposit: `deposited`.

Reusable value: tasks with different stories can use these rules. Examples: a committee copy whose numbers are out of date while the student is travelling (Task #200, which today stands in for the portal with a checklist email and a delay request by Mail); a defense date that must fit a visa or job start date; a replacement for a committee member that must be approved before the lock date; an embargo that depends on a co-author's unpublished paper or a patent filing; a format approval that would not cover a last-minute fix. The App holds no rubric and no task answer.

Related work, checked against the open proposals on 2026-11-23: #148 (paper release and submission receipts), #67 (course registration), #4 (research library) and Vouchfile #218 (dossiers and confidential letters, same author). None covers degree milestones, defense scheduling or dissertation deposit. Regalia uses the same names as Vouchfile for documents, versions and snapshots, so tasks can use both.

## 3. Potential app connections

Name possible connections and briefly explain their purpose. Internal personal apps include Mail, Calendar, Contacts and similar tools. Use `None` if no connection applies, or `TBD` if undecided.

For internal personal Apps, state which workflow needs each App and whether the connection is required or optional. Link the existing public interface/version, or identify the missing capability, owner and fallback.

- **Internal personal apps (required):** Mail 1.3.0, Files 1.0.0, Calendar 1.3.0 and Contacts 1.0.0, all optional; details below.
  - **Mail 1.3.0, optional, in v1 scope (workflow 5).** Regalia stores each notice as an outbound record in its own state and exposes it as a stable CloudEvent through the read-only tool `regalia.outbound` (all notices, or those after a given notice ID), as `messages.outbound` does for chat messages. It declares one `messaging.publishes` entry (channel `channel.email`, event type `org.pale.mail.notification.v1`, schema `urn:pale:email-message:2`, payload `urn:pale:email-payload:1`, and a `schema_ref` to a pinned, byte-identical copy of Mail's wire schema). Mail receives it with `mail.receive_notification`, as declared in [`examples/apps/mail/contribution/contribution.json`](../../examples/apps/mail/contribution/contribution.json). The host or runner reads new notices through `regalia.outbound` on an agent-role connection, which the task policy must allow, and calls `router.publish` on the Infra-supplied publisher connection; the Agent and the App handlers never publish. This polling is a platform dependency: if the runner does not publish, a Task can inject the same notice with Mail's own `mail.deliver` event. Delivery needs a router configuration that lists Regalia as a publisher with `allowed_recipients: [{app_id: mail, account_id: <student's mail account>, channel: channel.email}]`, and the recipient is `{kind: person_ref, id: <student's person ID from binding.json>}`. Regalia runs without Mail: notices are then recorded and nothing is published. No attachments are sent.
  - **Files 1.0.0, optional (workflows 3 and 4).** The agent reads a dissertation version with `files.read`, which returns base64 bytes and SHA-256, and passes both to `regalia.document.add`. There is no messaging between the two Apps.
  - **Calendar 1.3.0, optional (workflow 2).** The agent may check availability and record the defense with Calendar's own tools. Calendar has no messaging receiver, and Regalia sends it nothing.
  - **Contacts 1.0.0, optional (workflow 1).** The agent finds committee members with `contacts.list` and `contacts.get`. `regalia.committee.propose` takes each member's person ID from `fixtures/binding.json`.
- **Other apps and websites (required):** None at run time. Committee members, the chair, the graduate office and the format reviewer are outside actors; their actions arrive as validated Infra events whose timing the Task decides.

### Integration details

- **Operations and access (required):** MCP only in v1. Agent tools and their effects are listed below. Write tools take `expected_version` and `request_id`. `request_id` is Core's idempotency key; portal objects are addressed by `id`.

  | Tool | Effect | Precondition | Result | Failure example |
  | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
  | `regalia.status.get` | read | own account | every milestone with its status and computed deadline, including any delay request and the deposit | — |
  | `regalia.policy.get` | read | — | the portal's rules page and the term calendar, so every rule above is discoverable | — |
  | `regalia.documents.list` / `regalia.document.get` | read | own account | metadata; `get` returns bytes of a selected version | unknown ID → `not_found` |
  | `regalia.document.add` | write | base64 and SHA-256 match; PDF or UTF-8 text; at most 16 KB | new document or new version | hash mismatch → Core's `invalid_attachment`; larger file → `file_too_large` (App pre-check); instance budget exceeded → Core's `invalid_bundle` |
  | `regalia.committee.get` | read | own account | the committee in force, any pending proposal, member responses and decisions | — |
  | `regalia.committee.propose` | write | no proposal in `proposed`; a chair plus at least three other members, exactly one external; every member is a bound person; outside the lock | proposal `proposed` | a proposal already pending → `proposal_pending`; too few members → `committee_incomplete`; unbound person → `unknown_person`; inside the lock → `committee_locked` |
  | `regalia.committee.withdraw` | write | proposal `proposed` | proposal `withdrawn` | already decided → `proposal_closed` |
  | `regalia.defense.get` | read | own account | request, derived deadlines, delay requests and result | — |
  | `regalia.defense.request` | write | committee `approved`; defense at least 21 days ahead; no active request; no earlier pass | request `requested`; deadlines derived; notice recorded | an active request exists → `defense_active`; committee not approved → `committee_not_approved`; 20 days ahead → `too_late_to_schedule`; after a pass → `defense_closed` |
  | `regalia.defense.reschedule` | write | request `requested`; before the defense time; a new date at least 21 days ahead, or the same date with a new time, room or mode, which moves no deadline and voids nothing | new date; deadlines moved; open or approved delay `voided`; earlier submissions kept | 20 days ahead → `too_late_to_schedule`; after a result → `defense_closed` |
  | `regalia.defense.cancel` | write | request `requested`; before the defense time | request `cancelled`; delay `voided`; committee unlocked; earlier submissions stay readable | after a result → `defense_closed` |
  | `regalia.submissions.list` / `regalia.submission.get` | read | own account | snapshots with kind, document version, time and review result | unknown ID → `not_found` |
  | `regalia.copy.submit` | write | active defense request; before the committee-copy deadline or within an approved delay | immutable committee-copy snapshot | no active request → `no_active_defense`; after the deadline → `deadline_passed` |
  | `regalia.delay.request` | write | active defense request; before the deadline derived from the current defense date; no delay request in `requested` for that defense; requested time no later than 23:59 on the third business day after the deadline date | delay request `requested` | after the deadline → `deadline_passed`; fourth business day → `delay_too_long`; one already open → `delay_pending` |
  | `regalia.format.submit` | write | no submission `in_review`; version not reviewed before | format submission `in_review` for that version | one already in review → `review_in_progress`; version already reviewed → `version_already_reviewed` |
  | `regalia.deposit.submit` | write | defense result `passed` or `passed_with_revisions`; version has format approval; embargo chosen; before the term's deposit deadline; no deposit yet | deposit `deposited`, final | unapproved version → `version_not_approved`; second deposit → `already_deposited`; late → `deadline_passed` |
  | `regalia.outbound` | read | own account | recorded notices as stable CloudEvents; the tool reports no delivery state | — |

  Infra uses the shared management operations (`env.load_seed`, `env.apply_event`, `env.reset`, `env.snapshot`, `env.event_status`, `env.validate_bundle`, `env.advance_time`), which are not available on the Agent connection. `fixtures/policies.json` ships a read-only `scenario` profile and a `sandbox` profile that allows all writes; Tasks supply their own policy. Document bytes are read through the platform's resource-access check: the proposed registration `runtime/registrations/regalia.json` maps `document` to `regalia.document.get`, and reads also need the task policy to allow that tool.
- **State and lifecycle (required):** Core persists one state per run and App instance, and each instance holds exactly one student account. A seed loads the account, the term calendar, documents and versions, and any stage of the process: a committee proposal with responses, a defense request, submissions with review results, a delay request and a deposit. Reset restores the loaded baseline, and snapshots are Infra-only. Writes check `expected_version`; a repeated `request_id` with the same arguments, including `expected_version`, returns the original result, and with different arguments fails with `idempotency_conflict`. A failed write or event changes no business state and no clock. Current time comes from `ctx.virtual_now`. Deadlines are computed in the seed's IANA time zone as described in Section 2, and an event cannot move time backwards. Regalia's shipped bundles reject missing assets.
- **Events and notifications (required):** Events use the runtime shape `{event_id, at, operation, payload}`. Besides the business codes below, Core rejects a payload or operation that does not match the event schema with `invalid_arguments` and a time that moves backwards with `invalid_event`.

  | Operation | Payload | Effect | Rejected when |
  | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
  | `regalia.member.responded` | `proposal_id`, `member_id`, `response` (`accepted`, `declined`) | response recorded on the proposal; it informs the office and does not decide anything | unknown proposal or member → `not_found`; proposal not `proposed` → `invalid_transition` |
  | `regalia.office.committee_decided` | `proposal_id`, `decision` (`approved`, `rejected`), optional `reason` | proposal decided; on approval the earlier approved proposal becomes `superseded`; notice recorded | proposal not `proposed` → `invalid_transition`; approval inside the lock → `committee_locked`, and the proposal stays `proposed` |
  | `regalia.chair.delay_decided` | `delay_id`, `decision` (`approved`, `declined`), `until` when approved | delay decided; when approved, the copy deadline moves to `until`; notice recorded | delay not `requested` → `invalid_transition`; `until` not after the derived deadline → `invalid_input`; `until` after 23:59 on the third business day → `delay_too_long` |
  | `regalia.format.reviewed` | `submission_id`, `result` (`approved`, `changes_required`), `changes` list | review result bound to that submission's version; notice recorded | submission not `in_review` → `invalid_transition` |
  | `regalia.defense.result_recorded` | `defense_id`, `result` (`passed`, `passed_with_revisions`, `failed`) | request → `held`; open or approved delay `voided`; committee unlocked; either pass opens the deposit | request not `requested` → `invalid_transition`; before the defense time → `too_early` |
  | `regalia.term.updated` | `deposit_deadline` and/or `closure_days` (a list of dates that replaces the current list) | term calendar updated; later business-day counts use the new closure days; approved delays unchanged | deadline in the past → `invalid_input` |

  Example: `{"event_id":"evt-fmt-2","at":"2027-04-20T15:30:00-05:00","operation":"regalia.format.reviewed","payload":{"submission_id":"sub-4","result":"approved","changes":[]}}` approves the version in sub-4 and no other. The other five, for a seed in the `America/Chicago` zone and a defense df-1 at 10:00 on Tuesday, Apr 6, 2027, whose committee copy is due at 23:59 on Mar 23: `{"event_id":"evt-m3","at":"2027-02-10T09:00:00-06:00","operation":"regalia.member.responded","payload":{"proposal_id":"cp-1","member_id":"person-lin","response":"accepted"}}`; `{"event_id":"evt-cp-1","at":"2027-02-15T14:00:00-06:00","operation":"regalia.office.committee_decided","payload":{"proposal_id":"cp-1","decision":"approved"}}`; `{"event_id":"evt-dl-1","at":"2027-03-23T11:05:00-05:00","operation":"regalia.chair.delay_decided","payload":{"delay_id":"dl-1","decision":"approved","until":"2027-03-26T12:00:00-05:00"}}` (Mar 26 is the third business day after Mar 23); `{"event_id":"evt-df-1","at":"2027-04-06T12:30:00-05:00","operation":"regalia.defense.result_recorded","payload":{"defense_id":"df-1","result":"passed_with_revisions"}}`; and `{"event_id":"evt-term-1","at":"2027-04-01T09:00:00-05:00","operation":"regalia.term.updated","payload":{"deposit_deadline":"2027-05-07T17:00:00-05:00","closure_days":["2027-04-16"]}}`. A rejected event rolls back completely.

  Notice example, following the shape of `examples/workflows/delivery-to-mail/delivery/message.json`: `{"specversion":"1.0","id":"regalia-7c1e9a52","source":"urn:pale:app:regalia:account:regalia-wen","type":"org.pale.mail.notification.v1","time":"2027-03-23T11:05:00-05:00","datacontenttype":"application/json","dataschema":"urn:pale:email-payload:1","data":{"channel":"channel.email","recipient":{"kind":"person_ref","id":"person-wen"},"source_object":{"app_id":"regalia","account_id":"regalia-wen","object_type":"delay_request","object_id":"dl-1"},"content":{"title":"Delay approved for your committee copy","body":{"format":"text/plain","text":"Your chair approved a delay. The committee copy is now due by 12:00 on Mar 26."},"asset_ids":[]},"extensions":[]}}`. The plan includes one such example per notice type, each checked against Mail's wire schema with the public validator. The Regalia commit and the Mail commit are separate: a rejected or unknown receipt does not roll back anything in Regalia. The receipt is returned to the host by `router.publish` and logged by Messaging; Regalia does not learn it. Delivery is verified in Mail. A notice ID is `regalia-` plus a SHA-256 prefix of the source object ID, notice type, a per-object sequence number and the content, so a retry is stable and changed content gets a new ID.
- **Shared data and dependencies (required):** The student is bound to a person and an account, and the chair and committee members to people, through `fixtures/binding.json`, so a Task can align them with Contacts and Mail records. Object references are document version, proposal, member, defense, submission, delay and deposit IDs. Media: documents in PDF or UTF-8 text, each at most 16 KB. Each seed uses at most 16 assets (at most 256 KB), which leaves room for 48 more within the 64-asset / 1 MiB per-instance budget; run-time uploads count against the same budget. The package runs alone with the shared runtime and declares no package dependencies. Mail is a recipient chosen in the run assembly, and the integration test uses a router configuration and registry in the style of `examples/workflows/delivery-to-mail`.
