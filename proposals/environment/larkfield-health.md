## 2. What you plan to build

**✍️ Your answer (required):** A resettable member portal for one employer health plan, where a household reads claims and explanations of benefits (EOBs), answers the plan's information requests, follows prior authorizations (PAs), and files, supports or withdraws appeals within their deadlines. The App records facts, statuses, amounts and consequences; it does not decide medical necessity, rank options or say what the right action is. No third-party code, brand assets, real member data or proprietary billing codes; services use plain synthetic descriptions.

**Simulated policies and simplifications.** Time is the App's own virtual clock (Core keeps one per App, so in a #63 pairing the two clocks advance separately), in one time zone (America/New_York); day-based deadlines end at 23:59 on their last day; money is USD in integer cents; accumulators are kept per plan year (January 1 to December 31) by service date.

- One EPO-style plan: in-network care only, except emergency care, which is covered at in-network cost sharing and never needs PA. Embedded deductibles and out-of-pocket maximums (a member stops at the individual amount; the family stops at the family amount), 20% coinsurance, copays by service category; copays count toward the out-of-pocket maximum, not the deductible. Pharmacy claims belong to Mendwell Pharmacy (#63); Larkfield records pharmacy PAs and the plan's decisions on them.
- Adjudication is deterministic, 3 days after a claim is received, line by line in a stable order (service date, then line number): non-emergency out-of-network → `out_of_network`; a PA-listed non-emergency service needs a PA for the same member and service, in `approved` or `expired` status, whose validity covers the service date and whose remaining units cover the whole line, otherwise `prior_auth_missing` with no units used; among several qualifying PAs, units come from the one with the earliest `valid_until`, then the lowest PA ID; the allowed amount is the lesser of billed and fee-schedule amounts, split into copay, or deductible then coinsurance, within the out-of-pocket limits; each paid PA-covered line uses its units.
- Information requests open at the 3-day review and are typed: `other_coverage` on every line when the member's other-coverage status for that plan year is `unknown`, answered only by `coverage.cob.confirm` (`none`, `other_secondary` or `other_primary`); and `accident` on each line that `claim.received` flags `possible_accident`, answered only by `claims.info.confirm` (`not_accident`, `accident` or `work_related`). Opening a request emits `notice.info_requested`. An attestation sets the member's status for that plan year; while it is `other_primary`, later lines are denied `other_party_liable` without a new request. `other_primary` and `work_related` deny the affected lines `other_party_liable`, because two-plan and third-party payment are out of scope; the other answers adjudicate normally. Attestations are per member and plan year: one resolves, in received order, only that member's open other-coverage requests for lines whose service dates fall in that plan year; `coverage.cob.confirm` with no open request for that member and year returns `info_not_requested`. A claim stays `pending_info` until each request on it is answered or expires 45 days after its notice; expiry denies only the unresolved lines `missing_information` and keeps any `other_party_liable` denial an answer already set. The claim is then adjudicated in one pass, against the accumulators and PA units at that moment; request outcomes apply first, and a line they deny is not checked further. EOB v1 is issued once, when nothing is open.
- Deadlines are app policy modelled on US employer-plan rules: appeals are filed within 180 days of the denial notice; PA decisions are due 15 days after submission; appeal decisions are due 30 days (PA) or 60 days (claim) after filing; an approved expedite moves the due date to the earlier of the existing one and 72 hours after the expedite request. Due dates never pause, a simplification. A missed due date sets `overdue` once, including when an approved expedite moves the due date into the past; the App never decides a PA or an appeal itself.
- Only denied lines are appealable, a simplification (real plans also accept appeals of amounts). An overturned claim appeal re-prices only the lines it names, against the accumulators at that moment and without using PA units; lines it does not name stay denied, and other claims are never re-adjudicated. Each adult's records are private to that adult, which is stricter than the common practice of sending EOBs to the subscriber, so delegation is observable.

**Workflows.**

1. **Household access.** Only an adult member (for their own records) or a seeded guardian of a minor grants or revokes member-wide `view` or `act` (authorized representative) access, with an optional expiry, after a user confirmation. Grants made in another member's session arrive as `grant.changed` events.
2. **Claims and EOBs.** Read claims, lines, versioned EOBs and the caller's permitted accumulators. Answer information requests through `coverage.cob.preview` / `.confirm` and `claims.info.preview` / `.confirm`; both are attestations that need a user confirmation.
3. **Prior authorization.** Providers and pharmacies submit, supplement or withdraw PAs through events; only the plan decides them, from `pending` or `info_requested`. Approvals carry validity dates and units. A member may ask once for expedited review while a PA is undecided; the plan answers through an event.
4. **Appeals.** File against a denied claim (its denied lines) or a denied PA, with a statement; add statements while the appeal is open; withdraw. There is one internal appeal level: once an appeal on a target is `upheld` or `overturned`, filing on it again returns `not_appealable`; a withdrawn appeal may be filed again within the original window. Overturning a claim appeal overrides the lines named in `appeal.decided`; overturning a PA appeal approves the PA with the units and `valid_until` carried by `appeal.decided`, valid from the decision. An expedited PA appeal is due 72 hours after filing, with no separate plan approval step. Approving a PA never re-adjudicates processed claims.

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

Rules:

- Filing requires authority, a denied target, a date within 180 days of that denial's notice, no open appeal on the target (`duplicate_request`) and no decided appeal on it (`not_appealable`). Expedited filing is allowed only for a denied PA, with an urgency attestation. Checks run in this order and return the first failure: authority (`not_authorized`), an appealable target (`not_appealable`), no open appeal (`duplicate_request`), the window (`appeal_window_closed`), expedite eligibility (`expedite_not_allowed`).
- Re-adjudication creates a new EOB version, posts the accumulator difference and keeps every earlier version; the seed's accumulators must equal the sum of current EOB versions.
- An expedite request is allowed once per PA while it is `pending` or `info_requested`; a second request on such a PA returns `duplicate_request`, and a PA in any other state returns `expedite_not_allowed`. A `pa.submitted` whose dates and units an approved PA fully covers creates no new PA; it adds a `covered_submission` entry, visible in `pa.get`, to the covering PA, with no notice.
- Appeals in `upheld`, `overturned` or `withdrawn` accept no Agent writes; mutating calls return `invalid_state`, and records stay readable.

**Value for personal-agent tasks.** Insurance work for a family depends on whose records an agent may see, which denial is still appealable, which PA covers which date, and what the EOB actually charged. The App exposes reusable behavior, not one task's answer. Examples (separate Task proposals): appeal a child's denied imaging claim before the window closes, citing a PA approved for different dates; reconcile a spouse's bill without seeing his records until he grants access.

**Boundaries.** The Northlake Patient Portal (#14) shows provider bills and defers insurer adjudication, which Larkfield owns. With Mendwell Pharmacy (#63, same author) the link is a paired-event mapping run by a Task or Infra, not an App-to-App channel: Mendwell's `prior_auth_required` plan request can be mirrored here as `pa.submitted`, and this PA's decision mirrored back as Mendwell's `plan.decided` for that plan request. How Mendwell applies a decision is #63's rule; a new submission for the same drug is answered here from the approved PA. Both links are optional.

### Integration details

- **Operations and access (required):** 21 Agent-facing MCP operations. Read (9): `members.list`, `grants.list`, `plan.get` (benefits and permitted accumulators), `claims.list`, `claims.get` (lines, requests and EOB versions), `pa.list`, `pa.get`, `appeals.list`, `appeals.get` (the three `get` operations include deadlines, due dates and the `overdue` flag). Preview, read-only (5): `grants.change.preview`, `coverage.cob.preview`, `claims.info.preview`, `appeals.file.preview`, `appeals.withdraw.preview`. Write (7): `grants.change.confirm`, `coverage.cob.confirm`, `claims.info.confirm`, `pa.request_expedite`, `appeals.file.confirm`, `appeals.add_statement`, `appeals.withdraw.confirm`. The Agent acts as the bound account holder set by Infra, never through an argument.

  | Authority | Allows |
  | :--- | :--- |
  | Adult member | Everything for their own records; grant changes with a user confirmation |
  | Guardian of a minor | Everything for that minor |
  | `view` grant (member-wide) | That member's claims, EOBs, individual accumulators, PAs and appeals |
  | `act` grant (member-wide, includes `view`) | Only these plan transactions for that member: information answers, expedite requests, and filing, supporting or withdrawing appeals |

  Unknown IDs return `not_found`; records of a member the caller may not see return `not_authorized`. Every read, list and notice is filtered by member: `members.list`, `grants.list` and `plan.get` show only members the caller may see. `plan.get` returns family accumulator totals only if the caller may view every member enrolled in the family for that plan year, including minors; otherwise it returns only the permitted individual totals. Previews store nothing. `confirmation_invalid` means the approval is expired, already used, or from someone other than the required confirmer; restated arguments or a target version that no longer reproduce the token return `stale_preview`. A preview runs the same authority and state checks as its confirm and returns that error instead of a token; otherwise the token is a digest of the actor, the action, its full arguments, the run ID and the target object's own version (an App-maintained counter that changes only when that record changes, so unrelated writes and the approval event itself do not invalidate it). The confirm carries the token, restates the arguments and Core's current `expected_version` like any write; the App finds the approval by the token and compares it with a digest recomputed from current state (targets: the grant pair, the member's other-coverage record, the information request, the appealed claim or PA, and the appeal). Each of the five confirms needs a `user.confirmed` event naming the token from the bound account holder; it is single-use, expires after 30 simulated minutes, is consumed only by a successful commit. Confirms check authority, then the approval, then preview freshness, then state, and return the first failure. `pa.request_expedite` and `appeals.add_statement` are not gated because they change no coverage; the plan decides. Infra-only operations: `env.load_seed`, `env.apply_event`, reset and snapshot; Core returns `permission_denied` when the Agent calls them. App errors: `not_authorized`, `confirmation_required`, `confirmation_invalid`, `stale_preview`, `duplicate_request`, `appeal_window_closed`, `not_appealable`, `expedite_not_allowed`, `info_not_requested`, `invalid_state`, `not_found`; Core adds `idempotency_conflict`, `stale_version` and `permission_denied`.
- **State and lifecycle (required):** State is per run and account, loaded by `env.load_seed` and validated (unique IDs, grants consistent with members and guardians, accumulators equal to the sum of current EOB versions). Within an epoch, Core returns the stored receipt (marked duplicate) for an exact repeat of a committed request or event, and `idempotency_conflict` for the same ID with changed input; a new write or event (a fresh ID) with an old epoch or revision, including one from before a reset, gets `stale_version`. A failed Agent call preserves business data, version and notices; Core appends a denial audit entry. Reset is Core's new epoch: Core restores the seed and its fixed clock start, to which seed dates are anchored; approvals are discarded, and timers, being derived from deadlines in App data, return to the seed's.
- **Events and notifications (required):** Events use Core's envelope via `env.apply_event`: `claim.received`, `pa.submitted`, `pa.info_requested`, `pa.info_provided`, `pa.decided`, `pa.withdrawn`, `pa.expedite_decided`, `appeal.info_requested`, `appeal.decided`, `grant.changed`, `user.confirmed`, `clock.advanced` (empty payload; moves time only). The clock moves only through events; for each event Larkfield applies timers due before its time in a stable order, then the event, then timers due at that time. Late events change no business state: PA events apply only to a PA in a matching state, appeal events only to an open appeal; a late event is still committed as a no-op, so time advances and due timers run. Timers fire by due time, then object ID. Example: `{"event_id": "ev-21", "at": "2026-12-08T09:00:00-05:00", "operation": "appeal.decided", "payload": {"appeal_id": "ap-3", "outcome": "overturned", "lines": ["ln-2"]}}`. Notices, each emitted once per occurrence: `notice.eob_available`, `notice.info_requested`, `notice.claim_denied`, `notice.pa_decision`, `notice.appeal_received`, `notice.appeal_info_requested`, `notice.appeal_decision`, `notice.appeal_window_closing` (14 days before the deadline, only while the target is still appealable), `notice.decision_overdue`. Recipients are the member, guardians, and `view` or `act` holders for that member. Each notice is one log entry per recipient; an Infra-run sender adapter reads the log, rechecks each recipient's access at dispatch and calls `router.publish` for those with a bound Mail account and grant. A rejected delivery leaves Mail unchanged and does not roll back Larkfield; an unknown acknowledgement stops the run for reconciliation.
- **Shared data and dependencies (required):** Stable IDs for members, providers, claims, lines, requests, PAs and appeals; PA IDs can be paired with #63 plan requests. Notices are plain text, without attachments. Runtime needs: the public Core/Messaging runtime in PR #12 (runtime 1.4.0); Mail, #63 and #14 are optional; no external service.
