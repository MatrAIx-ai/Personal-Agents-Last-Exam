## 2. What you plan to build

**✍️ Your answer (required):** Switchback is a resettable campground reservation site with holds, a fee ladder and clock-driven closures, where a booking can check out cleanly and still be wrong for the people going.

The 15-minute cart hold and $10 change fee follow Recreation.gov's public pages. The release schedule, refund ladder, cutoffs and alert effects are Switchback's own simplified rules. As of 2026-11-16, Weather & outdoors has no environment or task proposal and, by the author's reading of the public PersonalAgentEval case list, no v1 task.

Workflows 1 to 4 are the acceptance baseline. Every rule is printed on the policy page and on the preview it affects. Times are facility time at a fixed UTC offset.

1. **Search and site detail.** Each site page lists electric hookup and amperage, maximum vehicle length, ADA access, walk-in distance, occupancy and pets; the matching filters are off by default. The booking form checks only equipment length, party size and pets. Night states: `unreleased`, `open`, `held`, `booked`, `closed`. Nights release 180 days ahead at 07:00.
2. **Reservation lifecycle.** `cart.hold` holds a site for 15 simulated minutes; then preview, confirm and receipt. The preview shows the primary occupant (account holder or saved companion, by ID), the pre-filled saved equipment and companions, the price, the ladder dates and the paying card with its cardholder and expiry. Confirm copies equipment and companion values into the reservation. A preview is single-use: the same `request_id` returns the same receipt, a new one `preview_consumed`. States: `confirmed -> checked_in -> completed`, with exits `cancelled`, `no_show` and `closure_cancelled`. A scheduled competing claim takes a night only if it is `open`.
3. **Modify, cancel and no-show.** Refund rungs change at 00:00 on the 14th and 4th day before arrival and at 15:00 on arrival day. In order, a cancel refunds everything; all but $10; all but $10 and the first night; nothing. Refunds never go below zero and return to the original card three days later. The cancel preview names the rung, refund, card and next rung change. Date or site changes cost $10, are allowed only in the first two rungs of the original arrival date, and release the original nights at confirm, which cannot be undone. The modify preview holds the new nights and shows the price difference and card; its confirm re-runs workflow 2's checks. Without check-in the site is released at 21:00 on arrival day, or 12:00 the next day after a late-arrival notice filed before 21:00, and the stay becomes `no_show` with no refund.
4. **Conditions and alerts.** Scheduled alerts cover fire-restriction stage, road or trail restriction, water or electrical outage and smoke advisory. Each names a facility, optional sites and a window, and affects a reservation whose site and any night fall inside. These leave the reservation `confirmed`, mark it affected and keep the ordinary ladder; an active outage also removes the site from the matching filter. A closure covering any night cancels the whole stay (`closure_cancelled`) with a full refund to the original card; its nights reopen when the window ends. A scripted forecast panel gives five days per facility; each version carries `issued_at` and the next issuance time.

Errors. `cart.hold`: `not_yet_released`, `site_taken`, `site_closed`. Previews: `hold_expired`, `equipment_exceeds_site`, `occupancy_exceeded`, `pets_not_allowed`, `outside_change_window`. Confirms: `preview_stale`, `preview_consumed`, `payment_declined` (expired card), `site_closed`. Cancel: `already_cancelled`, `already_checked_in`. Late arrival: `notice_too_late`. Any write: `version_conflict`.

Optional, outside the baseline: (A) a permit lottery in which naming one person on two applications voids both at the draw, as the policy page and application preview state; (B) passes and discounts; (C) a revocable trip-plan share link.

Value for personal-agent tasks: the routine path succeeds, so nothing prompts a second look. No form asks about power, step-free access or carry distance. Stale saved equipment, stale companions and another person's default card check out cleanly. Date changes cannot be undone, and holds, rungs and the notice cutoff are deadlines on the simulated clock. Personal facts come from Mail, Calendar or the conversation, never from Switchback.

Downstream tasks it is meant to host:

- Rebook a family's lake weekend "same as always" after one relative began needing overnight power and another bought a longer trailer, with a relative's card still the default.
- Go or no-go on a booked trip: wait for the next forecast issuance, then act before the refund rung changes.
- Notices arrive between sessions: a closure needs no action; an electrical outage removes the reason the site was chosen.
- A delayed drive on arrival day: file the late-arrival notice for the right stay, not a cancel in the no-refund rung.

## 3. Potential app connections

- **Internal personal apps (required):** Mail and Calendar only
  - Mail receives confirmations, refunds, closure notices and alerts, each carrying the reservation ID, dates and amount on labelled lines.
  - Calendar: Switchback emits a `calendar.upsert`-shaped stay entry (cancelled on removal) for the platform to apply; without an app-to-Calendar route, the scenario sets the entries.
  - Files, Contacts and Messages are not needed.
- **Other apps and websites (required):** None in the baseline

### Integration details

- **Operations and access (required):** Agent-facing operations are MCP tools: `facilities.search|get`, `sites.search|get`, `policy.get`, `cart.hold|release`, `reservations.preview|confirm|list|get|late_arrival`, `reservations.modify.preview|confirm`, `reservations.cancel.preview|confirm`, `alerts.list`, `forecast.get`, `account.get`, `wallet.list|set_default`, `equipment.save`, `companions.save`. All tools except `search`, `get` and `list` are writes, previews included, and take `expected_version` and `request_id`. The three confirms and `wallet.set_default` can be gated behind user approval. The renderer is a stateless view over the same tool results, as in #12; whether agents get that surface is the platform's decision. Platform operations: `env.reset`, `env.load_seed`, `env.snapshot`, `env.apply_event`. The business model is a pure rules core behind a thin interface layer, portable if the first infrastructure release differs from #12.
- **State and lifecycle (required):** App state (catalogue, policy, inventory), account state (wallet, saved records, reservations, receipts), run state (clock, holds, previews, call log) and evaluator state (the event schedule) are separate. Clock-derived transitions are a pure function of state and `virtual_now`, applied before every tool call and event in (`at`, kind, ID) order; where a notice must arrive without a tool call, the scenario schedules a `clock.sync` event. The call log records every call, including reads and rejected writes. A preview is void once its hold expires or a rung changes (`preview_stale`); `version_conflict` changes nothing. Reset reloads the seed, seeded reservations and schedule included, and removes everything created in the run.
- **Events and notifications (required):** Events use `{event_id, at, operation, payload}`. Derived from the clock: `inventory.released`, `cart.hold_expired`, `reservation.no_show_released`, `reservation.completed`, `refund.posted`. Set by the task scenario: `inventory.claimed`, `alert.posted|updated|expired`, `closure.ordered`, `forecast.issued`, `reservation.checked_in`, `clock.sync`. Published: the Mail and Calendar items above, in #12's formats.
- **Shared data and dependencies (required):** Every record type has a stable ID, including holds, saved equipment and companions. No attachments. Needs the shared Mail and Calendar apps and the platform clock.
