## 2. What you plan to build

**✍️ Your answer (required):** Switchback is a resettable campground reservation site with holds, a fee ladder and clock-driven closures, where a booking can check out cleanly and still be wrong for the people going.

The 15-minute cart hold and $10 change fee follow Recreation.gov's public pages. The release schedule, refund ladder, cutoffs and alert effects are Switchback's own simplified rules. When this was proposed (2026-11-16), Weather & outdoors had no environment or task proposal and, by the author's reading of the public PersonalAgentEval case list, no v1 task. Since then #123 (Rainshadow) and #126 (Gustline) have proposed general weather Apps, and Task #113 plans to run on Switchback; see the overlap notes in section 4.

Workflows 1 to 4 are the acceptance baseline. Every rule is printed on the policy page and on the preview it affects. Times are facility time at a fixed UTC offset.

1. **Search and site detail.** Each site page lists electric hookup and amperage, maximum vehicle length, ADA access, walk-in distance, occupancy and pets; the matching filters are off by default. The booking form checks only equipment length, party size and pets. Night states: `unreleased`, `open`, `held`, `booked`, `closed`. Nights release 180 days ahead at 07:00. An attribute missing from the seed is shown as `unknown` and never matches a filter.
2. **Reservation lifecycle.** `cart.hold` holds a site for 15 simulated minutes; then preview, confirm and receipt. The preview shows the primary occupant (account holder or saved companion, by ID), the pre-filled saved equipment and companions, the price, the ladder dates and the paying card with its cardholder and expiry. Confirm copies equipment and companion values into the reservation. A preview is single-use: the same `request_id` returns the same receipt, a new one `preview_consumed`. States: `confirmed -> checked_in -> completed`, with exits `cancelled`, `no_show` and `closure_cancelled`. A scheduled competing claim takes a night only if it is `open`.
3. **Modify, cancel and no-show.** Refund rungs change at 00:00 on the 14th and 4th day before arrival and at 15:00 on arrival day. In order, a cancel refunds everything; all but $10; all but $10 and the first night; nothing. Refunds never go below zero and return to the original card three days later. The cancel preview names the rung, refund, card and next rung change. Date or site changes cost $10, are allowed only in the first two rungs of the original arrival date, and release the original nights at confirm, which cannot be undone. The modify preview holds the new nights and shows the price difference and card; its confirm re-runs workflow 2's checks. Rung boundaries, refunds (fixed or a percentage), the change fee and the change window are a per-facility policy record in the seed; the ladder above is the default record, and #113's terms (free changes and cancellations until 18:00 three days before arrival, then 50%) are another. Without check-in the site is released at 21:00 on arrival day, or 12:00 the next day after a late-arrival notice filed before 21:00, and the stay becomes `no_show` with no refund.
4. **Conditions and alerts.** Scenario alerts cover fire-restriction stage, road or trail restriction, water or electrical outage and smoke advisory. Each names a facility, optional sites and a window, and can be updated or cancelled. An alert is active from its start until its end or cancellation, computed from `virtual_now`; while active it marks each reservation whose site and any night fall inside as affected, without changing its state or ladder, and an active electrical outage removes its sites from the electric filter. Both effects end with the alert. A closure covering any night cancels the whole stay (`closure_cancelled`) with a full refund to the original card; its nights reopen when the window ends. Forecasts are left to a weather App (#123 or #126).

Errors. `cart.hold`: `not_yet_released`, `site_taken`, `site_closed`. Previews: `hold_expired`, `equipment_exceeds_site`, `occupancy_exceeded`, `pets_not_allowed`, `outside_change_window`. Confirms: `preview_stale`, `preview_consumed`, `payment_declined` (expired card), `site_closed`. Cancel: `already_cancelled` (also for `closure_cancelled`), `already_checked_in`. Late arrival: `notice_too_late`. Any write: Core's `stale_version`; a tool outside the run's policy profile: Core's `permission_denied`.

Optional, outside the baseline: (A) a permit lottery in which naming one person on two applications voids both at the draw, as the policy page and application preview state; (B) passes and discounts; (C) a revocable trip-plan share link.

Value for personal-agent tasks: the routine path returns no error or warning when a booking does not fit the people going, so the mismatch shows only in the end state, where a check can read it. No form asks about power, step-free access or carry distance. Stale saved equipment, stale companions and another person's default card check out cleanly. Date changes cannot be undone, and holds, rungs and the notice cutoff are deadlines on the simulated clock. The facts that decide whether a booking fits come from Mail, Calendar or the conversation; Switchback holds only saved records, which may be stale.

Downstream tasks it is meant to host:

- Rebook a family's lake weekend "same as always" after one relative began needing overnight power and another bought a longer trailer, with a relative's card still the default.
- Go or no-go on a booked trip, with forecasts from #123 or #126: wait for the next issuance, then act before the refund rung changes. Task #113 (a smoke advisory and a companion's asthma, under its own change terms) already plans to run here.
- Notices arrive between sessions: a closure needs no action; an electrical outage removes the reason the site was chosen.
- A delayed drive on arrival day: file the late-arrival notice for the right stay, not a cancel in the no-refund rung.

## 3. Potential app connections

- **Internal personal apps (required):** Mail and Calendar, both optional
  - Mail 1.3.0 (optional; workflows 2-4 send to it): Switchback publishes confirmation, refund, closure and alert notices on Mail's `channel.email` receive contract (wire schema `urn:pale:email-message:2`, payload `urn:pale:email-payload:1`, type `org.pale.mail.notification.v1`, receiver operation `mail.receive_notification`; `examples/apps/mail/contribution/contribution.json`). Each is a `text/plain` body with the reservation ID, dates and amount on labelled lines and no assets. Workflows 1-4 complete without Mail: the notices stay in Switchback's outgoing records.
  - Calendar 1.3.0 (optional; no workflow needs it): Calendar has no messaging receiver, so Switchback publishes nothing to it. The Agent reads trips with `calendar.list` and `calendar.get` and, if a task asks, records a stay with `calendar.create`; a scenario can seed stays with Calendar's own `calendar.upsert` event.
  - Files, Contacts and Messages are not needed.
- **Other apps and websites (required):** None in the baseline

### Integration details

- **Operations and access (required):** Agent-facing operations are MCP tools: `facilities.search|get`, `sites.search|get`, `policy.get`, `cart.hold|release`, `reservations.preview|confirm|list|get|late_arrival`, `reservations.modify.preview|confirm`, `reservations.cancel.preview|confirm`, `alerts.list`, `account.get`, `wallet.list|set_default`, `equipment.save`, `companions.save`. All tools except `search`, `get` and `list` are writes, previews included, and take `expected_version` and `request_id`. Permissions use Core's per-profile `allow` lists, fixed when state is created, loaded or reset: a `browse` profile leaves out the three confirms and `wallet.set_default`, a `full` profile allows them. Switchback keeps no approval state; a user's approval is captured in the conversation or the task harness, which picks the profile. Pages are declared with the optional `pale.web` extension and served by the public business Web Host over the Agent MCP connection; whether a run gives the Agent that GUI is run configuration. Infra operations are Core's: `env.load_seed`, `env.apply_event`, `env.reset`, `env.snapshot`, `env.event_status`, `env.validate_bundle` and `env.advance_time`.
- **State and lifecycle (required):** App state (catalogue, facility policy records, inventory), account state (wallet, saved records, reservations with their revisions, receipts, outgoing notices) and run state (holds, previews) are separate; the event schedule stays with the task. Time follows the environment clock: Switchback has no scheduler or clock event, and hold expiry, rung changes, no-show release, refund posting and alert activity are computed from stored due times and `ctx.virtual_now` at each read and write. Reads compute them without storing anything; the next write or event commits them, and a notice that falls due (a refund posted three days after a cancel, a no-show release) is created then, so it reaches Mail at the first event or write after its deadline. A preview is void once its hold expires or a rung changes (`preview_stale`). A stale `expected_version` is Core's `stale_version` and changes nothing. Evaluator records come from three sources: Core's `state.audit` (successful reads and rejected calls, exported with `env.snapshot` before each reset, since reset clears it), the supplied Agent execution records (tool arguments and results, such as search filters) and Switchback's own committed history. One logical attempt is one `request_id`: a transport retry or a successful idempotent replay of it counts once (a replay adds no audit row), and repeated rejected calls with one `request_id` are collapsed. `env.reset` restores only this App instance (`reset_scope: app_instance`) to the loaded seed, seeded reservations included; records already delivered to Mail stay until Mail's own reset.
- **Events and notifications (required):** Accepted through `env.apply_event` as `{event_id, at, operation, payload}`; an event that names an unknown ID or does not apply is rejected with `invalid_event` and changes nothing. One example each:
  - `inventory.claimed` `{event_id: "ev-03", at: "2027-05-02T07:00:20-07:00", payload: {site_id: "cf-B14", nights: ["2027-06-18", "2027-06-19"]}}`: a competing booker takes each listed night that is `open`; `held` or `booked` nights are skipped and listed in the receipt.
  - `alert.posted` `{event_id: "ev-11", at: "2027-06-10T09:00:00-07:00", payload: {alert_id: "al-7", kind: "electrical_outage", facility_id: "cedar-flat", site_ids: ["cf-B10", "cf-B14"], from: "2027-06-17T12:00:00-07:00", to: "2027-06-20T12:00:00-07:00", text}}`: affected reservations and the electric filter follow workflow 4 while it is active; each affected holder gets a notice.
  - `alert.updated` `{event_id: "ev-12", at: "2027-06-12T09:00:00-07:00", payload: {alert_id: "al-7", to: "2027-06-18T18:00:00-07:00"}}`: replaces any of kind, sites, window and text; affected marks and the filter are recomputed from the new values, so a stay no longer covered stops being affected and its site returns to the filter; holders whose status changed get a notice.
  - `alert.cancelled` `{event_id: "ev-13", at: "2027-06-17T08:00:00-07:00", payload: {alert_id: "al-7"}}`: ends the alert at `at`; affected marks clear, sites return to the filter, and affected holders get a "lifted" notice. Expiry needs no event: the alert stops being active at `to` and sends nothing.
  - `closure.ordered` `{event_id: "ev-17", at: "2027-06-11T06:00:00-07:00", payload: {closure_id: "cl-31", facility_id: "cedar-flat", site_ids: [], from: "2027-06-15", to: "2027-06-22", reason: "road washout"}}`: workflow 4's closure; an empty `site_ids` means the whole facility.
  - `reservation.checked_in` `{event_id: "ev-20", at: "2027-06-18T16:10:00-07:00", payload: {reservation_id: "res-0207"}}`: `confirmed` to `checked_in`; rejected before arrival day or in any other state.

  Published: Mail notices on `channel.email`, declared in Switchback's `messaging.publishes` with a pinned copy of Mail 1.3.0's wire schema. Each committed write or event that creates a notice stores it in Switchback's outgoing records in the same commit; the run's host-side Messaging caller reads new ones from the result or the next `env.snapshot` and calls `router.publish`, using the notice ID as the CloudEvents `id`. Switchback does not record delivery. Committed receipt: one message in the recipient's Mail. Rejected: Mail unchanged and Switchback keeps its business result and notice (no rollback); that notice's integration check fails and business checks still pass. Retry with the same `id`: Messaging deduplicates, still one Mail message. Unknown acknowledgement: the run stops for reconciliation and is not scored. The closure notice for the event above:

   "datacontenttype": "application/json", "dataschema": "urn:pale:email-payload:1",
   "data": {"channel": "channel.email", "recipient": {"kind": "person_ref", "id": "person-alex"},
    "source_object": {"app_id": "switchback", "account_id": "sb-alex", "object_type": "reservation", "object_id": "res-0193"},

- **Shared data and dependencies (required):** Every record type has a stable ID, including holds, saved equipment and companions; the account holder is bound to the platform's shared person and Mail and Calendar accounts as in #12's `binding.json`, and a companion or cardholder carries a shared person ID only where the scenario binds one. No attachments.
