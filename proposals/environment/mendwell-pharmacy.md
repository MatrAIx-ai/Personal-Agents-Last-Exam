## 2. What you plan to build

**✍️ Your answer (required):** A resettable retail pharmacy (one fictional chain, three stores) where a household's prescriptions are requested, priced through the patient's drug plan, held for pharmacist review, collected in store, cancelled or returned to stock. The App records facts, statuses, pharmacist notes and consequences; it does not make clinical decisions, rank options or say what the right action is. No third-party code, brand assets or real patient data.

**Simulated policies and simplifications.** Time follows the environment clock (`ctx.virtual_now`), which Infra keeps in step across the enabled Apps; all stores share one time zone (America/New_York), used for opening hours and the 23:59 cut-off; money is USD in integer cents.

- A prescription is valid until 23:59 on its written date + 365 days. A fill is made when it first becomes `ready`, and every transition to `ready` checks validity and holds. If the prescription expires or is superseded before the fill is made, the fill is cancelled (reason `rx_expired` or `rx_superseded`) and any paid claim is reversed; a fill made before expiry can be collected afterwards.
- Review completes 2 hours after the request, or 2 hours after the next opening if the pharmacy is closed. A ready fill gets a warning 7 days (168 hours) and returns to stock 10 days (240 hours) after it became ready, counted as elapsed unheld time; a hold pauses both, and they resume with the remaining time.
- Collection by anyone other than the patient or a guardian needs an in-app pickup grant. This is stricter than usual US practice, where pharmacists may use judgment, so that authorization is observable. A date of birth identifies a record but not the person at the counter, so collection is a scenario event checked by the counter.
- Stock is per-store availability, not quantities. Claims belong to the chain, so changing store keeps the claim. Any allowed quantity uses one whole fill. `not_covered` has no formulary-exception path.

**Workflows.**

1. **Household grants.** The patient, or a guardian of a minor, grants or revokes `view`, `refill` or `pickup` access with an optional expiry, after a user confirmation; only a `pickup` grant may be limited to one fill. Changes made by other household members outside the Agent arrive as `grant.changed` events.
2. **Prescriptions, fills and renewals.** One active fill per prescription. With no fills left or an expired prescription, the Agent can request or withdraw a renewal; the prescriber's reply arrives as an event, and approval supersedes the old prescription.
3. **Plan claims and recovery.** A claim is paid or rejected (`refill_too_soon` with an eligible date, `prior_auth_required`, `not_covered`, `quantity_limit`). Recovery: wait for the eligible date, ask the pharmacy to request an early-refill override or prior authorization from the prescriber and plan, change to an allowed quantity, pay cash, or cancel.
4. **Pharmacist holds.** A scenario places or releases a hold with a note (for example an interaction flag). A hold pauses review, resubmission, restock-to-ready and the pickup warning and return timers; at release, review, warning and return resume with their remaining time, and a resubmission whose eligible date passed during the hold runs once if the fill still qualifies; prescription expiry and supersession still cancel unmade fills at their effective time. Collection attempts while held are refused (`on_hold`) and never queued. While held, `fills.change_quantity`, `fills.change_store` and the cash and prepay previews return `on_hold`; placing or releasing a hold changes the fill's version, so a pre-hold cash token returns `stale_preview` for an authorized caller, and a pre-hold prepay token does so once a matching, unused card-owner approval passes; other calls behave as usual.
5. **Collection, payment, cancellation and return to stock.** Covered by the table and rules below.

| From | Trigger | To |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| — | `fills.request` (refill authority; Rx valid; fills remaining ≥ 1; no active fill; allowed quantity) | `requested` |
| `requested` | review completes → claim paid, stock / no stock / rejected | `ready` / `waiting_stock` / `claim_rejected` |
| `claim_rejected` | `fills.wait` (`refill_too_soon`) → claim resubmitted when due, subject to the hold rule | `ready`, `waiting_stock` or `claim_rejected` |
| `claim_rejected` | `plan_requests.create` (`refill_too_soon` or `prior_auth_required`) | `pending_plan_decision` |
| `pending_plan_decision` | `plan.decided` approved → claim resubmitted | `ready`, `waiting_stock` or `claim_rejected` |
| `pending_plan_decision` | `plan.decided` denied, or `plan_requests.withdraw` | `claim_rejected` |
| `claim_rejected` | `fills.change_quantity` (`quantity_limit`) → claim resubmitted | `ready`, `waiting_stock` or `claim_rejected` |
| `claim_rejected` | `fills.choose_cash.confirm` | `ready` or `waiting_stock` |
| `waiting_stock` | `store.restocked` for this store and drug, or `fills.change_store` to a store with stock | `ready` |
| `ready` | `fill.collection_attempted` passing every check | `collected` |
| `ready` | 10 days unclaimed (hold time excluded) | `returned_to_stock` |
| any non-terminal | `fills.cancel.confirm`, or expiry or supersession before the fill is made | `cancelled` |

Rules:

- A scheduled resubmission is due at the later of 00:00 local time on the eligible date and the time of `fills.wait`; one already due when it is scheduled runs once in the same write, after `fills.wait`; a hold defers it until release, when it runs only if the fill still qualifies. It is consumed when it starts and cleared whenever the fill leaves `claim_rejected`; if the claim is rejected again, no further automatic attempt is made. `fills.change_quantity` is also allowed in `requested`; after authority checks, `fills.change_store` returns `on_hold` on a held non-terminal fill and `invalid_state` on an unheld `ready` fill or a terminal fill, and is otherwise allowed in `requested`, `waiting_stock`, `claim_rejected` and `pending_plan_decision`, keeping the claim; in `pending_plan_decision`, `fills.change_quantity` returns `request_pending` (`on_hold` first if the fill is held). A second pending renewal returns `duplicate_request`.
- The amount due is fixed when a fill becomes ready. Prepay is allowed only on a ready, unheld fill with a positive amount due and no live authorization, by a card owner who can see that fill; it authorizes exactly the amount due on that card.
- `fills.set_collector` records the intended collector, who must be the patient, a guardian or hold a current pickup grant for the fill (otherwise `not_authorized`). The record is informational and is cleared when that grant ends; the counter checks authority, not the record.
- Collection checks, in this order, that the fill is unheld (`on_hold`) and ready (`not_ready`), the store is open (`store_closed`), the collector is the patient, a guardian or holds a current pickup grant (`collector_not_authorized`), and payment succeeds (`payment_failed`); the first failure appends that refusal and the eligible `notice.refused` entries, and the refusal itself changes no status or finances (a timer due by then, such as a return at its deadline, is applied first and committed with the refusal). For a positive amount due, a live authorization is captured and `card_id` is ignored; with no live authorization, the collector's own card named in the event is charged. A failed capture, or a missing, unowned or declined card on the charge path, ends the attempt with `payment_failed` and no fallback, and a failed capture leaves the authorization live for a later attempt; zero due skips payment. Success uses one fill and ends the fill in one step, with at most one capture or charge.
- Cancellation and return to stock reverse a paid claim and void any live authorization; cancellation also withdraws a pending plan request. Fills remaining is unchanged.
- Terminal fills stay readable; once authority is established, an ungated write or a new preview returns `invalid_state`; a confirm follows the check order in §3, so it can stop earlier at authority, approval or freshness.

**Value for personal-agent tasks.** Acting for a family on medicines depends on whose prescription it is, what each person allowed, what the plan returned and what a pharmacist flagged. The App exposes reusable behavior, not one task's answer. Examples (separate Task proposals): collect an elderly parent's medicines on her preferred day under her grant, paying with the caregiver's own card; notice a pharmacist interaction note on her new antibiotic and raise it instead of only arranging collection.

**Boundary with #14 (proposed).** If both are built, the Northlake Patient Portal (#14) issues prescriptions and renewal decisions, and Mendwell owns fills, holds, claims, payment and collection; any connection is optional and would be agreed with that author; in v1, prescriptions and renewal replies arrive as task-supplied `rx.received` and `renewal.replied` events. #14's first version excludes household proxies, so household grants live here. A link to Larkfield Health Plan (#64, same author), mirroring prior-authorization requests and decisions between the two Apps, is left for a later version; this version takes plan decisions only as task-supplied `plan.decided` events. Proposal list checked on 2026-11-19: no other pharmacy proposal; related work is #14, #64, #85 (a family chat that proposes a `channel.chat` receive contract and names Mendwell as an optional sender; this version sends to Mail only) and #101 (parcel delivery, which lists Mendwell as related; delivery is excluded here).

### Integration details

- **Operations and access (required):** 25 Agent-facing MCP operations. Names below omit the App prefix; `tools.json` registers each as `mendwell-pharmacy.<name>` (for example `mendwell-pharmacy.fills.request`), because Core accepts only tool names that start with the App ID. Preview, read-only (4): `grants.change.preview`, `fills.choose_cash.preview`, `fills.prepay.preview`, `fills.cancel.preview`. Write (13): `grants.change.confirm`, `fills.request`, `fills.change_quantity`, `fills.change_store`, `fills.wait`, `fills.choose_cash.confirm`, `fills.set_collector`, `fills.prepay.confirm`, `fills.cancel.confirm`, `plan_requests.create`, `plan_requests.withdraw`, `renewals.create`, `renewals.withdraw`. The Agent acts as the bound account holder set by Infra, never through an argument.

  | Authority | Allows |
  | :--- | :--- |
  | Patient, or guardian of a minor | Everything for that patient; grant changes with a user confirmation |
  | `view` grant (patient-wide) | Prescriptions, fills, notes, claims and payments of that patient |
  | `refill` grant (patient-wide, includes `view`) | Requesting, changing, waiting, cash, plan requests, renewals, cancellation, naming a collector |
  | `pickup` grant (patient-wide or one fill) | Logistics view of the covered fills only (store, status, amount due, dates); being named collector and collecting |
  | Card owner who can see the fill | Prepaying it with their own card (a guardian may prepay a minor's fill) |

  Every delegated read, write and notice must match the grant's patient, scope, optional fill and expiry at that moment.

  Previews store nothing. `confirmation_invalid` means the approval is expired, already used, or from someone other than the required confirmer; restated arguments or a target version that no longer reproduce the token return `stale_preview`. A preview runs the same authority and state checks as its confirm and returns that error instead of a token; otherwise the token is a digest of the actor, the action (grant, revoke, prepay, cash or cancel), its full arguments (patient, grantee, scope, fill, expiry, card, amount), the run ID, the loaded seed's `seed_id` and the target object's own version (an App-maintained counter that changes only when that fill or grant changes, so unrelated writes and the approval event itself do not invalidate it; for a new grant, which has no version yet, the counter of the patient's grant set, which changes whenever any grant of that patient changes). The confirm carries the token, restates the arguments and Core's current `expected_version` like any write; the App finds the approval by the token and compares it with a digest recomputed from current state. Approvals are stored in App data, so a reset discards them. `grants.change.confirm` and `fills.prepay.confirm` also need a `user.confirmed` event naming that token from the person who must approve (the grantor, or the card owner); it is single-use, expires after 30 simulated minutes and is consumed only by a successful commit. Confirm calls check authority, then the approval, then preview freshness, then state, and return the first failure. `fills.choose_cash.confirm` and `fills.cancel.confirm` are Agent confirmations of a current preview, without a user approval. Their previews also list the business changes the confirm would commit, and the token digest covers them: for cash, the price, store, resulting fill status and any cleared resubmission; for cancellation, the claim reversal, authorization void, plan-request withdrawal and cancelled timers. The confirm recomputes those changes, returns `stale_preview` if they differ, and otherwise commits them. Confirmation status is visible in `grants.list` and `fills.get`. Infra-only operations: `env.load_seed`, `env.apply_event`, reset and snapshot, plus the host's `env.advance_time` on runtime 1.6.0; Core returns `permission_denied` when the Agent calls them. App errors: `not_authorized`, `confirmation_required`, `confirmation_invalid`, `stale_preview`, `duplicate_request`, `no_fills_remaining`, `rx_expired`, `rx_superseded`, `quantity_not_allowed`, `store_unavailable`, `request_pending`, `request_not_pending`, `on_hold`, `payment_failed`, `invalid_state`, `not_found`; Core adds `idempotency_conflict`, `stale_version`, `permission_denied`, `invalid_arguments` for input that fails a tool or event schema, and `invalid_event` for an event it cannot apply, such as a fresh one dated before the App's current time.
- **State and lifecycle (required):** State is per run and account, loaded by `env.load_seed` and validated (unique IDs, grants consistent with patients and guardians, one active fill per prescription). Each distinct seed has a required, unique `seed_id`, which preview tokens include; a token whose seed ID differs from the loaded seed's fails the freshness check, and reset restores the loaded seed ID. Within an epoch, Core returns the stored receipt (marked duplicate) for an exact repeat of a committed request or event, and `idempotency_conflict` for the same ID with changed input; a new write or event with an old epoch or revision gets `stale_version`, and a fresh retry of a competing fill request then sees `duplicate_request`. A stale preview is rejected with no side effects. Collection refusals are committed events that append a refusal record and eligible notices; a failed Agent call preserves business data, version and notices, and Core appends a denial audit entry; a late or ignored event skips its own action but is still committed: Core increments the revision, sets the virtual time to the event's `at` (which may equal the current time) and stores the event receipt and audit row, and timers due by then are applied before its action, committed with it and may change records. Reset is Core's new epoch: Core restores the loaded seed and the virtual time loaded with it (the task's `virtual_now` in `env.load_seed`) and clears receipts and prior audit rows, recording the reset; seeded records and their scheduled work return to their loaded values, and approvals and work created during the run disappear (seeds contain no approvals); an event whose ID is unseen in the current epoch gets `stale_version` when sent with an earlier epoch's version (an exact replay returns its stored receipt first). Reset covers this App instance only; notices already delivered to Mail stay there.
- **Events and notifications (required):** Events use Core's envelope via `env.apply_event`: `rx.received`, `renewal.replied`, `plan.decided`, `grant.changed`, `fill.hold_placed`, `fill.hold_released`, `store.restocked`, `fill.collection_attempted` (collector, and card when paying at the counter), `user.confirmed`. Time follows the environment clock, `ctx.virtual_now`: Core sets it at seed load and reset, Infra moves it forward with `env.advance_time` (which calls no App business handler), and an event's `at` sets it while that event is applied. Mendwell defines no clock event and keeps no separate scheduler: each timer (review completion, a scheduled resubmission, the pickup warning, return to stock, and prescription expiry for unmade fills) is a due time stored on its record; a hold replaces the due times of review, the warning and the return with their remaining durations, defers a due resubmission to release (where it runs once if the fill still qualifies) and does not pause prescription expiry, which still cancels an unmade fill at its 23:59 cutoff. Grant and approval expiry are comparisons with `ctx.virtual_now`; an ended pickup grant clears its intended-collector record as of its expiry. When Core invokes a write or event handler, Mendwell first applies all timers due at or before `ctx.virtual_now`, in a stable order (due time, then the affected fill's ID, then timer kind, with prescription expiry first for that fill), including timers that those timers start, and then applies the action; Core commits the timer effects and the action together. A timer and an action at the same instant resolve in the timer's favour. If the call then fails, through an App error or a Core rejection, no business data, version or notice is stored (Core keeps only its denial audit row): the timer effects roll back with it, and the next fresh write or event that succeeds persists them. An exact replay of a committed request returns its stored receipt without reaching the handler, so it persists nothing new. Reads and previews compute the same timer effects in memory and report the resulting statuses, amounts, claims and dates without storing them. A seed whose timers are already due at its `virtual_now` supplies its records, including the grants in force, as of immediately before the earliest of those timers, and is caught up from there in the same way. A timer takes effect as of its due time: the fill's timeline, any claim reversal and any notice it causes carry that time, and the notice is written when the timer is committed, so the committed timer effects match what reads projected at that instant, before the action's own effects. For a schema-valid event with an unseen ID and the current version, Core rejects an `at` earlier than the App's current time as `invalid_event`. A `plan.decided` for a held fill is stored (the first per request; later ones are ignored) and applied once at release only if its request is still pending; `store.restocked` makes the drug available at once, and a held waiting fill rechecks stock at release. Late events skip their own action: `plan.decided` applies only to a pending request linked to the current claim attempt, `renewal.replied` only to a pending renewal, and `store.restocked` moves only waiting fills. Notices: `notice.fill_ready`, `notice.return_warning`, `notice.returned`, `notice.refused`, `notice.held`, `notice.released`, `notice.claim_rejected`, `notice.plan_decision`, `notice.renewal_reply`, `notice.payment_voided`. Recipients are computed from the grants in force at the notice's time (for a timer notice, its due time: an expiry is compared with the due time, and no later grant change has been applied yet, because every write and event first applies due timers): the patient, guardians and `view` holders get all notices; `pickup`-only holders get only the four logistics notices (ready, return warning, returned, refused) with store, fill ID and amount due; `notice.payment_voided` also goes to the card owner, showing only their masked card, amount and void status; a person who qualifies twice gets one entry. Each notice is one log entry per recipient; seeded notice entries are history, and the adapter publishes only entries appended in the current epoch. An Infra-run sender adapter (package code) reads the log through `env.snapshot`, rechecks each recipient's access at dispatch and calls `router.publish` for recipients who have a Mail account bound in the run and an Infra Messaging grant for `channel.email`, using one router session, with its own grant, per recipient Mail account, because a session binds one receiving account; other entries stay in the log. A delivery rejected before dispatch (for example `incompatible_contract`) creates no Mail record; a rejection by Mail or an unknown acknowledgement halts the router for reconciliation; none of these rolls back Mendwell. Example notice as a full wire message (`notice.fill_ready` to a pickup-grant holder; it validates against Mail's `urn:pale:email-message:2` schema at `61b6371`): `{"specversion":"1.0","id":"e2-n-9","source":"urn:pale:app:mendwell-pharmacy:account:mendwell-p-2","type":"org.pale.mail.notification.v1","time":"2026-12-06T08:00:00-05:00","datacontenttype":"application/json","dataschema":"urn:pale:email-payload:1","data":{"channel":"channel.email","recipient":{"kind":"person_ref","id":"p-2"},"source_object":{"app_id":"mendwell-pharmacy","account_id":"mendwell-p-2","object_type":"fill","object_id":"f-15"},"content":{"title":"Fill ready for pickup","body":{"format":"text/plain","text":"Fill f-15 is ready at store st-2. Local business tests (workflow in brackets). The Core receipt, version, Infra-authority, clock and reset assertions in T02, T13, T21 and T22, marked or implied by [MCP], run in the package's real-MCP script on the public runtime, because `env.advance_time`, `env.snapshot`, receipts and versions are Core's; Mail delivery is checked through the receiving instance:

- T01 [W2, W5] `rx.received` → request → reviewed after 2 hours → ready → collected; fills −1 once.
- T02 [W2] Request while a fill is active → `duplicate_request`; concurrent stale write → `stale_version`, retry → `duplicate_request`.
- T03 [W2] No fills → `no_fills_remaining`; expired → `rx_expired`; renewal approved; old Rx → `rx_superseded`; new fill collected.
- T04 [W2] Renewal denied; second pending renewal → `duplicate_request`; reply after withdrawal ignored; withdrawing again → `request_not_pending`.
- T05 [W3] `refill_too_soon` → wait → resubmitted at the eligible date → ready; rejected again → no further automatic attempt; choosing cash first clears the resubmission.
- T06 [W3] Early-refill override approved → ready.
- T07 [W3] Prior authorization denied → cash → collected at the cash price.
- T08 [W3] `not_covered` → `plan_requests.create` → `invalid_state`; cash or cancel only.
- T09 [W3] `quantity_limit` → allowed quantity → paid; disallowed → `quantity_not_allowed`; change while a plan request is pending → `request_pending`.
- T10 [W3, W4] Request withdrawn then decided → ignored; decision during a hold, withdrawal, release → no effect.
- T11 [W3] Paid without stock → restock → ready; store change with stock → ready, claim kept; without → `store_unavailable`; expiry while waiting → `cancelled`, claim reversed.
- T12 [W5] Cancel from each non-terminal state: whichever exist of the paid claim, live authorization, pending plan request and scheduled resubmission are reversed, voided, withdrawn or cleared.
- T13 [W5] Warning on day 7 and return on day 10, each committed by the next fresh successful write or event and dated at its due time, with the claim reversed and fills unchanged; a collection at exactly the return deadline → the return applies first and the collection is refused `not_ready`; [MCP] for a requested fill whose claim pays and whose store has stock, with no business event: `env.advance_time` past its review time → `fills.get` and `fills.list` report `ready`, and a cancel preview returns a token and lists the reversal of the paid claim; `env.advance_time` past its return deadline → they report `returned_to_stock` with the claim reversed, a new cancel preview returns `invalid_state`, and a cancel confirm with the earlier token, a fresh request ID and the current `expected_version` returns `stale_preview` with one denial audit row; throughout, `env.snapshot` still shows the stored `requested` fill and no new notice; the next fresh successful write (for example `fills.request` on another prescription) commits the review, the warning, the return, the reversal and their notices once, each dated at its due time.
- T14 [W4] Hold defers review; collection while held → `on_hold`, not replayed; a prepay preview while held → `on_hold`, and a prepay confirm with a pre-hold token and a valid approval → `stale_preview`; a decision stored during the hold applies at release only if still pending; timers resume with remaining time.
- T15 [W1, W5] Caregiver collects under a pickup grant and pays with her own card.
- T16 [W1, W5] Prepay with the parent's card → `not_authorized`; declined card → `payment_failed`; a second prepay preview with a live authorization → `invalid_state`; a prepay preview on a zero-due fill → `invalid_state`; a card owner who can see only fill A prepays fill B with their own card → `not_authorized`.
- T17 [W1] Pickup grant limited to fill A: logistics view of A only; fill B and the prescription list → `not_authorized`; logistics notices for A only.
- T18 [W1] Revocation by `grant.changed` before collection → refusal `collector_not_authorized`; unrelated adult granting → `not_authorized`; guardian acting for a minor succeeds.
- T19 [W5] A live authorization is captured and a counter card ignored; a failed capture with a live authorization and a valid counter card → refusal `payment_failed`, the same authorization stays live and no counter charge is made; counter payment declined → refusal `payment_failed`, finances unchanged; outside hours → `store_closed`; non-ready fill → `not_ready`; zero due collects without payment.
- T20 [W1, W5] Grant and prepay each succeed with a matching approval; confirm without approval → `confirmation_required`; expired, reused or from the wrong person → `confirmation_invalid`; changed arguments or target → `stale_preview`.
- T21 [all] Repeated request ID → stored receipt; same ID with a changed payload → `idempotency_conflict`; unknown fill → `not_found`; Agent calling `env.apply_event` or `env.reset` → `permission_denied`.
- T22 [all] Repeated event ID → stored receipt, no second effect; a seed loaded after a fill's review time → reads report the reviewed fill, and the first fresh successful write persists the review once; a write with a pre-reset version → `stale_version`; terminal fills readable; a new cancel preview → `invalid_state`, and a cancel confirm with a pre-terminal token → `stale_preview`; reset restores the loaded seed and its virtual time and leaves Mail records unchanged; a cancel token previewed under a different seed → `stale_preview`; a cancel preview on a held `prior_auth_required` fill, then a reset to the same seed and a plan request that brings the fill back to the previewed version, then a confirm with the old token and the current Core version → `stale_preview`, and the plan request stays pending; a cash token reused after a restock changes its resulting fill status → `stale_preview`.

Each test also asserts the notice-log entries its transitions produce.
