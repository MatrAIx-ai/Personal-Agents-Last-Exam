## 2. What you plan to build

**✍️ Your answer (required):** A resettable grocery delivery marketplace with multi-store catalogs, package yields, allergen footnotes, fee thresholds, replacement rules and live shopper substitutions.

FreshCart is an independently branded synthetic grocery-delivery platform whose interaction patterns follow public grocery-delivery help pages (Instacart and Amazon Fresh). No third-party code, brand assets, trademarks or scraped store listings are used. Delivery slots, stock changes and shopper substitution holds advance on the simulated clock.

Initial workflows cover four complete loops end to end, including previews, confirmation, modifications, cancellations and realistic errors:

1. **Account, membership and store delivery windows.** Inspect saved delivery addresses, masked payment methods, membership status (`FreshPass` vs standard tier), fee schedule (`$0` delivery when pre-tax, post-promo `merchandise_subtotal >= $50.00`, `$7.99` delivery fee below `$50.00`, small-basket fee below `$20.00`, and percentage service fee), and account-level default replacement setting (`shopper_best_match` until changed). Browse stores for operating hours, markup policy (`in_store_prices` vs `markup`) and 1-hour delivery windows with capacity and priority-window fees.
2. **Catalog search, product detail and Buy-It-Again history.** Search and filter by keywords, aisle, brand, price and top-level dietary tags (`Vegan`, `Gluten-Free`, `Dairy-Free`, `Organic`). Search cards intentionally return thin summaries (title, item price, short unit label such as `"1 can"`, rating, `sponsored` flag and top-level tags), whereas opening an item detail page exposes full specifications: exact metric weight/volume and culinary yield (for example `"400 ml can"` or `"115 g jar (approx. 7 tbsp)"`), unit price (`$/100 g`), Nutrition Facts (calories, macros, sodium, added sugars), complete ingredient statement and facility cross-contact footnotes (for example `"processed on equipment shared with tree nuts"`, `"contains shrimp paste"`, or `"new recipe"`), live stock status (`in_stock`, `low_stock`, `out_of_stock`), and prior order count. `Buy It Again` lists all previously ordered SKUs with purchase counts without separating weekly staples from one-off buys.
3. **Cart, per-item replacement rules and checkout preview/confirm.** Add, update and remove cart items, configure per-item replacement instructions (`shopper_best_match`, `specific_backup` with a chosen `backup_sku_id`, or `refund_item`), and apply promo codes. Checkout uses `orders.preview` followed by idempotent `orders.confirm`: `preview` places a short hold on the selected delivery slot and itemizes cent-level math separating pre-tax, post-promo `merchandise_subtotal` (which governs the `$50.00` free-delivery threshold) from tax-and-fee-inclusive `estimated_total`. Errors include `slot_full`, `below_store_minimum`, `promo_ineligible`, `promo_expired`, `backup_sku_invalid`, `backup_sku_out_of_stock`, `preview_expired` and `price_or_stock_changed`.
4. **Live fulfillment, shopper substitutions and post-delivery issues.** Orders transition on the clock through `scheduled → shopping → action_required → out_for_delivery → delivered`, or `cancelled` (free cancellation before `shopping`; fee preview once shopping begins). While `scheduled`, items, quantities, replacement instructions and delivery windows can be modified (`orders.modify`). During `shopping`, out-of-stock events apply each item's configured rule: items left on `shopper_best_match` generate a concrete proposed substitute SKU (which can introduce an allergen/dietary mismatch, a smaller pack size or a lower price that drops `merchandise_subtotal` below `$50.00` while `estimated_total` remains over `$50.00`) and open a timed review window (`orders.respond_substitution`: `approve`, `swap_sku` or `refund`) before dispatch. Delivered orders provide itemized receipts and a `7-day` self-serve refund claim flow (`orders.report_issue`) for wrong or missing items.

Value for personal-agent tasks: everyday grocery restocking is where a routine checkout can succeed cleanly and still be wrong for the household. Usual staples are implicit in months of receipts; dinner-party cooking requires scaling recipe units onto package yields minus what is already in the kitchen; top-level search cards and `"Vegan"` badges hide ingredient/cross-contact footnotes and pre-tax fee thresholds; and shopper substitutions during fulfillment change both allergen safety and delivery fees. This environment powers the linked weekly restock and dinner-party scaling Task proposal submitted alongside this PR, plus planned follow-up tasks on recurring auto-replenishment hygiene and dietary household coordination.

## 3. Potential app connections

- **Internal personal apps (required):** Mail: receives confirmations, shopper substitution alerts, itemized receipts and refund credits. Calendar, Notes, Reminders and Messages: read or updated by tasks for home availability, saved recipes, shopping lists and household texts.
- **Other apps and websites (required):** None in initial scope; payment authorization, promos and delivery windows are modeled inside FreshCart.

### Integration details

- **Operations and access (required):** Agent-facing MCP operations: `stores.list|get|windows`, `products.search|get|buy_again`, `lists.list|get|update`, `cart.get|add|update_item|remove_item|set_replacement|apply_promo|clear`, `orders.preview|confirm|list|get|modify|cancel|respond_substitution|report_issue`, `account.get|update_preferences`. Approval-gated writes: `orders.confirm`, `orders.modify` when total rises, and `orders.cancel` when a cancellation fee applies. A lightweight GUI renderer (~8 pages: storefront, search results, product detail, Buy It Again, cart & replacements, checkout preview, live order/substitution tracker, itemized receipt) supports computer-use evaluation. Platform-facing operations: `env.reset`, `env.load_seed`, `env.apply_event`, `env.snapshot`, `env.validate_bundle` (following PR `#12`).
- **State and lifecycle (required):** App state (stores, SKU catalog, ingredients/nutrition, fee and promo schemas) is separated from Account state (membership, addresses, masked cards, past orders, default replacement policy) and Run state (simulated clock, active cart, expiring checkout previews, timed substitution holds, event log). `validateSeed` verifies integer-cent pricing, stock levels, time windows and SKU foreign keys; reset restores byte-identical initial state and clears transient previews and holds; `orders.confirm` is idempotent by `preview_id`; slot or inventory mutations between preview and confirm return conflict errors.
- **Events and notifications (required):** Accepted events: `inventory.stock_changed`, `orders.shopping_started`, `orders.substitution_proposed`, `orders.status_changed`, `delivery.window_delayed`, `promo.expired`, `account.session_expired` / `account.session_restored`. Published notifications: CloudEvents (`cloudevents/1.0`) text emails to shared `Mail` for order confirmation, timed shopper substitution action prompts, delivered itemized receipts and issue-credit notices. Task scenarios specify event schedules.
- **Shared data and dependencies (required):** Stable IDs (`person_id`, `account_id`, `store_id`, `sku_id`, `order_id`, `preview_id`); synthetic SVG/PNG placeholder item icons registered in `contribution/assets.catalog.json` (`< 2 MB` bundle); depends on shared `Mail` (plus task-authorized `Calendar`, `Notes`, `Reminders`, `Messages`) and the platform clock; zero external runtime services.
