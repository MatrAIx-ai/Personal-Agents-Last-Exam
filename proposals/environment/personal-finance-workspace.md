## 2. What you plan to build

**✍️ Your answer (required):** A resettable personal-finance workspace with bank accounts, brokerage cash and holdings, transaction history, market observations, financial announcements, and concrete-action confirmations. It supports inspecting financial context, transferring simulated cash between linked accounts, and buying or selling whole shares with explicit approval. Connections to Mail and Calendar let task authors introduce discoverable obligations and changing cash needs across sessions. The environment makes actions and their consequences inspectable while agents decide how to interpret and fulfill the user's intentions.

Initial workflows:

1. Inspect accounts, posted transactions, holdings, time-available prices and announcements; distinguish posted cash from expected income and uncertain information.
2. Preview an exact transfer, obtain a user decision, then execute or reject it. Retry a completed operation using its existing receipt. Support cancelling a pending proposal and replacing it with newly reviewed parameters.
3. Preview a whole-share buy/sell, show its reference quote and cash/position effects, obtain approval, and execute. A changed quote, account state, instruction revision, expired approval or revoked authority requires a new valid decision.
4. Deliver scheduled account postings and related Mail/Calendar records over simulated time, preserving state across sessions. Task authors can assess whether an agent discovers the obligation, revises its plan, and completes the currently authorized outcome.

An illustrative downstream task starts with money earmarked for a need in six months. A later conversation brings the need forward to two months and increases the amount. The agent must reconcile the new instruction with current accounts and existing plans, communicate what changed, and execute only actions covered by current authority. This proposal delivers the reusable environment; executable task rubrics and model-difficulty qualification would follow as separate Task proposals.

Reuse baseline: our research workspace contains a Python USD ledger shared by Bank/Brokerage, market replay, mobile/desktop UI, action previews and confirmations, audit records, and snapshot restoration. Its nine finance regression checks passed on 2026-11-15. PALE integration, independent reproduction, proposal cancellation and finance-specific instruction-revision enforcement remain implementation deliverables. A versioned, reviewable source package and reuse/license manifest will accompany implementation.

## 3. Potential app connections

- **Internal personal apps (required):** Mail: read dated obligation notices and changed payment dates. Calendar: read dates associated with the user's cash needs and create/update an explicitly requested reminder. MVP integration covers Mail reads and Calendar reads/writes; Mail writes are deferred. All observations and writes use the same entities and simulated clock as the financial ledger. PALE app interfaces are TBD pending the infrastructure release and maintainer review.
- **Other apps and websites (required):** A bundled synthetic market/announcement feed supplies deterministic observations. Existing public-market provider code can be reused as an optional demonstration after a source/reuse review. Cross-app shopping, travel, external bank connections and live trading are deferred.
