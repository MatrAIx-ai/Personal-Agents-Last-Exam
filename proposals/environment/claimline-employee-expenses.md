## 2. What you plan to build

**✍️ Your answer (required):** A resettable app that supports expense evidence collection while an employee is attending an activity, followed by policy-aware report preparation, employer review and reimbursement reconciliation. The fundamental record links **credit-card charge / itemized receipt / purpose**, each with its actual source and status. A card posting, a receipt total, a plausible explanation, an approved report and an actual payment are distinct records. The tested agent decides what evidence to retrieve or request and how to proceed; the app supplies inspectable sources and business operations.

Initial workflows:

1. **Activity and expense-evidence workspace.** Create an activity/trip workspace and expense candidates from authorized charge observations or user records. Store merchant, transaction reference, masked payment reference, currency, occurred/posted times, pending/settled state and amounts. Pending authorization and the final settled charge can be linked without inventing a second expense. Save itemized receipt originals with source IDs, document hashes and capture times. Track receipt items, tax/tip and total separately from extracted summaries. Retain corrected/voided versions.
2. **Charge–receipt–purpose association.** Link the actual charge, supporting itemized receipt and a purpose grounded in obtained evidence: activity/project, date, known attendees and a short explanation where applicable. Record the purpose source, uncertainty and any user clarification. Split a mixed personal/business receipt under explicit synthetic rules. Mark missing/mismatched elements as unresolved. Similar merchant/date/amount records require evidence before linking; a corrected receipt and an original receipt do not create two claims.
3. **Continuous collection and evidence requests.** Import an obtained email attachment, save a receipt returned by a permitted merchant-download operation, or attach a user-supplied photo/file. Track outstanding receipt/purpose requests and the actual responses. Task fixtures may include public retrieval expiries and a temporary opportunity to obtain a paper receipt; only the actor able to supply it can create that original evidence. The agent cannot generate a receipt or manufacture employer/merchant confirmation. Scheduling and proactive checks belong to the agent/host, with recorded permissions.
4. **Policy and report lifecycle.** Inspect versioned employer rules, eligibility, required fields, deadlines and date bases. Create/edit line items; preview policy/evidence findings and the reimbursement total; submit or discard. Amend a returned report and resubmit against its revision. An unsafe/missing field is surfaced clearly; the app does not infer the user's business purpose from a generic category. Submission states include draft, submitted, returned, approved and rejected, with a durable history.
5. **Employer decisions and reimbursement.** Authorized scenario/reviewer operations return a report for clarification or approve/reject specified amounts. The employee's agent cannot approve its own claim. Observe scheduled/paid/failed reimbursements and partial payments, reconcile the paid amount to the approved line items and source evidence, and retain outstanding balances. Request IDs prevent duplicate submissions and payment events. No real reimbursement or financial transfer occurs.

Illustrative downstream task: before a conference, the employee delegates ongoing meal-expense capture. During the conference, receipts arrive through several authorized channels and one must be requested while the user can still obtain it. The agent preserves the charge/receipt/purpose associations, handles a tip-adjusted posting and corrected receipt, then prepares a complete report and accurately tracks any remaining evidence or payment. A companion Task proposal develops this temporal evidence-collection problem.

**Distinct scope:** document environment [#33](https://github.com/MatrAIx-ai/Personal-Agents-Last-Exam/pull/33) stores and relates records; Claimline implements employee claim line items, employer policy/review roles and actual reimbursement state. Patient portal [#14](https://github.com/MatrAIx-ai/Personal-Agents-Last-Exam/pull/14) names medical-benefits reimbursement as a future connection. Claimline's initial scope is ordinary employee business expenses, with no FSA/HSA or insurance claims. [Finance #6](https://github.com/MatrAIx-ai/Personal-Agents-Last-Exam/pull/6) owns bank/brokerage operations; Claimline can function against fixture-backed charge/payment observations and has an independent state/reset boundary.

## 3. Potential app connections

- **Internal personal apps (required):** Mail supplies merchant receipts, requests/replies, returned-report notices and payment notices; Files stores actual receipt bytes and exports; Calendar supplies the authorized activity context. The host can expose scoped receipt notifications and agent-registered wakeups. A Calendar event alone does not prove a purchase or a business purpose. Messages/photo intake is optional if the same user file handoff can be represented through Files.
- **Other apps and websites (required):** A local synthetic card feed and merchant-receipt endpoints supply charge records and downloadable receipt originals. Any receipt expiry is publicly stated. An optional Finance #6 integration can replace equivalent charge/payment observations after contracts are agreed. No live employer, bank, card or merchant credentials are used.

### Integration details

- **Operations and access (required):** Browser plus same-state public MCP/API reads for charges, receipts, purpose evidence, policy, report revisions and payment status; actions for saving obtained receipt files, associating evidence, requesting missing details, editing/preparing/submitting reports and reconciling payments. Setup/reset, receipt delivery, reviewer decisions and payment posting are platform/scenario operations outside the employee agent's role. Public scheduling/permission contracts are agreed with maintainers; the adapter never finds the correct receipt or fills in a purpose for the agent.
- **State and lifecycle (required):** Separate employer/policy definitions; employee/reviewer account roles; and per-run charges, receipt bytes/hashes/versions, purpose provenance, associations, requests, report revisions, approvals, payment records and event/delivery cursor. Use exact cents and explicit currency. Store pending/settled lineage and correction relationships. Confirmed submissions bind a report revision and current authorization; concurrent evidence changes produce visible conflict/review. Reset restores all logical state and original assets; restore resumes the recorded workspace. Unknown or incomplete effects retain that status until reconciled.
- **Events and notifications (required):** Incoming charge pending/settled/adjusted/refunded events, receipt delivery/correction/expiry, participant/user clarification, policy publication, reviewer return/decision and reimbursement posting/failure. Example: a meal authorization settles with a tip; the final charge updates the same evidence lineage, and the agent must reconcile it with the obtained itemized receipt. Events carry public references, not hidden rubric labels. Outgoing Mail/Calendar or request messages use agreed host routes and stable activity/expense/report IDs.
- **Shared data and dependencies (required):** Activity, employee, merchant, charge/authorization, receipt/version, expense, purpose-source, report/revision, decision and payment IDs. Receipt originals use documented local PDF/image/text fixtures with MIME type, byte hash and source/capture time; extracted fields point to their source. Sensitive fields are fictional/masked. Local fixture providers avoid dependence on external environments. Resolve actual file/attachment transport and adopted App contracts at design review.
