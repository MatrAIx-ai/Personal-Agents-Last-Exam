## 2. What you plan to build

**✍️ Your answer (required):** Ridgeway MVD is a resettable portal of a fictional state motor vehicle agency, where a renewal, an address change, a notice of sale or an office booking can go through cleanly and still be wrong for this person. One account holder sees their driver record, the vehicles they own, the holds that block them, and office slots; other agencies report holds, emissions tests and insurance by event, and deadlines, late fees and mailing dates follow the environment clock. Four workflows are complete in the first version.

**Entities.** Person (driver record with licence status, expiry, REAL ID status and addresses), dependant, vehicle (owners, registration period, county, emissions and insurance status), hold, emissions test, insurance filing, fee schedule, payment method, preview, payment, mailing, address record, transfer notice, office, service, slot, appointment, notice.

**Simplifications.** One fictional state with three counties and one time zone; business days are Monday to Friday minus the seed's closed dates. One App instance holds one account holder. Money is integer cents; cards are masked synthetic records. These rules follow California DMV's public pages: open citations and a missing emissions certificate block an online renewal, an address change should be reported within 10 days of moving, mail goes to the address on record so a recent change needs 3 business days before a renewal, and an address change issues no new card. Fees, windows, penalty tiers, cut-offs and lock periods are Ridgeway's own. Ridgeway MVD writes a notice only when something happens to the account (a hold placed or cleared, a returned mailing) and a receipt for each payment; it sends no renewal or appointment reminders, so those dates are read from the records.

- **W1 Records, holds and fees.** Read the driver record, each vehicle the holder owns, and holds with their type, agency, amount and where they can be cleared. A hold that is payable at the agency (a parking citation) is paid by preview, confirm and receipt; a toll or insurance hold is cleared only by the other agency's event. Paths: modify = not applicable (records change only through W2–W4 or events); cancel = an unconfirmed preview simply expires; errors = another adult's record or a vehicle the holder does not own (`not_authorized`), a hold that cannot be paid here (`not_payable_here`), `already_cleared`, `preview_expired`, `preview_used`, `card_expired`, `card_declined`.
- **W2 Registration renewal.** `renewals.preview` returns eligibility, each fee line, the late penalty tier, the new expiry date and the address the sticker will be mailed to; `renewals.confirm` charges the chosen card and returns a receipt, the new registration period and a mailing record. Online renewal opens 75 days before expiry and closes 90 days after it. Paths: modify = a new preview replaces an old one; cancel = not applicable after confirm, because a paid renewal is final online; errors = `outside_renewal_window`, `office_visit_required`, `hold_present`, `emissions_required`, `insurance_not_on_file`, `not_registered_owner`, `amount_changed` (the penalty tier moved since the preview), plus the preview and card errors of W1.
- **W3 Record changes.** An address change (residence, optional mailing address, move date, and which of the holder's licence and vehicles it applies to) and a notice of transfer when a vehicle is sold or given away (transfer date, new owner's name, odometer). Both are preview then confirm; the preview states every consequence. Paths: modify = a later address change supersedes an earlier one and both stay in the history; cancel = not applicable, because a filed change or notice cannot be deleted online, and a notice of transfer is final; errors = `not_authorized`, `invalid_address`, `transfer_date_in_future`, `transfer_date_before_ownership`, `already_transferred`, `preview_expired`, `preview_used`.
- **W4 Office appointments.** List offices and the services each offers, search open slots, book for the holder or a dependant, reschedule and cancel. Booking returns the documents this person must bring for this service. Paths: modify = `appointments.reschedule`; cancel = `appointments.cancel`; errors = `slot_taken`, `service_not_offered`, `duplicate_appointment` (one open appointment per person and service), `not_eligible_until` (for example a road test before a learner permit has been held 180 days), `too_late_to_change` (inside 2 hours of the start), `rebooking_locked` (14 days after a no-show).

Rules:

- Time is the environment's. Ridgeway MVD has no timers, no scheduler and no clock event: Infra moves `virtual_now` with an event or with Core's `env.advance_time`, which runs no App code. Every time-based state is therefore stored as a date or a due time and derived from `ctx.virtual_now` wherever it is read or checked: a registration's status, renewal window and penalty tier from its expiry date, a preview's `expired` from its 15 minutes, the mailing address in effect from the 3 business days, a mailing's `mailed` from its mail date, and an appointment's cut-off, a no-show lock and an eligibility date from their own dates. Writes and events use the same derivation: a confirm on a preview that has run out returns `preview_expired`, a confirm after the penalty tier has moved returns `amount_changed`, and a mailing update before the mail date is rejected. Nothing is rewritten when a date passes.
- Authority. The Agent acts as the bound account holder. It can read and act on that person's driver record and on vehicles where the person is a registered owner, and it can book appointments for the person or a seeded dependant. Any other adult's record returns `not_authorized`. Either registered owner of a co-owned vehicle may renew it or file its transfer.
- Renewal. A renewal needs: the window to be open; no open hold on the vehicle or on the owner; a passing emissions test in the last 90 days when the vehicle's county and age require one; insurance on file; and a valid card. The fee is the class fee, the county fee of the address on record, the late penalty for the current tier and a service fee, each shown as its own line. The new expiry is counted from the previous expiry, not from the payment date.
- Mailing. Stickers and cards go to the mailing address that is in effect on the confirm date. A change filed fewer than 3 business days earlier is not yet in effect for mailing; the preview names the address that will be used and the date the new one takes over.
- Address. The county of the new residence decides the county fee and the emissions requirement from the next renewal. A change reported more than 10 days after the move date is recorded as late; there is no fee. No new card is issued.
- Transfer. From the transfer date the seller is no longer responsible for the vehicle: later holds placed on it do not attach to the seller, and the seller can no longer renew it. A notice cannot be withdrawn online.
- Appointments. A slot holds one booking. A reschedule or cancel is allowed until 2 hours before the start. Changes to the driver record after a visit, such as REAL ID compliance, arrive with the `appointment.closed` event.

**Value for personal-agent tasks.** Every step here has a default that is fine for most people and wrong for some. Renewing the day after an address change sends the sticker to the old address; "the car" may be the one sold in March, or the one that belongs to a spouse whose record the agent may not touch; a hold can be paid here or must be cleared at another agency first; the right office and slot depend on the person's week and on a flight that needs REAL ID; a teenager's road test is not bookable until a date only the record shows. Ridgeway MVD makes these checkable: which preview was confirmed with which card, what was mailed where, which change was filed with which dates, and which slot was held for whom. Distinct task situations (separate Task proposals): (1) renew a registration during a house move without sending the sticker to the old address or paying the wrong county fee; (2) a toll hold blocks a renewal and has to be cleared at the toll authority first, before the penalty tier changes; (3) get a REAL ID appointment before a booked flight when the nearest office has no slot in time; (4) file a notice of transfer with the exact date from the sale messages, so that later tolls are not the seller's; (5) book a dependant's road test on the first eligible day that fits the family's calendar; (6) an insurer change leaves a gap on file that must be fixed before renewal. Fixtures are plain seed data, so each task loads its own people, vehicles, holds, fees, offices and history.

**Boundaries and overlap.** Proposal list checked on 2026-11-21: no other motor vehicle or licensing environment. #16 (CivicDesk, same domain) is a portal for public comments on proposed rules and shares no workflow. #144 (ShelfMark, same domain, opened on 2026-11-21) is a public-library catalog with holds and loans and shares none either. #27 (Crossway) is a toll authority's account; its notices, disputes and transponders stay there. Crossway's proposal says the seller of a vehicle "files a notice of transfer and separately ends possession on Crossway"; Ridgeway MVD could be the agency that receives that notice, and a toll hold here could mirror an unpaid Crossway notice. #46 (Coverline) collects auto-insurance quotes and binds no policy; insurance status reaches Ridgeway MVD as an insurer's event. None of these shares state with Ridgeway MVD: a task that uses two of them seeds consistent data in each. I will ask the authors of #27 and #46 in this PR whether the hold and insurance events fit their models.

The first version has no GUI and no reviewer console.
- **State and lifecycle (required):** State is separate per run and App instance, and one instance holds one account holder (`isolation_keys: run_id, app_id`). The seed schema carries agency records (counties, fee schedule, offices, services, slots, closed dates), the holder's records (driver record, dependants, vehicles, registration periods, holds, emissions tests, insurance filings, payment methods) and history (address records, transfer notices, payments, mailings, appointments, notices, activity rows), so a task can load a household in the middle of a move or with a renewal already overdue. `validateSeed` rejects duplicate IDs, dangling references, timestamps without a UTC offset, a vehicle without an owner, overlapping registration periods, a booked slot without its appointment, and an account that the binding does not declare (`invalid_seed`). Writes follow Core: an exact repeat of a committed `request_id` or `event_id` returns the stored receipt marked `duplicate`, so a retried confirm never charges twice; the same ID with different input returns `idempotency_conflict`; a stale `expected_version` returns `stale_version`; a failed call leaves data, version, payments and notices unchanged and Core appends a denial audit row. Reads never change business data: a registration's status and penalty tier, a preview's remaining time, a mailing's `scheduled` or `mailed` state and an appointment's cut-off are computed from stored records and `virtual_now`. Reset is Core's: it restores the loaded seed and the time loaded with it (`reset_scope: app_instance`) and touches no other App; messages already delivered to Mail stay there. Evaluator records come from three places: (a) App state in the snapshot (previews with their shown terms, payments with card and amount, mailings with the address used, address and transfer records with their dates, appointments with slot and person, and activity rows with sequence, virtual time, actor `agent` or `event`, action, object ID and the `request_id` or `event_id` behind them, one for every committed Agent write, one for every applied event and one for each further effect of that commit, such as a notice or a receipt written); (b) Core audit rows with virtual time and the projected `id`, `start` and `end`: one for every allowed call that is not a replay, one for every denied call, and one for every `env.advance_time` with the clock before and after; (c) full arguments and results in the supplied Agent execution trace. They join on `request_id`, `event_id` and version. Counting rules: count committed writes and their effects from the activity rows, one row each. Core returns the stored receipt for a repeated `request_id` or `event_id` before it appends an audit row, so a replay appears only in the trace, as a call whose result carries `duplicate: true`: count it as a retry of the original, never as a second payment, filing or booking. A rejected attempt writes no activity row: count rejected attempts from the denial audit rows and the trace, one for each attempt, repeats included. Count reads from the allowed audit rows and the trace. Time-driven changes (a preview running out, a registration entering its window or expiring, a penalty tier moving, an address taking effect for mailing, a mailing becoming `mailed`, a cut-off or lock passing) run no App code and write no row: read them from the stored dates and the clock, and from the audit row of the `env.advance_time` call or the event that moved it. Take the snapshot before a reset, because reset clears the audit.
- **Events and notifications (required):** Seven events through `env.apply_event`, in Core's envelope. Ridgeway MVD has no clock event: Infra moves time with an event or with `env.advance_time`. An event is handled at its own time T, which is `ctx.virtual_now` inside the handler in runtime 1.6.0: it is applied to the states as they are at T (a mailing whose mail date has not come takes no update, and a hold placed after a transfer date does not attach to the seller), then notices are written.
