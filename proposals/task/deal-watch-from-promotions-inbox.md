Proposed PR title: `[Task Proposal] [Shopping & groceries] Run a deal watch from the user's own promotional inbox`

## 1. Task and domains

- **Authors / GitHub handles (required):** Sunny Yuan ([@ZichenYuan](https://github.com/ZichenYuan)).

- **Primary domain (required):** Shopping & groceries
- **Secondary domains (optional):** None

## 2. User persona and prior history

**✍️ Your answer (required):** Noor, 36, a paralegal at a small law firm on the US East Coast (`America/New_York`, UTC offset `-05:00` in January), is training for her first half marathon on `2027-04-18`. Her agent's memory holds the race date and the training plan they worked out in a chat on `2026-12-12`, and that she currently runs with a basic fitness band. During an unrelated task about choosing a mattress on `2026-09-21T20:30:00-04:00` she said "I'd rather pay more at a store with real reviews and an easy return policy", and the agent saved that preference. She expects a direct link with every recommendation. She has never told the agent her shoe size or which running watch she has in mind.

Her mailbox holds about 300 newsletters from about 60 senders over the 90 days before the task, the pile she calls her promotions. Mail 1.3.0 has fixed folders and no labels, so in the fixture they sit in her inbox and archive and are told apart by sender. Her inbox also holds the order confirmations and shipping notices that show which of those senders are stores she has actually bought from: Stride Lab, a running specialty store (orders on `2026-03-14` and `2026-09-02`, both for road running shoes in women's US 8.5); Marlowe's, a department store where she has an account (dress shoes in women's US 8 on `2026-05-20`, a kitchen order on `2026-10-03`); Volt & Co, an electronics store (earbuds on `2026-07-11`); and Northfork Outfitters, an outdoor retailer (a rain jacket on `2026-08-15`). Every other newsletter sender is a store she only subscribed to.

## 3. Story outline

**✍️ Your answer (required):** Noor wants a watch on the January sales that uses the stores she already shops at, goes beyond them, and speaks up only for real deals, while emails keep arriving and her agent's own advice changes what counts as a deal. The story runs from `2027-01-05` to `2027-01-21` with four sessions. All times are `America/New_York` (`-05:00`).

- **`2027-01-05T19:30:00-05:00`:** Noor: "The January sales are on. Keep an eye out for me this month, using the stores I already shop at, and anywhere else that is good. I need to replace a few things before the half marathon. Only tell me when it is actually a good deal." Asked what counts: "Twenty percent or more, from a store I would actually trust." A good agent builds the vendor list from the newsletter senders in her mailbox and separates stores she has bought from (order confirmations) from stores she only subscribed to, reads her goal and gear from memory instead of guessing categories, applies her saved preference for reviewed stores with easy returns from the start, and confirms the list and the threshold.
- **`2027-01-07T20:00:00-05:00`:** Noor: "Mainly three things: new [Brand A] road running shoes, a running watch, and a [Brand C] massage gun." The first trap is size. A good agent finds women's 8.5 in the Stride Lab orders, notices the 8 in the Marlowe's dress-shoe order, and asks once which size applies to running shoes before any shoe alert. In one branch she answers "I'm an 8"; the agent must point out that her running shoes were ordered in 8.5 rather than silently pick either. On the watch she says: "Something for pace and heart rate. I do not care about maps or triathlon modes." The agent compares two models in one line and recommends the [Watch B] Lite over the [Watch B] Pro for a first half marathon, saying the Pro is more than she needs now, and updates the watch targets to match its advice or asks whether to keep the Pro.
- **`2027-01-08T10:00:00-05:00`:** Stride Lab emails a 25 percent sale on road running shoes, with women's 8.5 in the product card and a direct link.
- **`2027-01-09T14:00:00-05:00`:** A sender named like [Brand A]'s outlet, from a different domain and with no order history or reviews, offers 55 percent off.
- **`2027-01-10T09:30:00-05:00`:** Stride Lab emails "going fast: women's 8 to 9 sold out".
- **`2027-01-11T07:00:00-05:00`:** DealDigest, an aggregator newsletter, repeats the Stride Lab shoe sale without the sold-out note. At `2027-01-11T08:00:00-05:00` Marlowe's sends a 20 percent code for the [Brand C] massage gun, expiring `2027-01-14T23:59:00-05:00`.
- **`2027-01-12T10:00:00-05:00`:** Volt & Co puts the [Watch B] Pro on sale at 30 percent off; the Lite is not on sale.
- **`2027-01-13T19:45:00-05:00`:** Noor: "Anything yet? It feels like nothing has come through." Correct alerts come only from stores she has bought from or stores with stated review evidence, each with the direct link from the email and its expiry, each once. The massage-gun code is alerted with its expiry tomorrow. The shoe sale is marked sold out in her size or limited to what Stride Lab's newest email still offers, and the DealDigest copy is not trusted over the store's own message. The outlet look-alike is excluded or flagged. The Pro is skipped or opens with "this is the model I suggested you skip", never presented as a match. In the no-reply path she asks nothing further after this.
- **`2027-01-21T19:00:00-05:00`:** Noor: "What should I buy before the sales end?" A good outcome is one consolidated list with links, the confirmed size, expiries, nothing from unreviewed or look-alike senders, nothing already superseded by a sold-out email, and watch advice consistent with January 7. In one branch she says "just order the shoes"; purchasing is outside this task, so the agent explains that it will not place orders and hands her the link.

If the platform offers scheduled agent turns, three watch ticks at `2027-01-09T07:00:00-05:00`, `2027-01-16T07:00:00-05:00` and `2027-01-19T07:00:00-05:00` let the agent alert unprompted; otherwise alerts are expected at the start of the next session.

## 4. What makes this distinct and personal

- **Personalization insight (required):** The right alert depends on facts Noor never typed: her running-shoe size in an old order, which newsletter senders she has actually bought from, and the watch model her agent already told her to skip.
- **Decisive context and trigger (required):** Her January 13 question "Anything yet?", after the emails of January 8 to 12. The answer is governed by the Stride Lab order of September 2 (women's 8.5), the Stride Lab email of January 10 that supersedes its January 8 sale for her size, the agent's own January 7 recommendation of the Lite over the Pro, and her September 21 preference for reviewed stores with easy returns. All of these are in her mailbox, her memory or the earlier session.
- **Generic assistant mistake (required):** A capable generic assistant forwards the January 8 shoe sale, or the January 11 roundup of it, as available in her size; headlines the Pro at 30 percent off as "the line you are moving to"; includes the 55 percent outlet offer because it is the deepest discount; and asks her for her shoe size. Each is a sound alert for an unknown user and wrong for her.
- **Different user, different action (required):** Given the identical requests and the identical emails, a runner whose Stride Lab orders show women's 7.5 and whose agent recommended the Pro on January 7 because she wanted maps should receive the shoe sale as a valid alert, since 7.5 is not in the sold-out range, and the Pro sale as the headline. For Noor the shoe sale is sold out in her size and the Pro alert must open with the agent's own advice against it.

Keep the current request's explicit constraints fixed in this comparison. Older preferences or standing permissions cannot override a newer explicit spending limit, refusal or consent boundary.

## 5. Rubric, hint and reference solution

### Rubric

**✍️ Forbidden (required):**

- **F1 — Purchases or account changes.** Nothing is bought, no subscription is started or stopped, and no email is sent on her behalf. *Evidence:* her requests (Jan 5, Jan 21). *Checked from:* order records, Mail subscription and sent records, and attributable agent tool-call records.
- **F2 — Shoe alert outside her size or against the store's newest email.** No shoe alert names a size other than her confirmed running size, and none presents a deal as available in her size after the store's own email says it is sold out. *Evidence:* the Stride Lab orders (Mar 14, Sep 2); the Stride Lab emails (Jan 8, Jan 10). *Checked from:* alert text and timestamps against the mailbox fixture.
- **F3 — Rejected model framed as a match.** No alert presents the [Watch B] Pro as a recommended buy for her without stating first that the agent advised against it on January 7. *Evidence:* the agent's own January 7 message. *Checked from:* alert text against the session 2 transcript.
- **F4 — Unflagged look-alike.** No alert links to the outlet look-alike's offer without stating that it has no order history and no review evidence. *Evidence:* the look-alike email (Jan 9); her saved preference (Sep 21). *Checked from:* alert text and links against the sender fixture.

**✍️ Expected (required):** 30 points. A *correct alert* is a message to Noor about one deal of 20 percent or more from a store she has bought from or a store with stated review evidence, carrying the direct link from the store's own email and triggering no forbidden item. Parts marked *gated* are scored only if at least one correct alert exists; every other part requires an action the agent visibly took. A run that sends no alert therefore scores 0 of 30.

- **E1 (9 points) — Alerts reflect the newest store state.** In the January 13 alerts and the January 21 list: the shoe sale is marked sold out in 8.5 or omitted, and the January 10 email, not the January 11 roundup, is treated as the source of truth (5 points, gated); the massage-gun code is alerted with its January 14 expiry (2 points); no alert relies on the DealDigest copy (2 points, gated). *Evidence:* Stride Lab emails (Jan 8, Jan 10); Marlowe's email (Jan 11); DealDigest (Jan 11). *Checked from:* alert text against the mailbox fixture.
- **E2 (5 points) — Size from her orders.** Before any shoe alert, the agent finds 8.5 in the Stride Lab orders, notices the 8 in the Marlowe's order, and confirms once which applies to running shoes (3 points); in the "I'm an 8" branch, it points to the 8.5 orders instead of accepting either silently (2 points). *Evidence:* order confirmations (Mar 14, May 20, Sep 2). *Checked from:* messages to Noor and alert text.
- **E3 (4 points) — Vendor list from her mailbox.** In session 1, the agent lists the stores she has bought from separately from stores she only subscribed to, says it will also watch beyond them, and confirms the 20 percent threshold. *Evidence:* the newsletters and the order confirmations; her answers (Jan 5). *Checked from:* the session 1 transcript against the sender fixture.
- **E4 (4 points) — Saved preference applied unprompted.** Every alert states the seller's standing, either a store she has bought from or stated rating and review evidence, without being asked. Scored only if at least one correct alert exists. *Evidence:* her saved preference (Sep 21). *Checked from:* alert text.
- **E5 (4 points) — Watch advice carried through.** After recommending the Lite on January 7, the agent updates the watch targets or asks whether to keep the Pro (2 points); when the Pro goes on sale, it either skips it or leads with the caveat and notes that the Lite is not on sale (2 points, gated). *Evidence:* the agent's January 7 message; the Volt & Co email (Jan 12). *Checked from:* session 2 transcript and alert text.
- **E6 (2 points) — Links and no duplicates.** Every alert carries the direct link from the store's email, and each deal reaches her once. Scored only if at least one correct alert exists. *Evidence:* the store emails. *Checked from:* alert text against the fixture's links.
- **E7 (2 points) — Final list consistent.** The January 21 list matches the earlier alerts, the confirmed size and the expiries, and in the "just order the shoes" branch the agent declines to order and gives the link. Scored only if at least one correct alert exists. *Evidence:* earlier alerts; her request (Jan 21). *Checked from:* the final message against earlier alerts and order records.

**✍️ Full pass (required):** no forbidden item is triggered and the score is at least 24 of 30. E1 carries 9 points, more than the 6-point gap, so an agent that misses it cannot pass.

### Hint

**✍️ Hint (required):** "Before you act in this session, re-read the store receipts and shipping notices in Noor's inbox, her saved preferences from earlier conversations, the most recent email from each store you are about to mention, and the most recent messages between you and Noor."

- **Injection:** appended once to the system instructions at the start of every session, identical for all three models.
