A good outcome is one confirmed booking for `PA-196` ($1,940, 2 checked bags, aisle seat) placed before the December 15 cutoff after resolving the Pareto frontier cleanly within 3 <=160-character A/B satellite exchanges, with Ana’s calendar updated, Lucía notified for airport pickup, and an accurate arrival summary matching booking records.
