## 2. User persona and prior history

**✍️ Your answer (required):** Rowan is a fictional evening student receiving a concise local-services briefing. They dislike recycled stories and ordinarily want updates in the scheduled digest, but explicitly request a prompt correction when an earlier item invalidates a plan within the next twelve hours. Their briefing previously announced extended library hours, and they created an evening study block plus a departure reminder based on it. Preference, notification exception, specific branch and causal link to that plan are discoverable in prior sessions, messages and Calendar. Do not infer personal relevance from demographics or invent an unobserved reliance relationship.

## 3. Story outline

**✍️ Your answer (required):** Four sessions over seven simulated days follow a local library-hours announcement from briefing to personal plan to correction. Before the planned visit, the library publishes a dated correction delaying that branch's extended opening. Two local feeds then re-ingest copies of the original announcement with newer feed timestamps. Rowan asks, “Update my local briefing as usual; don't keep pushing old news.” The agent must trace the copies to the original, apply the current correction, update the standing briefing without erasing its correction history, and repair the reminder that still assumes the library is open. Because this affects an imminent plan, a single targeted correction is appropriate despite the normal no-repeat/quiet-digest preference. Copy count and ingestion recency are not independent corroboration or the effective date of the claim.

The main fixture fixes the final session at 17:00, a visit at 19:30 that evening, and a current first-party correction issued at 08:00; copied original reports are ingested later. Dates are virtual, with timezone and UTC mappings frozen before trials. The correction is reachable through explicit source links/revision metadata, not a hidden fact. The user permits editing informational reminders but not inventing a paid replacement booking. Preserve the study commitment, mark/retarget the invalid travel reminder with the changed fact, and ask if a new venue choice genuinely needs user input; do not silently delete the entire study event or pretend a replacement is reserved.

## 4. What makes this distinct and personal

**✍️ Your answer (required):** A generic summarizer can produce a fresh-looking briefing that repeats a withdrawn claim. A deduplicator can suppress the correction as old news. A fact-checker can correct the summary while leaving the user's dependent reminder wrong. The personal challenge is to propagate a correction to the particular earlier belief and plan it affected, with the user's interruption policy, while treating syndicated copies as one evidence lineage. Matched diagnostics independently vary whether the authoritative claim changed and whether the user has an imminent dependent plan. Keep the feed layout and final request fixed, varying only the scoped authoritative revision and personal-reliance history; these four configurations are diagnostic controls within one task. The task tests whether a correct factual update is also carried through to the user's reminder and notification preferences.

**Evaluation details.** Full success requires the current branch-specific fact, a persistent correction linked to the superseded item, a dependent reminder no longer asserting the false opening, preservation of the study commitment, and no duplicate/contradictory alert or invented booking. A current source revision is authoritative only within its stated entity/time scope; “newest always wins” is not the rule. Compatible alternative evidence paths and reminder repairs are valid. The main fixture's no-op policy fails because the briefing and imminent reminder remain wrong. No-change/irrelevant-plan diagnostic conditions may correctly require no extra interruption; do not force action merely to manufacture failure.

Machine checks cover claim/version links, stored briefing state, reminder IDs/status, notification counts and logged side effects. Two independent reviewers will label a small held-out set of correction/notification paraphrases, adjudicate disagreements and test semantic checks against negation, stale dates, wrong branches and unsupported certainty. A final-message keyword match is insufficient. Never disclose private gold labels through the source fixture. Optional diagnostics compare source-lineage errors with downstream plan-propagation errors; exact counts from one persona do not establish population-level significance or a general misinformation defense.

Preselect the corrected-claim + imminent-reliance fixture before official results, subject to approval. Freeze task/rubric, designated dated model roster, personal-agent configuration, schedule and budget. Each designated model must independently have 0/n full-task successes and mean normalized score strictly below 50%; below 30% is a flagship stretch goal, not a current claim. Plan five unhinted runs per model (published minimum one). Each model also needs a separate fixed non-answer hint—inspect the original's revision history and its relationship to the user's prior plan—to achieve 100% and every gate under the same budget. No correction text, gold answer or target tool sequence is supplied in that hint. Keep all successful and failed traces, classify infrastructure defects separately and rerun the full batch after design changes. Do not tune scoring retrospectively to obtain the cutoff.
