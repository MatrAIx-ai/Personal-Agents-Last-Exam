## 2. User persona and prior history

**✍️ Your answer (required):** Riley wants a personal assistant to keep a useful skin diary and maintain Black Friday skincare price alerts. Riley describes their skin as combination, with an oily T-zone and cheeks that feel dry in colder weather. Dated self-reports mention occasional small chin spots and fine lines around the eyes. These are owner observations and concerns, not a dermatologist's diagnosis or a measured clinical severity score. Riley prefers a small routine and wants a backup of a moisturizer they have actually used and currently wish to repurchase. A September product-use record identifies the **exact formula revision** from the package label: `C-50/F1`, 50 ml, with no discomfort reported during that use period and an explicit willingness to repurchase. A store receipt for the same product family does not establish which formula was used or whether it was tolerated. Riley also has a past serum entry marked stopped and not wanted again; fine-line concerns do not authorize restarting it or adding a new active treatment.

Riley's October standing preference is: monitor only a personally tried formula with a current repurchase intention; a similarly named reformulation is a separate candidate. If a later diary entry says a product has been set aside after discomfort, pause its purchase alerts until Riley explicitly opts back in. This rule governs attention and repurchase decisions, **not medical suitability**. Previously reporting no discomfort cannot guarantee future tolerance, and reporting stinging does not establish an allergy or its cause.

Riley permits private Notes updates and creation/update of product-price watches in a controlled shopping fixture. The ceiling is **$30 total for one 50 ml unit**, including applicable quoted shipping and tax, under account terms Riley already qualifies for. No new membership, subscription, purchase, seller contact, medical-data sharing or regimen change is authorized. Current product identity and payable price must be checked before a deal is presented.

Reuse Notes for private skin/use history and the Environment's four proposed business operations:

1. `pricewatch.offers.list`: read released current product/quote records and their source/time/expiry, including formula, pack size, quantity, currency, seller, shipping, tax and account-eligible coupon terms.
2. `pricewatch.watch.upsert`: create or update one watch with an exact formula/pack target, one-unit quantity, allowed sellers, inclusive-price ceiling and active/paused state. All writes follow the pinned Core `expected_version` and `request_id` contract; the version is the App state `{epoch, revision}`, not the watch's own business revision. Exact same-argument retries may reuse the request ID; a changed version/action requires a new one. A logical watch key prevents duplicate targets. Pin the approved Environment version and payload schema before Task integration; these remain proposed fields, not a claim that the App already exists.
3. `pricewatch.watch.list` and `pricewatch.watch.get`: inspect the configured rule and its append-only candidate/transition history, including available, withdrawn and expired states.

All four tools belong to the separate Environment's proposed single App ID `pricewatch`, satisfying the shared runtime's App-prefix rule. Task #207 does not build a private copy or extend another App's public contract. Implementation is blocked on agreed #221 scope and a compatible runnable App; record the actual App/Core/Notes versions and contract hashes in the Task assembly.

The required state is small: a watch ID and business `watch_revision`, plus the separate Core App-state version for writes; neutral product family/formula/size, seller, currency, quantity and price-rule fields; active/paused state; and candidate IDs linked to the business watch revision/activation, offer identity, item/shipping/tax/eligible-discount lines, observation time and expiry. Private Notes link real watch/candidate IDs to the personal justification; the service does not receive the raw diary.

Price-event content and scheduling belong to this Task; the existing Infra `env.apply_event` invokes #221's App-owned quote/event operation, with the exact reviewed schema pinned at integration. The event handler commits the new quote and evaluates **the rule the agent actually registered**. It must not read the private skin history, grader answers or a precomputed “suitable for Riley” flag. Selecting an unsuitable formula remains technically possible and scoreable. Known exact identity, account-eligible all-in total, quantity, seller and current validity determine the mechanical match.

A match creates one candidate with status `available_in_app`. A replay of the same event/offer episode creates no duplicate; a genuinely new episode can produce a new candidate if the watch remains active. Pausing a watch withdraws its currently available candidates without erasing their prior availability history; separate session transcripts retain any actual presentation evidence. The service has no self-reported “user read” or “presented” field. Subsequent price changes do not create candidates while paused. Expiry changes current validity without rewriting history. Watch creation/update also evaluates a currently available quote, so a qualifying product chosen at S2 is not missed merely because the quote predates that call.

`available_in_app` means a candidate is committed in this fixture. It does not prove a phone push, that the user read anything or that the agent has spoken. The S3 assistant reply is the separate evidence of presentation. No generic push receiver, new Infra scheduler, open-web scraper, real merchant account or checkout is included. Native phone notification delivery would require a separately accepted receiving App and integration; [Notifications #191](https://github.com/MatrAIx-ai/Personal-Agents-Last-Exam/pull/191) is open/unmerged as checked November 23 and is not assumed available. Notes/Calendar alone do not implement a price watch or delivered alert.

## 4. What makes this distinct and personal

- **Personalization insight (required):** A cheap skincare offer is relevant only to the formula Riley actually tried and still wants. New personal experience can invalidate a live recommendation, so a later price drop must not revive its paused alert.
- **Decisive context and trigger (required):** At S2, the current general request and enticing same-name F2 offer do not identify what Riley used. The dated package-label/use record ties personal experience to F1; a receipt, skin-type label and manufacturer marketing do not transfer it to F2. The assistant must persist the correct exact-formula watch, rather than merely describe a preference. At S4, a new skin diary entry and the older set-aside rule change that watch and its active candidate before the next price event.
- **Generic assistant mistake (required):** A competent generic shopping assistant carries a product-family watch forward to the store's reformulated listing because the name and size match, promotes the largest advertised reduction, or continues recommending an attractive offer after the user has set that product aside. A generic skin organizer may record the discomfort accurately but leave the shopping watch active. Both miss the need to update persistent cross-domain eligibility.
- **Different user, different action (required):** Keep both current requests, skin-type description, repurchase preference, sellers, prices, size, inclusive budget and permissions identical. Change only `used_formula_revision` in the September use/label record from F1 to F2; both histories report no discomfort during that use and a willingness to repurchase. At S2, the comparison user should have an F2/50 ml watch and receive the valid $19.44 F2 notice. Riley should keep the F1 watch and receive no qualified F2 notice. This does not assume older is safer than newer: the personal evidence identifies the eligible version. The control ends at S2, before the later main-story diary events.

**Related work and scope:** [Task #21](https://github.com/MatrAIx-ai/Personal-Agents-Last-Exam/pull/21) already proposes personalized deal monitoring from purchases, preferences and changing merchant emails. This task's proposed increment is **formula-bound personal-use evidence and a later personal skin experience invalidating an operational watch and an already valid candidate**, including an unseen later price event that must remain suppressed. Generic discount ranking, seller checks, size matching and deduplication alone are not claimed as novel. The submitted [dog-care Task #205](https://github.com/MatrAIx-ai/Personal-Agents-Last-Exam/pull/205) instead centers on review cadence, actual care and service composition; this task requires a price-watch state machine. Maintainers must still assess distinctness and actual difficulty; subject matter alone does not establish a separate accepted task.
