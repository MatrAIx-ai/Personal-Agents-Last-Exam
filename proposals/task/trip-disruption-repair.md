## 2. User persona and prior history

**✍️ Your answer (required):** Maya is a fictional researcher traveling with her partner Sam from Los Angeles to a New York conference. Her prior messages and Calendar identify a required presentation time, the latest workable arrival, a preference for keeping useful paid bookings, a bounded additional-spend mandate and a preference for cash refunds over restricted credits unless she approves otherwise. The existing itinerary contains two passenger tickets, a fixed two-night hotel stay for two guests, an airport pickup and hotel arrival instructions. Confirmation IDs, local/UTC times, transfer duration/service windows and provider terms are discoverable in authorized records.

Maya can be temporarily unreachable. Her current grant specifies which changes the agent can make, the budget and expiry, and which decisions need her response. Older standing preferences do not enlarge that grant. The agent must investigate whether arrangements still fit; receipt, accepted flight offer, ticket issuance, provider acknowledgment and confirmed hotel service are distinct observations.

## 3. Story outline

**✍️ Your answer (required):** Five conversational sessions plus legitimate notifications and agent-registered wakeups span 72 simulated hours. Initially the trip is coherent: flight to JFK, pickup from JFK and arrival at the booked hotel during its agreed window. The airline then cancels or significantly changes the flight and offers an alternative arriving late at EWR. The user asks the agent to keep the trip workable for the conference within the established limits. One apparently cheap replacement misses the personal arrival constraint; another meets it but requires changing the pickup and arranging after-midnight hotel entry.

The agent must inspect the current offer and actual booking status, alternative-flight availability and all-in costs, ground-transfer options and the hotel's specific arrival conditions. It should confirm an authorized viable replacement and read back the ticketed result, update or replace the airport pickup with explicit airport/date/time and passenger coverage, and obtain a hotel arrival commitment that applies to the new expected arrival instant. A sent late-arrival request is not that commitment. Unrelated existing hotel arrangements remain valid unless their own conditions changed.

Provider events introduce a bounded partial-failure case: the first requested transfer change is unavailable, or a hotel reply acknowledges the request but omits the required dated window. Feasible alternatives/clarification paths are discoverable. The adapter cannot choose an appropriate option or favorable provider response for the agent. The flight and transfer fixtures execute real state changes independently; they offer no fictional atomic rollback across providers. Confirmation status is reconciled before retrying an uncertain write.

Maya later asks what changed, what remains confirmed and what it will cost now. A final check follows another public flight update or an unchanged-condition control. Main-branch success is a source-linked, actually confirmed itinerary whose flight/transfer/hotel-arrival commitments fit the required conference timing, both travelers and current authority. Obsolete bookings are handled within their visible deadlines, actual charges/receipts are recorded, and expected refunds or credits are not reported as spendable cash already received. The final message describes confirmed arrangements and outstanding items; it does not claim that physical travel or hotel service has already occurred.

The hotel scope is deliberately compatible with proposed [StayReady #44](https://github.com/MatrAIx-ai/Personal-Agents-Last-Exam/pull/44): keep the property, booked nights and two-person headcount fixed, and use its arrival/request/reply/commitment operations. No reference solution requires changing room dates, obtaining another room or performing a hotel payment/refund. Hotel commitments are graded as recorded promised arrangements, not proof of physical service. UTC/explicit local-date mappings are frozen; any adapter conversion uses public timestamps only. Reuse is proposed, pending the author's and maintainers' interface agreement and implementation.

If released flight/ground apps are unavailable, propose narrow task-owned fixtures for one trip, a small explicit list of flight offers and a pickup/change/cancel provider. These implement public business observations and effects only; they never infer personal feasibility, propagate revisions, register the correct timer or choose a solution. Get approval for this dependency before implementation. They are not separate Environment contributions or claims of existing infrastructure.

## 4. What makes this distinct and personal

**✍️ Your answer (required):** The right response to the same flight offer depends on a remembered conference commitment, both travelers, a budget and existing provider arrangements. A generic rebooking assistant can obtain a valid flight while leaving pickup at the wrong airport, after-midnight hotel entry unresolved, or an obsolete chargeable reservation active. Conversely, an agent can unnecessarily discard still-useful hotel commitments or accept a credit inconsistent with the user's instructions.

The task tests temporal feasibility and revision propagation across independently changing services. Matched diagnostic branches can keep the airline change identical while varying the user's must-arrive time or a provider's explicit service window. Other branches keep personal context constant while varying a transfer reply or actual flight confirmation. These are controls within one Task, not additional task credits. Alternative valid itineraries/clarifications are accepted when they meet the same public constraints and action authority.
