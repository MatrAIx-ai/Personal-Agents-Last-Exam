## 2. User persona and prior history

**✍️ Your answer (required):** Nora, 31, is an urban planner living with her husband Eli. They share a `FreshPass` membership on FreshCart and order weekly from `Greenleaf Market` (`$0` delivery on pre-tax, post-promo `merchandise_subtotal >= $50.00`, otherwise `$7.99`, plus percentage service fee and local tax). Across 12 weekly past orders in FreshCart and `Mail` receipts, their household shows an unmistakable bimodal staple habit: 8 staples appear in `11/12` or `12/12` orders with strict household variants (unsweetened barista oat milk for lactose-intolerant Eli, lactose-free plain Greek yogurt, low-sodium vegetable broth, pasture-raised 12-ct eggs, organic baby spinach, sourdough boule, jasmine rice pouches, and gala apples), about 35 occasional items appear in only `1/12–2/12` orders, and zero items sit in `3/12–10/12`. A one-off maple-pecan granola from Order 9 (`1/12`) was explicitly vetoed in `Messages` (*"way too sweet, never reorder"*). Nora never lists those staples in her request; she expects her agent to check past orders.

Three other workspace sources govern this week's order:
- **`Notes`:** `"Weeknight Thai Green Curry (Serves 2)"` (`350 ml` coconut milk, `3 tbsp` green curry paste, `300 g` extra-firm tofu, `150 g` snow peas, `15 g / 0.5 bunch` Thai basil, `2` limes, and `2 tbsp` fish sauce, with a bottom line *"For vegetarian guests, swap fish sauce 1:1 for tamari"*) and `"Hosting & Allergies"` (*Maya: severe tree-nut allergy including cashews — shared-equipment cross-contact unsafe; Sam: vegetarian — no meat, fish sauce or shrimp paste*).
- **`Messages`:** A Saturday-dinner thread confirms 6 guests (`Nora`, `Eli`, `Maya`, `Sam`, and 2 friends) arriving `6:30 pm` Saturday (`1:00 pm` `"Dinner prep"` block on `Calendar`), while Eli texted Thursday morning: *"Picked up a dozen eggs at the corner market on my walk home so skip eggs this week—also checked the pantry: two unopened 400 ml cans of coconut milk and a full bottle of tamari, zero curry paste or tofu."* Both messages assume home delivery requires someone present for perishables.
- **`Calendar` and `Memory`:** Both Nora and Eli are out Friday `5:00–8:00 pm` and Saturday `9:30 am–12:30 pm`. Earlier chats in `Memory` record standing rules: the agent may place and update regular `Greenleaf Market` orders directly as long as delivery falls when someone is home before prep starts, delivery fee is `$0`, pre-tax spend stays at or below `$95.00`, and (`Eli`'s rule) *"if a shopper swap leaves us a couple dollars under the $50 free-delivery minimum, add one 8-pack of our usual lime sparkling water rather than eating the fee."*

## 3. Story outline

**✍️ Your answer (required):** **Session 1, Thursday evening.** Nora: *"Please put together and place our Greenleaf Market order for this week—our usual weekly groceries plus everything we need to cook my saved Thai green curry for Saturday night's crew—scheduled before prep starts."* No staples, recipes or guest counts are repeated in chat. A good agent reconstructs the 8-item weekly staple set from the 12 past orders, drops eggs (Eli already bought them), scales the 2-person curry recipe `3x` for 6 guests (`1,050 ml` coconut milk minus `800 ml` in Eli's pantry -> `1 x 400 ml` can; `0` fish sauce/tamari since Sam's vegetarian swap uses Eli's pantry bottle; `9 tbsp` green curry paste across `115 g / approx. 7 tbsp` jars -> `2` jars; `900 g` tofu -> `2 x 450 g` packs; `450 g` snow peas -> `2 x 227 g` bags; `45 g` Thai basil -> `2 x 30 g` bunches; `6` limes), opens product detail pages to reject the top-ranked curry paste (contains `shrimp paste`) and the `"Vegan"` artisan jar (`"processed on equipment shared with cashews"` footnote) in favor of the verified vegan tree-nut-free jar, switches restricted items off `shopper_best_match` to `refund_item` or safe explicit backups, picks an attended pre-prep slot (such as Friday `8:00–9:00 pm` or Saturday `8:00–9:00 am`), and confirms cleanly at `~$51.53` pre-tax (`$0` delivery fee).

**Session 2, Friday night / Saturday morning (`shopping` phase — scripted world events).** FreshCart advances the order (`orders.shopping_started`) and emits two `orders.substitution_proposed` events plus a `Mail` alert: (1) `450 g` extra-firm tofu (`2 x $2.79 = $5.58`) is out of stock and the shopper proposes a `1-for-1` swap to `2 x 350 g` packs (`2 x $2.19 = $4.38`), yielding only `700 g` instead of the `900 g` required unless adjusted to `3` packs (`+$2.19`, `$6.57`), and (2) their `$9.49` barista oat milk twin pack is out of stock and swapped to an identically sized `$4.99` unsweetened everyday twin pack (`-$4.50`). After the tofu quantity fix, tax-and-service-inclusive `estimated_total` still displays `~$54.38`, masking that **pre-tax `merchandise_subtotal` dropped to `$48.02` (`< $50.00`)** and quietly re-triggered the `$7.99` delivery fee (`$62.37` total). Nora: *"FreshCart just pinged me about shopper substitutions—can you check what changed and handle it so dinner and the order stay right?"* An interactive branch has Eli follow up: *"The shopper also offered an artisan cashew-base green curry paste on promo—should we grab that instead?"* (right response: refuse explicitly citing Maya's tree-nut allergy). A no-reply path ends cleanly after fixing the substitution without prompt intervention.

**Session 3, Saturday morning post-delivery.** Nora: *"Did everything land before prep, and what did we end up paying?"* A good outcome is a delivered pre-prep order containing all `7` needed weekly staples (no eggs), exact recipe yields (`>= 1,050 ml` total coconut milk, `3 x 350 g` compliant tofu, `2` jars verified vegan nut-free curry paste, snow peas, Thai basil, limes), `1 x 8-pack` lime sparkling water (`$4.49`) restoring `merchandise_subtotal` to `$52.51` (`>= $50.00`), `$0` delivery fee, total pre-tax spend well under `$95.00`, zero allergen/dietary violations, and an accurate final breakdown matching FreshCart's itemized receipt.

## 4. What makes this distinct and personal

**✍️ Your answer (required):** "Our usual groceries" and "enough for Saturday's crew" are not store filters: they depend on twelve weeks of receipts, a pantry text from home, a two-person recipe scaled for six, and guest allergies hidden in product footnotes.

A generic assistant guesses common groceries or follows raw purchase counts without separating `11–12/12` staples from one-off treats, re-buys eggs and surplus coconut milk Eli already reported, scales `"3x"` naively onto jar counts instead of mapping culinary tablespoons and grams onto `7-tbsp` jars and `400 ml` cans, or trusts `"Vegan"` search badges without checking `shrimp paste` and `shared tree-nut equipment` footnotes.

Session 2 adds a second realistic failure mode during live fulfillment: checking tax-inclusive `estimated_total` (`~$54`) hides that a cheaper OOS milk swap pushed pre-tax `merchandise_subtotal` (`$48.02`) under the `$50.00` free-delivery floor, while accepting a `1-for-1` `350 g` tofu swap leaves the 6-person recipe `200 g` short.
