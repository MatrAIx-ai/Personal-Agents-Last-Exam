## 2. User persona and prior history

**✍️ Your answer (required):** Mira is a fictional recreational lifter trying to sustain two regular gym sessions per week, normally Monday and Thursday at 18:00–18:45. She keeps body-mass/body-fat observations, supplement habits and actual exercise/equipment loads and repetitions. Her difficulty is restarting after work disrupts the routine: carrying every missed workout forward makes the backlog harder to face.

In `fitness-agreement`, dated `2026-10-26T20:00:00-07:00`, Mira chose a specific recovery agreement. After **two explicitly confirmed missed regular sessions**, the assistant may, at their next ordinary sync, close the tentative catch-up intention, replace its future fitness-only Calendar placeholder with one pre-authored ten-minute home restart in Saturday's 10:00–10:10 fallback window, and keep one result check-in pending for their next user-started session. This does not count as a full gym workout or authorize extra volume. Missing logs alone never activate it.

Mira also authorizes ordinary logging and one short result check-in exchange at a later user-started session. Once she has reported the result, the assistant must not reopen the resolved result question or repeat unsolicited workout prompts; normal acknowledgment and genuine clarification are allowed. Work/family events are protected. Body observations retain device and observation time; supplement plans and reported intake are distinct. The assistant maintains her supplied routines rather than inventing exercise or supplement prescriptions. All people, values, notes and options below are fictional. The benchmark measures assistance and follow-through, not resulting health improvement.

## 4. What makes this distinct and personal

- **Personalization insight (required):** Mira's own lapse-recovery agreement determines whether the assistant should repair a stalled fitness plan now or ask and wait. Useful help also remembers to close that loop at the next ordinary check-in.
- **Decisive context and trigger (required):** At `2026-11-20T20:00:00-08:00`, the narrow sync request arrives after `miss-1116` and `miss-1119` establish the condition in the agreement of `2026-10-26T20:00:00-07:00`. Main-history action: cancel the future tentative catch-up, create the single Saturday 10:00 restart and persist the pending result check-in. At `2026-11-22T18:00:00-08:00`, the intake-only request does not repeat a workout request; the pending intention should produce a relevant result question and follow-through.
- **Generic assistant mistake (required):** A capable log assistant can update every number but leave the stalled intention unresolved. A generic planner can roll missed full workouts forward or create a new timetable without ever checking what happened. Automatically applying either behavior to everyone also misses the counterpart's ask-first boundary. Scoring distinguishes actual state changes and the later exchange from supportive wording.
- **Different user, different action (required):** Replace only the recovery paragraph in the same dated agreement: this user requires one permission question after two confirmed misses and no Calendar change until they reply. Hold the sync request verbatim, logs, options, Calendar, other permissions and session-3 no-reply behavior fixed. This history requires asking once, retaining a draft/awaiting-reply state and leaving the Saturday catch-up unchanged; automatic replacement is wrong. The independent permission for a later result check-in is unchanged, so session 4 may ask what happened and act on the new reply. Neither history can infer attendance, override a newer refusal or edit protected commitments. Do not duplicate the recovery paragraph in another unchanged seed record.

Related [#29](https://github.com/MatrAIx-ai/Personal-Agents-Last-Exam/pull/29) already covers adaptive routines/backlogs; [#52](https://github.com/MatrAIx-ai/Personal-Agents-Last-Exam/pull/52) covers proactive fitness/nutrition adherence; [#117](https://github.com/MatrAIx-ai/Personal-Agents-Last-Exam/pull/117) covers physiotherapy constraints and incomplete sensor evidence. The proposed increment is a stalled intention's full lifecycle: confirmed lapse, person-specific authority to close or hold it, bounded restart, later result question and evidence-backed return to the normal routine. Records support continuity; load progression is not the main mechanism. [#34](https://github.com/MatrAIx-ai/Personal-Agents-Last-Exam/pull/34) informs the history-only counterfactual and explicit grading. These comparisons disclose overlap, not accepted novelty.
