# Evaluation data realism revision

Addresses [the v1 review](https://github.com/MatrAIx-ai/Personal-Agents-Last-Exam/pull/179#issuecomment-7228338477). The approved proposal, business implementation, GUI, runtime and small smoke fixtures are unchanged. The dataset remains 192 polls in 24 monthly partitions. Synthetic provenance and seed `1792026` are retained; the manifest is version 2.

## Changes and observations

Purpose order, actors, participant subsets, duration, candidate count/time, and lifecycle outcomes are drawn independently with a seeded PRNG. The monthly outcome mixture also varies; none is keyed to a fixed purpose position. Drafts remain private; published unresolved polls preserve missing and withdrawn replies. Replies arrive over days rather than consecutive minutes. Reopening follows a specific named attendee's scheduling conflict, removes the previously confirmed candidate, adds a different candidate, receives fresh replies, and reconfirms later.
