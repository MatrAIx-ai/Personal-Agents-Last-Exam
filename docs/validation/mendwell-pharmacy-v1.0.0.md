### GUI workflow evidence

`tests/web.browser.cjs` drives the page with real clicks and checks each result against App state read through a separate Infra MCP client on the same run. The generic part (`tests/web/harness.cjs`) starts the shared Host, the inspector and Chromium and writes the report to `.artifacts/web/mendwell-pharmacy/`.

- **W1 grants.** Alex gives Sam access to Alex's own records: the preview shows the code, the confirmer and the 30-minute rule; Confirm waits until Infra applies `user.confirmed` (with `dev/confirm.cjs`), Refresh shows it, and Confirm re-previews before it sends; expired and wrong-person confirmations are refused, and `dev/confirm.cjs` refuses a missing run, an option without its value and an unknown option before issuing any event; an unknown person ID is named as such, and an access change Mendwell cannot make (the same access already held, an end time already passed) is explained for grants; after a reload no preview is kept in the browser, the confirmation stays listed and the same preview gets the same code, already confirmed; a preview made stale by another change sends nothing; revoking a grant clears the collector it backed.
- **W1 access changes while the page is open.** Elena revokes Alex's refill access between two refreshes, while the page is reading (with the retry succeeding and failing) and while a request about her records is unanswered: her records, the selected fill, an unsent change and the identifying text of the unanswered request disappear and do not come back with a later grant; a fill-limited pickup grant shows only the pickup details; a change made while the page is reading makes it read again; when refill access drops to pickup, an unanswered prepayment is described without the drug, any household list answer the page receives, including the page shell's own read, removes at once the records of a person whose access ended, and a pickup whose covering pickup grant ended leaves the screen at once, both also when a later read of the same refresh fails.
- **W2.** A prescription with no fills left offers no fill request; the renewal is withdrawn and asked again; a late reply is ignored; a write based on an outdated view is refused; a fill request on the renewed prescription whose reply is lost is retried and returns the stored receipt; a store change made with Move fill is recorded by Mendwell (read through MCP) and shown on the same fill after a reload.
- **W3.** A claim rejected as too soon, a plan request sent and withdrawn, a scheduled resubmission and a switch to the cash price; a plan approval arriving; a quantity change after a quantity limit; a prior authorization denied and paid in cash.
- **W4.** A held fill refuses changes; the release resumes the review with its remaining time; a collection attempted while another fill is held is refused and not replayed.
- **W5.** Prepay needs the card owner's confirmation; a declined card is refused and the household card authorized; a prepaid fill's cancellation preview is kept, then confirmed, and the cancelled fill shows nothing outstanding with its original price on a separate line; a collector named under Sam's pickup grant is cleared when Alex revokes the grant; the counter captures the prepayment.
- **Boundaries.** Management requests from the page session are refused with 403; a Mendwell that keeps changing during a refresh gives a clear error; every section fits a 390 px screen; no JavaScript errors and no console or CSP errors other than the induced network failures and refusals. On a second run, a Give access form limited to a fill that is then cancelled clears the limit, says so and keeps Preview disabled (nothing is sent) until a fill or all fills are chosen again; a cancellation withdraws the fill's pending plan request and says so; previews and forms from before a reset or reload are dropped and nothing from before is sent; an answer stamped for another epoch is not taken as success; a reset between the re-preview and the write leaves the refusal on screen; a reset right after a commit keeps that change's success message; a preview repeated or answered across a reset is not kept; answers lost before a reload are reported as unconfirmed; a policy that omits `plan_requests.create` removes the control on Refresh while a forced call is refused with 403, and a refusal and a read Mendwell refuses are explained in plain words without internal IDs.

### Delivery dataset

The Environment's evaluation data is the delivery dataset `mendwell-delivery-2025-2026` in [`apps/mendwell-pharmacy/dataset/`](../../apps/mendwell-pharmacy/dataset/), covering 2025-01-01 to 2026-12-31 inclusive, counted in America/New_York ([coverage report](../../apps/mendwell-pharmacy/dataset/COVERAGE.md), [`COVERAGE.json`](../../apps/mendwell-pharmacy/dataset/COVERAGE.json)).

- **Design: a base seed plus a dated replay.** `dataset/base.json` is the household and the catalog at 2025-01-01, with no records; `dataset/replay.json` holds 476 committed steps in time order, the household members' tool calls and Infra events; `dataset/full.json` is the state after every step. `dataset/generate.cjs` produced all three by replaying the App's own tools and events with a fixed seed, validating every state against the seed schema and `validateSeed`, so every record is one the App wrote.
- **As-of selection through the handlers.** `dataset/as-of.cjs --at <instant>` replays only the steps dated before a Task's first-session instant; timers due before the instant are committed in the selection; the later Infra events that apply to the selection as it stands are returned for staging, and the fills open at the instant are listed with the withheld household actions on them.
- **Fixture and dataset.** The smoke fixture `fixtures/minimal.json` and the dataset share the household and the catalog (equal at the fixture clock, store stock included, checked by `tests/t32-dataset.test.cjs`) but have independent histories.
- **Counts.** 1,225 dated entries loaded into the App (158 fills, 472 notices and the other records) on 260 distinct dates, the coverage figure; 262 with the replay log. Earliest 2025-01-01, latest 2026-12-31; fills in all 24 months (3 to 12 a month). Real 0, synthetic 1,225; the drug catalog's label facts are paraphrases of public DailyMed labels, cited in `contribution/dataset.json`.
- **Gaps.** No real November 2026 anchor: prescriptions, fills, claims and payments are a real patient's protected health information, shown by the reference product only in a signed-in account, and the approved proposal excludes real patient data and data collection (stated gap, pending maintainer agreement). No Mail history for the dataset's 472 notices, which are in the App's own notice log only (stated gap; Mail is optional).
- **Loading.** `dataset/coverage.cjs` loads five selections through real Core over MCP (2026-01-01, 2026-03-27T00:00:00.001-04:00, the fixture clock, 2026-12-15 and 2027-01-01, with the seed IDs listed in `COVERAGE.json`); the Agent bound as Alex reads every fill the grants in force let him see; the open fills equal those Core shows and the monthly read-back equals the computed table at every instant.
- **Commands**, from `apps/mendwell-pharmacy`: `node dataset/generate.cjs --check`; `node dataset/coverage.cjs --check --runtime ../../runtime`; `node dataset/as-of.cjs --at 2026-11-18T12:00:00-05:00 --out selection.json`.

## Differences from the proposal and interpretations

- **GUI.** The proposal scoped v1 without a GUI ("no GUI"); the platform reviewer's [comment on PR #111](https://github.com/MatrAIx-ai/Personal-Agents-Last-Exam/pull/111#issuecomment-7123353250) asked every Environment for App-owned pages on the shared Web Host, and the GUI is added on that basis with the approved business behaviour unchanged. Confirmations a page cannot issue are applied locally with `dev/confirm.cjs`, an Infra helper outside the loaded package.
- **Fixture provenance and size.** The proposal says "Hand-written JSON fixtures, no generator or model output" and names 12 prescriptions. The task-timeline records were authored with AI assistance, then checked by `dev/check-fixture.cjs` and reviewed by the contributor; the history from 2025-01-01 is generated by `dev/generate-history.cjs`, which replays the App's own tools and events, so the fixture holds 25 prescriptions (12 current, 13 earlier).
- **Fixture clock.** The fixture's present is the shared registry's start instant, Wednesday 18 November 2026, 12:00 in New York.
- **Prior authorization.** An approved prior-authorization request waives the rule for its own fill only, a simplification of real drug plans, where an approval usually covers a period of time.
