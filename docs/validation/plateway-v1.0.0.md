### Delivery dataset

The Environment's evaluation data is the delivery dataset `plateway-delivery-2025-2026` in [`apps/plateway/dataset/`](../../apps/plateway/dataset/), covering 2025-01-01 to 2026-12-31 inclusive, counted in America/New_York ([coverage report](../../apps/plateway/dataset/COVERAGE.md), [`COVERAGE.json`](../../apps/plateway/dataset/COVERAGE.json)).

- **Design: a base seed plus a dated replay.** `dataset/base.json` is the account and the catalog at 2025-01-01T00:00:00-05:00, with no orders; `dataset/replay.json` holds 1,518 committed steps in time order (913 tool calls by the account holder and 605 Infra events); `dataset/full.json` is the state after every step. `dataset/generate.cjs` produced all three by replaying the App's own tools and events through the test harness with a fixed seed, validating every state against the seed schema and `validateSeed`, so every record is one the App wrote.
- **As-of selection through the handlers.** `dataset/as-of.cjs --at <instant>` replays, through the App's handlers, only the steps dated before a Task's first-session instant: later records do not exist in the selection, an order in progress has only the history it had then, and the counters and clock are the App's at that instant. Later Infra events on objects already in the selection are returned for staging through `env.apply_event`, timers due before the instant are committed in the selection (listed as `timers_committed`), and orders still open at the instant are listed with their withheld account-holder steps. Each selection has its own seed ID (the instant to the millisecond and a content hash), so a preview token from one selection is `stale_preview` on another; its `data`, `virtual_now` and provenance `bundle` (citing only the `delivery-dataset` source) go to `env.load_seed` unchanged.
- **Fixture and dataset.** The smoke fixture `fixtures/minimal.json` is unchanged. It and the dataset share the account and the catalog (equal at the fixture clock, checked by `tests/t26-dataset.test.cjs`) but have independent order histories: the fixture serves the business tests, the examples and the proposal's example events; the dataset serves evaluation. `contribution/dataset.json` lists the dataset as its own `delivery-dataset` source.
- **Counts.** 1,462 dated records loaded into the App (198 orders, 536 order lines, 34 refunds, 73 ratings, 8 reported lines, 613 notices) on 227 distinct dates, the coverage figure; 248 with the replay log's 1,518 steps, which add 21 days holding only cart edits and catalog events. Earliest 2025-01-01, latest 2026-12-31; orders in all 24 months (4 to 13 a month). Real 0, synthetic 1,462; the 109 records dated at or after the fixture clock are labelled generated, never observed. Rating texts are drawn only from those their order's record supports.
- **Gaps.** No real November 2026 anchor: DoorDash is only the workflow reference, the approved proposal excludes collecting its data ([`proposals/environment/plateway-delivery.md`](../../proposals/environment/plateway-delivery.md), line 96: "no crawling or copied menus") and the restaurants are invented (stated gap, pending maintainer agreement). No Mail history for the 613 notices, which are in the App's own notice log only (stated gap; Mail is optional).

## Differences from the proposal and interpretations

- **GUI.** The proposal scoped v1 without a GUI; the reviewer's [comment of November 24](https://github.com/MatrAIx-ai/Personal-Agents-Last-Exam/pull/111#issuecomment-7123353250) made a GUI on the shared Web Host a required deliverable, and it is added as described above.
- **Fixture provenance.** The proposal says "Hand-written JSON fixtures, no generator or model output". The task-timeline records were authored with AI assistance, then checked by `dev/check-fixture.cjs` and reviewed by the contributor. The history before 2026 is generated by `dev/generate-history.cjs`, which the reviewer's [comment of November 24](https://github.com/MatrAIx-ai/Personal-Agents-Last-Exam/pull/111#issuecomment-7122958570) allows ("Synthetic data is fine for filling out the historical side"); its seed, range and constants are recorded in `contribution/dataset.json`.
- **Fixture clock.** The fixture's present is the shared registry's start instant, as described under Item 3.
- **Per-test seeds.** The proposal lists "one fixture per test". The App-only tests build their seeds in JavaScript (`tests/seeds.cjs`, `tests/flows.cjs`) from one base seed at a fixed test clock, and the integration scripts use the representative fixture.
- **Seed rules.** Beyond the proposal's list, `validateSeed` rejects fields missing or present for an order's status and mode, placement, release and delivery times out of order, refunds that do not match their lines, options that are not on their dish (seeded cart lines are also checked against the option-group limits; historical order lines are not), labels over 40 characters, unknown time zones and notice IDs longer than 80 characters (so `e<epoch>-<id>` fits Mail's 100-character thread ID). It also rejects refunds other than exactly what the App would have refunded (a cancellation refund only on a cancelled order and equal to its cancellation record; each line refund equal to that line's subtotal and tax, capped at what remained of the charge), a missing estimated delivery time and numeric ID suffixes above 999,999,999. A seeded cancellation must match its refund record and the refund rule for its reason (for the account holder, the cancellation's `stage` field, which the App records on every cancellation, or else the stage recorded before it in the timeline; when neither is known, either holder rule is accepted; a recorded `stage` must be one its reason allows, timeline statuses outside the order statuses are ignored, a cancellation cannot follow a recorded pickup or delivery, and an active order cannot carry a later status in its timeline), with the kept amount equal to the charge less all refunds; a reported line needs a delivered order, a release reason needs a scheduled order and a rating follows the delivery. The App sets no date floor and does not restrict its clock, so a registry without an initial time still loads; an order cannot be changed, and an event for it is refused, at a time before that order's own recorded history (`invalid_state` for writes and previews, `invalid_event` for events).
- **Limits not named in the proposal.** A cart holds at most 50 lines; option price deltas are never negative; a tip is at most $1,000, a dish price at most $10,000 and a delivery fee at most $1,000; preparation and travel times are 1 to 180 minutes; a dish has at most 10 option groups of 20 options, a group allows at most 10 selections and an option adds at most $1,000; a day has at most 4 opening spans; a rating average is between 1 and 5; numeric ID suffixes go up to 999,999,999, after which reads still work but writes that need a new ID are refused with `invalid_state`. A removed or reported $0 line gets no refund record; its `notice.item_refunded` is titled “Item removed” or “Item reported” and says no refund was due. The placed notice falls back to the short form when the receipt exceeds 20,000 characters counted either as code points or as UTF-16 units, because the wire and Mail validators count UTF-16 units.
- **Sender inputs.** Besides the Plateway and Router clients, the sender takes the grants from Infra's Router configuration and publishes only when they allow one of the account holder's bound Mail accounts on `channel.email`. Reconciliation after `unknown` is a sender helper (`receiverSetup`, `reconcile`) that compares Mail's run, account and epoch with those recorded at Router setup and reports `committed` only when both the receipt and the inbox record are found (otherwise `indeterminate`); `send` returns the `event_id` it needs.
- **Seven-day horizon.** Measured in local wall time, so a scheduled time seven local days ahead is accepted across a daylight-saving change.
- **ID counters.** An optional seed field `issued` records the highest numeric suffix issued per ID prefix, so IDs are never reused after a removal; the App maintains it.
- **Scheduled times in tokens.** A scheduled delivery time is converted to the seed's time zone before it enters the checkout token, so the same instant written with different offsets (for example `18:00Z` and `13:00-05:00`) previews and places as one.
- **History and new notices.** The sender treats the notice IDs in the loaded seed (`initial_data` in `env.snapshot`) as history; no extra field is stored on notices.
- **Mail failure cases** are induced without changing the runtime or Mail, as described in the package README.
