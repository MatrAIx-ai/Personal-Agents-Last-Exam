### GUI workflow evidence

`tests/web.browser.cjs` drives the page with real clicks and checks each result against App state read through a separate Infra MCP client on the same run. The generic part (`tests/web/harness.cjs`) starts the shared Host, the inspector and Chromium and writes the report to a new folder under `.artifacts/web/larkfield-health/`.

- **The page.** The shared Host serves the App-owned page; every section of the fixture is shown in Eastern Time, at 1280 and 390 pixels.
- **Claims and accident questions.** Two accident questions on one claim, answered through preview, an outside approval, Refresh and Confirm.
- **Household access.** Jordan gives, changes and removes access to Riley’s records; every change needs Jordan’s approval from Infra.
- **Prior authorization and expedite.** A PA for Riley arrives from Infra; Jordan asks for expedited review, the plan grants it and then denies the PA; Jordan files an expedited appeal.
- **Appeals.** Statements on Riley’s appeal ap-3, its withdrawal and a refiling across a reload.
- **Lost and reduced access.** Casey’s grants to Jordan come and go through Infra; the page removes what Jordan can no longer see or do, also when a refresh fails.
- **Resets and new data.** Previews, drafts, retries and receipts stay bound to the run state they were made in.
- **Two claims, two questions.** Two claims whose IDs and line IDs join to the same text each keep their own question, preview and approval; a preview belongs to the record it was made on.
- **Other coverage for 2027.** Claims with 2027 services open other-coverage questions; one answer covers them.
- **Platform rules.** The page cannot reach Infra; the narrow layout holds after the run.
- **Policies without some tools.** A second fresh run: a policy without appeals.add_statement and claims.info.confirm, then a policy without plan.get, each loaded with new data.

### Delivery dataset

The Environment's evaluation data is the delivery dataset `larkfield-delivery-2025-2026` in [`apps/larkfield-health/dataset/`](../../apps/larkfield-health/dataset/), covering 2025-01-01 to 2026-12-31 inclusive, counted in America/New_York ([coverage report](../../apps/larkfield-health/dataset/COVERAGE.md), [`COVERAGE.json`](../../apps/larkfield-health/dataset/COVERAGE.json)).

- **Design: a base seed plus a dated replay.** `dataset/base.json` is the household, the plan, the providers and the services at 2025-01-01, with no records; `dataset/replay.json` holds 167 committed steps in time order, the household members' tool calls and Infra events; `dataset/full.json` is the state after every step. `dataset/generate.cjs` produced all three by replaying the App's own tools and events with a fixed seed, validating every state against the seed schema and `validateSeed`, so every record is one the App wrote.
- **As-of selection through the handlers.** `dataset/as-of.cjs --at <instant>` replays, through the App's handlers, only the steps dated before a Task's first-session instant; timers due before the instant are committed in the selection; the later steps are withheld, and the Infra events among them that apply to the selection as it stands, with the same effect as in the dataset, are returned for staging.
- **Fixture and dataset.** The smoke fixture `fixtures/minimal.json` and the dataset share the household, the plan, the providers and the services (equal at the fixture clock, checked by `tests/t32-dataset.test.cjs`) but have independent histories.
- **Counts.** 744 dated entries loaded into the App (97 claims, 98 EOBs, 322 notices and the other records) on 219 distinct dates, the coverage figure; 220 with the replay log. Earliest 2025-01-01, latest 2026-12-30; claims in all 24 months. Real 0, synthetic 744.
- **Gaps.** No real November 2026 anchor: claims, EOBs, prior authorizations and appeals are a real member's protected health information, shown by the reference product only in a signed-in member account, and the approved proposal describes a synthetic plan (stated gap, pending maintainer agreement). No Mail history for the dataset's 322 notices, which are in the App's own notice log only (stated gap; Mail is optional). Three prior authorizations for a pharmacy item (pa-3, pa-7, pa-11) are approved and never used: the catalogue has no pharmacy among its providers and the dataset invents no pharmacy claims (stated gap).
- **Loading.** `dataset/coverage.cjs` loads 5 selections through real Core over MCP (2025-07-09T23:59:00.001-04:00, 2026-01-01T00:00:00-05:00, 2026-11-18T12:00:00-05:00, 2026-12-15T00:00:00-05:00, 2027-01-01T00:00:00-05:00, with the seed IDs listed in `COVERAGE.json`) and reads them back as the Agent.
- **Commands**, from `apps/larkfield-health`: `node dataset/generate.cjs --check`; `node dataset/coverage.cjs --check --runtime ../../runtime`; `node dataset/as-of.cjs --at 2026-11-18T12:00:00-05:00 --out selection.json`.

## Differences from the proposal and interpretations

- **GUI.** The proposal scoped v1 without a GUI; the platform reviewer's [comment on PR #111](https://github.com/MatrAIx-ai/Personal-Agents-Last-Exam/pull/111#issuecomment-7123353250) asked every Environment for App-owned pages on the shared Web Host, and the GUI is added on that basis with the approved business behaviour unchanged. Approvals a page cannot issue are applied locally with `dev/confirm.cjs`, an Infra helper outside the loaded package.
- **Fixture provenance and size.** The proposal planned hand-written JSON fixtures with no generator output and names 16 claims. The fixture is produced by a script that replays the App, with a generated history from 2025-01-01, and the 2026 task timeline is a hand-written list of dated steps replayed through the App; it holds 58 claims, the 16 of the task timeline and 42 in the history.
- **Fixture clock.** The fixture's present is the shared registry's start instant, Wednesday 18 November 2026, 12:00 in New York.
