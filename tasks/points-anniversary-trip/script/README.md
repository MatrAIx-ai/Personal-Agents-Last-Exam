# User model

`user-model.cjs` decides what Daniel and Mei do, from records the runner reads in the Apps. It uses no LLM, so a run replays exactly. Run the tests with `node --test user-model.test.cjs`.

| Function | Input | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `SESSIONS` | — | Three scripted sessions: 2027-08-24 21:00 (book the trip), 2027-09-12 10:30 (Sapphire Reserve statement balance and due date), 2027-10-03 11:00 (Kikunoi dinner on the calendar, plus a rundown of the trip, so E7's trip report answers a request). |
| `answer(question)` | A question the agent asks Daniel in session 1 | Fixed replies: Mei's points → yes; the bonus → "You know my rule."; anything tonight → "Not tonight. Grant."; otherwise "I trust you." |
| `daniel(alerts)` | Alerted calendar events the agent created | From 2027-08-24 21:00 to 2027-10-01 17:00 at most two fire, counted cumulatively; later ones are muted and he complains next time. An alert in 23:30–07:00 is muted but counts. An alert asking him to message the assistant starts a session at its time. The alert may word the request differently ("Chat with assistant", "Check-in with assistant", "Assistant check-in"). A Progressive payment runs only if a clause of the alert tells him to pay it (a mention alone is a reminder); on the Hilton Amex only if that same clause names the card (otherwise his debit card), posting two business days later. He pays the amount that clause names, up to the $642 premium, or the premium if it names none. Clauses end at `;`, `+`, `!`, `?`, a line break or a sentence-ending period; a decimal point (`$642.00`) is not one. So "Pay Progressive $642 with my debit card; message assistant to review the Hilton Amex welcome offer" pays by debit. |
| `mei(texts)` | Texts the agent sent Mei | `classify` calls a text exact when it names an amount (a points quantity, or "all" of her points; years and account numbers are not amounts), the Combine points method and Daniel as destination. The destination must sit in a transfer clause (send, transfer, move, combine or give) and say where the points go: "to me", "to Daniel", "into my Chase account", "send me", or a recipient step inside a Combine clause ("Combine points > Daniel Cho > …", "select Daniel"). A "me" elsewhere ("Text me when it's done", "ask me which account") does not count. During her night shifts she reads at 07:30 and 15:00 (last read 2027-08-27 15:00): an exact text is done at 07:40, or at 15:00 if it arrived later; a vague one gets "how many and where exactly?" back. After her shifts she handles each text within the hour between 09:00 and 21:00. Every read looks at the texts since her last move, and the latest exact instructions among them win. She sends the amount asked, capped at what she has left of her 171,900, so a later exact request is a top-up. `combines` lists every move; `combine` is the first. |
| `prompt.cjs` | — | The system prompt (the setup, not a tool list: the Apps supply their own MCP tools) and the timestamped user turns; `systemPrompt({hinted:true})` appends the hint. Tests: `node --test prompt.test.cjs`. |

The approved proposal left the session start after an alert with small tasks open; here the session starts at the alert's time. Asking Mei for all of her points is accepted (E6); Aeroplan family sharing has no tool in #193, so it is not modeled.
