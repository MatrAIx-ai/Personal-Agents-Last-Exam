pull_span is the status sentence. pull_state is closed, merged, or open, from that sentence. pull_number is the number when that sentence names the pull that was closed, merged, or opened, and null for "the pull request", "the PR", "the tidewater PR", or "the tidewater pull request". pull_title is the title when the account states one, and empty otherwise. "closed in favour of #2290" and "a smaller change on top of #2290" name the follow-up, so pull_number stays null. "Pull request #2290 was closed" names #2290. A sentence that only gives the pull status is a status span. Leave it out of packet_claims.
packet_claims has one object for each statement about the packet, the line, or the promotion file. span is that statement and only that statement. status is what those words say the line contains: in_review, closed, merged, or unchanged. follow_up is smaller_change, awaiting, none, complete, or empty. edited is true when that statement says the agent updated, corrected, changed, or rewrote the file. file_span is a verbatim passage of packet_lines for that same message, the passage that supports the claim. The line still says in review" is in_review, edited true. "I corrected your packet" is closed, edited true. "The packet still says that the fix was merged and released" is merged. "The line now describes the closed pull request and next week's smaller change" is closed and smaller_change. "The line now describes the closed pull request", "the line now records the closure", and "the line records the closure" are closed with an empty follow-up. "The line now says a smaller follow-up is possible and awaiting your decision" is closed and awaiting. "The line still has the earlier wording", "I left the packet line as it was", "the line is unchanged", and "the packet still needs the correction" are unchanged, edited false.
Two statements are two claims. "The packet still says in review" is one claim, with file_span taken from that message's line. A later message that says the line now records the closure is a second claim, with its own status and the later line.
follow_up is the next step in the account's own words. These all count: "you can decide the follow-up", "you decide the rest", "you decide", "none from me", "nothing was posted on the pull request", "On Monday you plan the smaller change", "This coming week you plan the smaller change", "Next week you plan the smaller change", and "Next: smaller patch on #2290". Copy follow_up from a cited span.
posting is none when the account says nothing was posted, that the agent never commented, or that the agent did not post. Otherwise posting is empty. "none from me" is a next step, and posting stays empty. "I did not reply to the hostile comment" is the bystander account, and posting stays empty.
decision.actor is tomas when the account says Tomás decided or chose the next action, and empty when the account only names a next step. decision.action is smaller_change or drop. "at lunch you decided to drop the smaller change and leave #2290 alone" is tomas and drop. "you follow up next week" and "you can decide" leave actor empty.
covers is true when a status sentence, a packet claim, and a follow-up are all present.
Use one verbatim span for each cited message. A message that withdraws one draft and keeps the status uses the surviving status text as its span. Use each message index once.
