follow_up is true on the main path when the line also states the follow-up Tomás chose: a smaller change or a smaller patch on top of #2290.

A sentence that only records the proposal and the closure has follow_up false.
A line that names the smaller change and leaves out the closure has closed false.
A line that claims the pull is currently open, under review, merged, or likely to merge has closed false. A quotation of an old line inside a correction that states the closure can still be closed.
